## Answers

#### 1. What is a fundamental reason why Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) is important?  
A) ✓ Because users interact with technology in many different contexts  
B) ✗ HCI is relevant to all users, not just programmers  
C) ✓ Asking the right questions is central to HCI  
D) ✗ HCI is not limited to hardware design  

**Correct:** A, C


#### 2. Who can be considered a "user" in the context of HCI?  
A) ✗ Users are not limited to end-users of consumer software  
B) ✓ Programmers developing APIs are users too  
C) ✓ Researchers can be users if they interact with systems  
D) ✓ Anyone interacting with or affected by a computing system is a user  

**Correct:** B, C, D


#### 3. What is the "essence" of HCI as described?  
A) ✗ Visual design is important but not the essence  
B) ✓ Asking and answering the right questions is the core of HCI  
C) ✗ Coding is a tool, not the essence  
D) ✗ Hardware building is not the main focus  

**Correct:** B


#### 4. Why is it often difficult to identify the right questions in HCI?  
A) ✗ Users may not always know what they want, but this is not the main reason

D) ✗ HCI deals with practical problems, not only theoretical ones  

**Correct:** B


#### 5. What skills does learning HCI help develop?
