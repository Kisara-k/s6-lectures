## 5. Interaction Design Basics

## Questions

#### 1. What does the "golden rule of design" primarily emphasize in Human-Computer Interaction?  
A) Designing systems that assume users will not make errors  
B) Understanding both the technical capabilities of computers and the psychological and social aspects of users  
C) Prioritizing aesthetic appeal over functionality  
D) Focusing solely on the interface without considering documentation or user support  

#### 2. Which of the following best describe the role of personas in interaction design?  
A) They are real users who test the system during development  
B) They help designers imagine user needs and behaviors that differ from their own  
C) They serve as detailed, fictional user profiles to guide design decisions  
D) They replace the need for direct user observation or interviews  

#### 3. Why is iteration and prototyping considered essential in the design process?  
A) To ensure the final product is released as quickly as possible without testing  
B) Because the first design is usually perfect and only needs minor tweaks  
C) To avoid making any trade-offs during design  
D) To discover what users really need and to improve the design based on feedback  

#### 4. In navigation design, which of the following are part of the "four golden rules" for helping users find their way?  
A) Knowing the exact time it will take to complete a task  
B) Knowing where you’ve been  
C) Knowing where you are  
D) Knowing what you can do  

#### 5. When designing screen layouts, which principles help improve user comprehension and scanning?  
A) Ignoring white space to maximize information density  
B) Grouping related items physically together  
C) Using as many colors and fonts as possible to attract attention  
D) Aligning text left for languages read left-to-right  

#### 6. Which statements about scenarios in interaction design are true?  
A) They help communicate design ideas through detailed user stories  
B) Acting out scenarios can reveal hidden interaction problems  
C) Scenarios always show multiple branching paths and user choices  
D) Scenarios replace the need for prototypes and usability testing  

#### 7. What are affordances in the context of user interface design?  
A) Visual or physical cues that suggest how an object or control should be used  
B) The cultural assumptions behind interface icons and symbols  
C) The cost of producing a physical device  
D) The technical limitations of a platform or device  

#### 8. Which of the following are valid reasons to design for human error rather than blaming users?  
A) Human error is predictable and can be anticipated in design  
B) Users should be trained to avoid all errors before using a system  
C) Physical materials like concrete are designed to fail under stress, so software should too  
D) Designing for error improves overall system safety and usability  

#### 9. In the context of localization and internationalization, which challenges must designers consider?  
A) Simply translating text is sufficient for all cultures  
B) Color usage has universal meaning and does not require adaptation  
C) Cultural meanings of symbols (e.g., ticks and crosses) can vary significantly  
D) Layout changes such as text direction and size adjustments may be necessary  

#### 10. What are common pitfalls of prototyping and iteration in interaction design?  
A) Moving forward without a clear understanding of what needs improvement  
B) Iterating too quickly without gathering user feedback  
C) Starting with a strong initial design and clear goals  
D) Using prototypes only to confirm that the design is already perfect

## Answers

#### 1. What does the "golden rule of design" primarily emphasize in Human-Computer Interaction?  
A) ✗ Assumes users will not make errors is unrealistic and poor design practice.  
B) ✓ Emphasizes understanding both computers’ capabilities and users’ psychological/social aspects.  
C) ✗ Prioritizing aesthetics over functionality ignores usability and user needs.  
D) ✗ Design includes documentation and user support, not just the interface.  

**Correct:** B


#### 2. Which of the following best describe the role of personas in interaction design?  
A) ✗ Personas are fictional, not real users testing the system.  
B) ✓ They help designers imagine users different from themselves.  
C) ✓ Personas are detailed fictional profiles guiding design decisions.  
D) ✗ Personas complement, not replace, direct user observation and interviews.  

**Correct:** B, C


#### 3. Why is iteration and prototyping considered essential in the design process?  
A) ✗ Prototyping slows down release but improves quality; rushing without testing is risky.  
B) ✗ The first design is rarely perfect; iteration is needed.  
C) ✗ Trade-offs are inevitable; iteration helps manage them, not avoid them.  
D) ✓ Helps discover real user needs and improve design based on feedback.  

**Correct:** D


#### 4. In navigation design, which of the following are part of the "four golden rules" for helping users find their way?  
A) ✗ Exact task completion time is not part of navigation rules.  
B) ✓ Knowing where you’ve been helps users track their path and avoid confusion.  
C) ✓ Knowing your current location is essential for orientation.  
D) ✓ Knowing available actions helps users decide what to do next.  

**Correct:** B, C, D


#### 5. When designing screen layouts, which principles help improve user comprehension and scanning?  
A) ✗ Ignoring white space makes screens cluttered and harder to read.  
B) ✓ Grouping related items physically together aids understanding.  
C) ✗ Overusing colors and fonts causes confusion and reduces clarity.  
D) ✓ Left alignment matches reading habits in left-to-right languages.  

**Correct:** B, D


#### 6. Which statements about scenarios in interaction design are true?  
A) ✓ Scenarios communicate design ideas through detailed user stories.  
B) ✓ Acting out scenarios can reveal hidden problems in interaction.  
C) ✗ Scenarios usually show one linear path, not multiple branches.  
D) ✗ Scenarios complement but do not replace prototypes or usability testing.  

**Correct:** A, B


#### 7. What are affordances in the context of user interface design?  
A) ✓ Affordances are cues that suggest how to use an object or control.  
B) ✗ Cultural assumptions affect symbols but are not affordances themselves.  
C) ✗ Affordances are not about cost or production.  
D) ✗ Affordances relate to perception, not technical limitations.  

**Correct:** A


#### 8. Which of the following are valid reasons to design for human error rather than blaming users?  
A) ✓ Human error is predictable and can be anticipated in design.  
B) ✗ Expecting users to avoid all errors is unrealistic and unfair.  
C) ✗ Unlike physical materials, software should be designed to prevent failure, not fail under stress.  
D) ✓ Designing for error improves safety and usability.  

**Correct:** A, D


#### 9. In the context of localization and internationalization, which challenges must designers consider?  
A) ✗ Translation alone is insufficient; cultural adaptation is needed.  
B) ✗ Color meanings vary culturally and require adaptation.  
C) ✓ Symbols can have different meanings across cultures.  
D) ✓ Layout changes like text direction and size adjustments are often necessary.  

**Correct:** C, D


#### 10. What are common pitfalls of prototyping and iteration in interaction design?  
A) ✓ Moving forward without understanding what to improve wastes effort.  
B) ✓ Iterating too quickly without feedback leads to poor improvements.  
C) ✗ Starting with a strong design and clear goals is good practice, not a pitfall.  
D) ✗ Prototypes are for exploring and improving, not confirming perfection.  

**Correct:** A, B
