## Answers

#### 1. Which of the following statements about the QWERTY keyboard layout are true?  
A) ✗ Not the fastest layout; alternatives like Dvorak can be faster.  
B) ✓ Social resistance keeps QWERTY dominant despite alternatives.  
C) ✗ QWERTY does not place common letters ergonomically; it’s inefficient.  
D) ✓ Designed to prevent typewriter jams, not for typing speed.  

**Correct:** B, D


#### 2. What are the main challenges faced by handwriting recognition systems?  
A) ✓ Segmenting joined writing into letters is difficult.  
B) ✓ Different handwriting styles complicate interpretation.  
C) ✓ Capturing stroke path and pressure is essential for natural input.  
D) ✗ OCR converts images to text, but handwriting recognition does not convert typed text to handwriting.  

**Correct:** A, B, C


#### 3. Which of the following are characteristics of optical mice compared to mechanical mice?  
A) ✗ Optical mice usually work on regular surfaces without special pads.

C) ✓ Optical mice are less affected by dust and dirt than mechanical ones.  
D) ✗ This describes mechanical mice, not optical.  

**Correct:** B, C


#### 4. Regarding touchscreens, which statements are correct?

B) ✓ Finger contact leaves smudges and marks on screens.  
C) ✗ Extended use can cause arm fatigue due to holding the arm up.  
D) ✗ Fingers are blunt, making precise selection of small targets difficult.  

**Correct:** A, B


#### 5. Which of the following are true about virtual reality (VR) headsets and their limitations?
