## 10. Universal Design

## Questions

#### 1. Which of the following are principles of Universal Design?  
A) Size and space for approach and use  
B) Perceptible information  
C) Flexibility in use  
D) High physical effort to encourage user engagement  

#### 2. Multi-modal systems differ from multi-media systems because:  
A) Multi-media systems use different types of media but may rely on a single sensory mode  
B) Multi-modal systems use multiple sensory modes for interaction  
C) Multi-media systems always involve sound and speech  
D) Multi-modal systems only use visual and tactile senses  

#### 3. Which of the following senses are currently practical for use in computer interfaces?  
A) Smell  
B) Touch  
C) Sight  
D) Taste  

#### 4. In speech recognition, which factors contribute to the difficulty of accurately understanding spoken language?  
A) Consistent syntax across all sentences  
B) Variations in accent and intonation  
C) Background noise interference  
D) The presence of filler words like “um” and “err”  

#### 5. Regarding speech synthesis, which statements are true?  
A) It is most successful when users have few alternatives and are motivated  
B) Speech synthesis can be intrusive in shared work environments  
C) Synthesized speech is always easier to review than text  
D) Prosody is easy to replicate naturally in synthesized speech  

#### 6. Auditory icons and earcons differ in that:  
A) Auditory icons cannot convey positional information  
B) Earcons are synthetic and structured combinations of notes  
C) Earcons always have a natural mapping to interface tasks  
D) Auditory icons use natural sounds with inherent meanings  

#### 7. Which of the following are challenges specific to handwriting recognition technology?  
A) Personal differences in letter formation  
B) Preference of many users for keyboards over handwriting input  
C) Co-articulation effects between strokes  
D) The inability to digitize strokes into dots  

#### 8. Which of the following are benefits of using multi-sensory systems in interfaces?  
A) They rely solely on visual information for clarity  
B) They enhance accessibility for users with special needs  
C) They provide redundant information that can resolve ambiguity  
D) They eliminate the need for any one sensory channel  

#### 9. When designing for users with disabilities, which of the following are appropriate assistive technologies?  
A) Speech input/output for users with physical impairments  
B) Screen readers for visually impaired users  
C) High physical effort interfaces to improve dexterity  
D) Text communication and captions for hearing impairments  

#### 10. Which statements about co-articulation and prosody in speech are correct?  
A) Co-articulation simplifies speech recognition by standardizing phonemes  
B) Co-articulation refers to how phonemes change depending on context  
C) Prosody is irrelevant for understanding the meaning of sentences  
D) Prosody involves variations in tone, stress, and pitch that add meaning

## Answers

#### 1. Which of the following are principles of Universal Design?  
A) ✓ Size and space for approach and use is essential for accessibility.  
B) ✓ Perceptible information ensures information is accessible to all users.  
C) ✓ Flexibility in use is a core principle allowing diverse user preferences.  
D) ✗ High physical effort contradicts the principle of low physical effort.  

**Correct:** A, B, C


#### 2. Multi-modal systems differ from multi-media systems because:  
A) ✓ Multi-media systems use different media but may rely on a single sensory mode (usually visual).  
B) ✓ Multi-modal systems use multiple sensory modes (e.g., visual and auditory).  
C) ✗ Multi-media systems do not always involve sound and speech; they may be visual only.  
D) ✗ Multi-modal systems are not limited to just visual and tactile senses; they can include any combination.  

**Correct:** A, B


#### 3. Which of the following senses are currently practical for use in computer interfaces?  
A) ✗ Smell is not yet used in practical computer interfaces.  
B) ✓ Touch is sometimes used, e.g., touchscreens and haptic devices.  
C) ✓ Sight is the primary sense used in most interfaces.  
D) ✗ Taste is not practically used in current computer interfaces.  

**Correct:** B, C


#### 4. In speech recognition, which factors contribute to the difficulty of accurately understanding spoken language?  
A) ✗ Syntax varies widely; consistent syntax would make recognition easier.  
B) ✓ Variations in accent and intonation affect recognition accuracy.  
C) ✓ Background noise interferes with speech recognition.  
D) ✓ Filler words like “um” and “err” complicate processing.  

**Correct:** B, C, D


#### 5. Regarding speech synthesis, which statements are true?  
A) ✓ It works best when users are motivated and have few alternatives.  
B) ✓ Speech synthesis can be intrusive, especially in shared environments.  
C) ✗ Synthesized speech is harder to review than text because it is transient.  
D) ✗ Prosody is difficult to replicate naturally in synthesized speech.  

**Correct:** A, B


#### 6. Auditory icons and earcons differ in that:  
A) ✗ Auditory icons can convey positional information using stereo sound.  
B) ✓ Earcons are synthetic, structured sounds representing actions or objects.  
C) ✗ Earcons do not have natural mappings and can be harder to associate with tasks.  
D) ✓ Auditory icons use natural sounds with inherent meanings.  

**Correct:** B, D


#### 7. Which of the following are challenges specific to handwriting recognition technology?  
A) ✓ Personal differences in letter formation cause recognition difficulties.  
B) ✓ Many users prefer keyboards despite handwriting recognition being usable.  
C) ✓ Co-articulation effects between strokes complicate recognition.  
D) ✗ Strokes can be digitized into dots; this is not a problem.  

**Correct:** A, B, C


#### 8. Which of the following are benefits of using multi-sensory systems in interfaces?  
A) ✗ Multi-sensory systems do not rely solely on visual information.  
B) ✓ They improve accessibility for users with special needs.  
C) ✓ Redundant information helps resolve ambiguity.  
D) ✗ They do not eliminate the need for any one sensory channel; rather, they complement them.  

**Correct:** B, C


#### 9. When designing for users with disabilities, which of the following are appropriate assistive technologies?  
A) ✓ Speech input/output assists users with physical impairments.  
B) ✓ Screen readers help visually impaired users access information.  
C) ✗ High physical effort interfaces are not suitable; low effort is preferred.  
D) ✓ Text communication and captions support hearing-impaired users.  

**Correct:** A, B, D


#### 10. Which statements about co-articulation and prosody in speech are correct?  
A) ✗ Co-articulation complicates, not simplifies, speech recognition.  
B) ✓ Co-articulation refers to phoneme changes depending on context.  
C) ✗ Prosody is very relevant for understanding meaning beyond words.  
D) ✓ Prosody involves tone, stress, and pitch variations that add meaning.  

**Correct:** B, D
