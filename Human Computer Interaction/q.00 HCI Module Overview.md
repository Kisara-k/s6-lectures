## Answers

#### 1. What are the two main perspectives that Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) combines?  
A) ✓ Studying human behavior and designing technology — HCI studies people and designs systems for interaction.  
B) ✗ Programming languages and hardware architecture — These are technical areas but not the core perspectives of HCI.  
C) ✗ Analyzing social networks and marketing strategies — Not central to HCI’s main focus.  
D) ✓ Understanding human perception and system implementation — HCI involves understanding humans and how systems work.  

**Correct:** A, D


#### 2. Which of the following disciplines contribute to the field of HCI?  
A) ✓ Psychology — Important for understanding human behavior.  
B) ✓ Sociology — Helps understand social contexts of technology use.  
C) ✓ Business and Management — Relevant for organizational adoption and design.  
D) ✗ Mechanical Engineering — Not typically a core discipline in HCI.  

**Correct:** A, B, C


#### 3. The three “use” words in HCI—useful, usable, and used—refer to:  
A) ✓ The system’s functionality, ease of use, and actual adoption — These are the correct meanings.  
B) ✗ The system’s design, programming, and testing phases — These are development stages, not the “use” words.  
C) ✗ The user’s motivation, attention, and memory — These relate to human factors but not the “use” words.  
D) ✗ The hardware, software, and network components — These are system parts, not the “use” words.  

**Correct:** A


#### 4. How did the nature of human-computer interaction change from the 1980s to today?  
A) ✗ From one person per machine to multiple people sharing one machine — Usually one person per machine, not multiple sharing.  
B) ✓ From isolated individual use to interconnected, ubiquitous devices — Reflects the shift to networked, pervasive computing.  
C) ✗ From graphical interfaces to command-line interfaces — The trend was the opposite; GUIs became dominant.  
D) ✗ From social computing to purely task-oriented computing — Social computing has increased, not decreased.  

**Correct:** B


#### 5. Which statement best describes ubiquitous computing?  
A) ✓ Computing devices are embedded everywhere and integrated into daily life — This is the definition of ubiquitous computing.  
B) ✗ Computers are only used in professional environments — Opposite of ubiquitous computing.  
C) ✗ Users interact with a single, dedicated computer at a time — Ubiquitous computing involves many devices.  
D) ✗ Computing is limited to desktop and laptop devices — Ubiquitous computing extends beyond these.  

**Correct:** A


#### 6. What does the shift from “dialogue with the computer” to “dialogue with the world” imply in HCI?  
A) ✓ Interaction now involves multiple devices and social contexts — This captures the expanded scope of interaction.
