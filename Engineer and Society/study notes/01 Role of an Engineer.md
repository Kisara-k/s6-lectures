## 2. Role of an Engineer

## Study Notes

### 1. 🛠️ What is Engineering and Who is a Chartered Engineer?

Engineering is a profession that combines knowledge from mathematics and natural sciences with practical experience and judgment. The goal is to develop ways to use materials and natural forces efficiently and economically for the benefit of humanity. This means engineers don’t just understand scientific facts—they apply them to create useful solutions that improve everyday life.

A **Chartered Engineer** is a professional engineer who has met specific standards of competence, experience, and ethics. They are recognized as experts who can take responsibility for complex engineering tasks and ensure their work benefits society safely and sustainably.

#### Engineer vs Scientist

- **Scientists** focus on discovering and explaining natural phenomena. They study how the world works.
- **Engineers** take those scientific discoveries and use them to design, build, and improve devices, systems, or processes that solve real-world problems.

A famous quote by Theodore Von Karman sums it up well:  
*"Scientists study the world as it is, engineers create the world that never has been."*  
This highlights that engineers are creators and innovators who transform ideas into practical realities.


### 2. 🌍 The Relationship Between Society and Engineers

Society is made up of people living together who need goods and services. Engineers play a crucial role in meeting these needs by designing and delivering products and systems that are:

- **Reliable:** They work consistently without failure.
- **Safe and Secure:** They protect users from harm.
- **Standardized:** They meet agreed-upon quality and safety standards.
- **Economical:** They use resources efficiently to keep costs reasonable.

Engineers must always keep society’s interests at the forefront, ensuring their work improves quality of life without causing harm.


### 3. 🎓 Competencies Required to Become a Chartered Engineer

To become a Chartered Engineer, one must develop a broad set of skills and knowledge, including:

- **Engineering Principles:** Deep understanding of core scientific and mathematical concepts.
- **Design and Development:** Ability to create new processes, systems, services, or products.
- **Responsibility and Leadership:** Managing projects and teams effectively.
- **Communication Skills:** Clearly sharing ideas and collaborating with others.
- **Professional Commitment:** Upholding ethical standards and continuing to learn.

These competencies ensure that Chartered Engineers can handle complex challenges responsibly and professionally.


### 4. ⚖️ Roles, Responsibilities, and Functions of an Engineer

Engineers have a wide range of roles, but their primary responsibility is to **prioritize public safety and welfare** above all else. This means:

- Being accountable for how their work affects people and society.
- Carefully considering all relevant facts and possible outcomes before making decisions.
- Engaging in the full engineering process: design, development, deployment, and evaluation.

#### Social Responsibilities of Engineers

Engineers must:

- Ensure the safety and well-being of the public.
- Use society’s resources wisely and ethically.
- Refuse to participate in projects that are unethical or harmful.
- Speak out against wrongdoing or illegal activities.
- Support whistleblowers who expose unethical practices.
- Contribute to non-profit and community projects.
- Educate future engineers about their social responsibilities.
- Conduct ethical risk and safety assessments.
- Inform the public about the consequences of new technologies.
- Design sustainable technologies that protect the environment.
- Follow the **precautionary principle**, which means avoiding actions that could cause serious harm when the risks are uncertain.
- Build safety features and redundancies into their designs to prevent failures.


### 5. 💼 Benefits of Being an Engineer

Choosing engineering as a career offers many rewards:

- **Job Satisfaction:** Solving real problems and seeing your work make a difference.
- **Variety:** Opportunities in many industries and specializations.
- **Challenging Work:** Constant learning and problem-solving.
- **Intellectual Growth:** Developing critical thinking and creativity.
- **Societal Impact:** Improving lives and communities.
- **Financial Security:** Competitive salaries and job stability.
- **Prestige:** Respect as a professional contributing to society.
- **Professional Environment:** Working with skilled colleagues and continuous development.
- **Innovation:** Being part of technological and scientific advancements.


### 6. 📜 Rules of Conduct and Professional Ethics

Engineers must always uphold the **dignity and reputation** of their profession. This means acting with:

- **Integrity:** Being honest and trustworthy.
- **Fairness:** Treating everyone justly.
- **Respect for Health, Safety, and the Environment:** Protecting people and nature.
- **Competence:** Using their skills to the best of their ability.
- **Support for Others:** Helping colleagues and mentoring junior engineers.

These ethical principles guide engineers to make decisions that protect the public and maintain trust in the profession.


### 7. 👷‍♂️ The Role of an Engineer in Practice

Engineers often take on multiple roles such as:

- **Team Leader:** Guiding and coordinating a group.
- **Manager:** Overseeing projects and resources.
- **Representative:** Acting as the face of the engineering team or company.
- **Motivator:** Encouraging and supporting colleagues.
- **Planner:** Organizing tasks and timelines.
- **Decision Maker:** Choosing the best course of action.
- **Rule Follower:** Ensuring compliance with laws and standards.

#### Responsibilities

Engineers must oversee all operations under their control, ensuring:

- Timely delivery of results.
- Cost-effectiveness without sacrificing quality.
- Adherence to safety and regulatory standards.
- Optimal use of resources.

#### Categories of Responsibilities

- **Network Planning and Design:** Creating systems that meet technical and user needs.
- **Project Implementation:** Managing the execution of engineering projects.
- **Operation and Maintenance:** Ensuring systems continue to function properly.
- **Research and Development:** Innovating new technologies and solutions.


### 8. 📊 Key Job Functions of an Engineer

Engineers are involved in:

- **Planning and Budgeting:** Aligning activities with corporate goals and managing finances.
- **Resource Allocation:** Assigning tasks and tools to team members.
- **Process Management:** Establishing and improving workflows.
- **Progress Monitoring:** Comparing expected outcomes with actual results.
- **Staff Development:** Organizing training for technical and soft skills.
- **Employee Welfare:** Supporting the well-being of staff and their families.
- **Performance Appraisal:** Evaluating work quality and productivity.
- **Counseling and Coaching:** Helping staff improve and grow professionally.


### 9. 📜 Legislative and Regulatory Framework

Engineers must comply with various laws and regulations, including:

- **Engineering Council Act 04/2017:** Governs the registration and practice of engineers.
- **Institution of Engineers Sri Lanka (IESL) Act:** Regulates professional standards.
- **Safety and Factory Ordinance:** Ensures workplace safety.
- **Industrial Dispute Act:** Addresses labor relations.
- **Environmental Regulations:** Protect natural resources and promote sustainability.

Understanding and following these laws is essential for ethical and legal engineering practice.


### Summary

Engineering is a profession dedicated to applying scientific knowledge to create practical solutions that benefit society. Chartered Engineers are highly skilled professionals who carry significant responsibilities, including ensuring public safety, ethical conduct, and sustainable development. Their roles are diverse, ranging from technical design to leadership and management, all while adhering to strict codes of conduct and legal requirements. The profession offers rewarding career opportunities with the chance to make a meaningful impact on the world.
