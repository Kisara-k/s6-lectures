## 7. Management and Administration for Engineers

## Questions

#### 1. Which of the following resources are typically managed by engineers in managerial roles?  
A) Marketing strategies and customer relationship management  
B) Materials and time management for project completion  
C) Human resources including recruitment and performance evaluation  
D) Financial resources such as budgeting and expenditure control  

#### 2. In the context of government organizations, which statements about administrative regulations are correct?  
A) Financial Regulations apply only to private sector organizations  
B) The Establishment Code governs employee conduct and recruitment in fully government organizations  
C) Public Administration Circulars provide additional guidelines for government administration  
D) Semi-government organizations do not need to comply with any government circulars  

#### 3. Which of the following labour laws in Sri Lanka specifically address employee benefits and compensation?  
A) Payment of Gratuity Act No 12 of 1983  
B) Employees Provident Fund (EPF) Act No 15 of 1958  
C) Industrial Disputes Act No 43 of 1950  
D) Shop and Office Employees Act No 19 of 1954  

#### 4. What are the key functions of management that engineers must understand when taking on managerial roles?  
A) Designing, testing, and implementing technical solutions  
B) Planning, organizing, leading, and controlling  
C) Marketing, sales, and customer service  
D) Budgeting, procurement, and legal compliance  

#### 5. Which of the following statements about procurement processes are true?  
A) Tender Boards are responsible for awarding contracts  
B) Procurement involves defining specifications before inviting suppliers to bid  
C) Preparation of tender documents is done after evaluating bids  
D) Technical Evaluation Committees assess bids based on price, quality, and delivery time  

#### 6. Regarding project management knowledge areas, which of the following are correctly paired with their focus?  
A) Stakeholder Management – Managing expectations of all parties involved  
B) Risk Management – Planning the project schedule and deadlines  
C) Scope Management – Defining what is included and excluded in the project  
D) Communications Management – Ensuring timely and appropriate information flow  

#### 7. Which of the following statements about financial management concepts are accurate?  
A) Capital Expenditure (Capex) includes day-to-day expenses like salaries and utilities  
B) Financial authority levels determine who can approve spending and at what limits  
C) Operational Expenditure (Opex) refers to long-term investments in assets

#### 8. What additional knowledge areas should engineers develop to be effective managers beyond technical skills?  
A) Human resource management including attendance and disciplinary matters  
B) Legal knowledge related to labour laws and government compliances  
C) Business strategy, vision, and mission understanding  
D) Advanced technical design and engineering calculations  

#### 9. Which of the following correctly describe the types of organizations and their management oversight?  
A) Private organizations have more flexibility but must comply with national laws  
B) Provincial government organizations issue their own independent management guidelines separate from the Ministry of Public Administration  
C) Semi-government organizations follow guidelines from the Ministry of Treasury’s Public Enterprise Department  
D) Fully government organizations are managed by a Board of Directors  

#### 10. In human resource management, which of the following employee types require different management approaches?  
A) All employees are managed identically regardless of employment type  
B) Casual employees  
C) Fixed-term or contract employees  
D) Permanent employees

## Answers

#### 1. Which of the following resources are typically managed by engineers in managerial roles?  
A) ✗ Marketing strategies and customer relationship management are usually handled by specialized departments, not typically by engineers.  
B) ✓ Materials and time management are key resources engineers manage in projects.  
C) ✓ Engineers manage human resources including recruitment and performance evaluation.  
D) ✓ Financial resources such as budgeting and expenditure control are part of management responsibilities.  

**Correct:** B, C, D


#### 2. In the context of government organizations, which statements about administrative regulations are correct?  
A) ✗ Financial Regulations apply to government organizations, not only private sector.  
B) ✓ The Establishment Code governs employee conduct and recruitment in fully government organizations.  
C) ✓ Public Administration Circulars provide additional guidelines for government administration.  
D) ✗ Semi-government organizations must comply with government circulars and guidelines.  

**Correct:** B, C


#### 3. Which of the following labour laws in Sri Lanka specifically address employee benefits and compensation?  
A) ✓ Payment of Gratuity Act governs end-of-service payments, a compensation form.  
B) ✓ EPF Act mandates retirement savings, a form of employee benefit.  
C) ✗ Industrial Disputes Act deals with dispute resolution, not benefits.  
D) ✗ Shop and Office Employees Act regulates working conditions, not specifically benefits.  

**Correct:** A, B


#### 4. What are the key functions of management that engineers must understand when taking on managerial roles?  
A) ✗ Designing, testing, and implementing are technical tasks, not management functions.  
B) ✓ Planning, organizing, leading, and controlling are core management functions.  
C) ✗ Marketing, sales, and customer service are not core management functions for engineers.  
D) ✗ Budgeting, procurement, and legal compliance are important but fall under broader management functions.  

**Correct:** B


#### 5. Which of the following statements about procurement processes are true?  
A) ✓ Tender Boards are responsible for awarding contracts.  
B) ✓ Procurement requires defining specifications before inviting suppliers to bid.  
C) ✗ Preparation of tender documents happens before evaluating bids, not after.  
D) ✓ Technical Evaluation Committees assess bids based on price, quality, and delivery time.  

**Correct:** A, B, D


#### 6. Regarding project management knowledge areas, which of the following are correctly paired with their focus?  
A) ✓ Stakeholder Management involves managing expectations of all parties involved.  
B) ✗ Risk Management deals with identifying and responding to risks, not scheduling.  
C) ✓ Scope Management defines what is included and excluded in the project.  
D) ✓ Communications Management ensures timely and appropriate information flow.  

**Correct:** A, C, D


#### 7. Which of the following statements about financial management concepts are accurate?  
A) ✗ Capital Expenditure (Capex) refers to long-term investments, not daily expenses.  
B) ✓ Financial authority levels determine who can approve spending and at what limits.  
C) ✗ Operational Expenditure (Opex) refers to day-to-day expenses, not long-term investments.  
D) ✓ Budgeting involves planning how money will be allocated and spent.  

**Correct:** B, D


#### 8. What additional knowledge areas should engineers develop to be effective managers beyond technical skills?  
A) ✓ Human resource management including attendance and disciplinary matters is essential.  
B) ✓ Legal knowledge related to labour laws and government compliances is important.  
C) ✓ Business strategy, vision, and mission understanding are critical for managerial roles.  
D) ✗ Advanced technical design is important for engineers but not an additional management skill.  

**Correct:** A, B, C


#### 9. Which of the following correctly describe the types of organizations and their management oversight?  
A) ✓ Private organizations have more flexibility but must comply with national laws.  
B) ✗ Provincial government organizations follow guidelines issued by the Ministry of Public Administration, not independent ones.  
C) ✓ Semi-government organizations follow guidelines from the Ministry of Treasury’s Public Enterprise Department.  
D) ✗ Fully government organizations are managed by ministries, not Boards of Directors.  

**Correct:** A, C


#### 10. In human resource management, which of the following employee types require different management approaches?  
A) ✗ All employees are not managed identically; management varies by employment type.  
B) ✓ Casual employees are managed differently due to their temporary nature.  
C) ✓ Fixed-term or contract employees have different terms and management needs.  
D) ✓ Permanent employees have different rights and benefits compared to others.  

**Correct:** B, C, D
