## Answers

#### 1. What best describes the role of an engineer in society?  
A) ✗ It’s not just the tasks, but how they are done that matters  
B) ✓ Engineers’ impact and manner of work are key  
C) ✗ Knowledge alone doesn’t define the role fully  
D) ✗ Blindly following orders ignores social responsibility  

**Correct:** B


#### 2. Which of the following accurately define "morality"?  
A) ✓ Morality is about societal rules on right and wrong  
B) ✗ Laws are formal rules, not morality itself  
C) ✗ This defines ethics, not morality directly  
D) ✗ Morality is societal, not just personal opinion  

**Correct:** A


#### 3. How does ethics differ from legality?  
A) ✓ Ethics is based on morality; legality on laws by rulers  
B) ✗ Not all ethical actions are covered by law  
C) ✓ Laws can be unethical if rulers are unethical  
D) ✗ Ethics and legality can differ significantly  

**Correct:** A, C
