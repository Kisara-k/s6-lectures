## 8. CSE Perspective

## Questions

#### 1. What distinguishes ethics from legality in professional practice?  
A) All ethical actions are always covered by law.  
B) Ethics is based on societal morality, while legality is based on laws made by rulers or parliaments.  
C) Laws can be unethical if they serve only the interests of those in power.  
D) Ethical rulers always make unethical laws.  

#### 2. Which of the following are characteristics that define a profession?  
A) Autonomy in decision-making and evaluation of work.  
B) Expert knowledge gained through formal education.  
C) Focus solely on maximizing personal financial gain.  
D) Governance through setting entrance requirements and disciplining members.  

#### 3. How do professional bodies differ from trade unions?  
A) Trade unions enforce codes of conduct for ethical behavior.  
B) Professional bodies focus on governance and ethical standards.  
C) Trade unions primarily focus on protecting workers’ rights and benefits.  
D) Both professional bodies and trade unions regulate entry requirements for professions.  

#### 4. Which statements about professional licensing are true?  
A) Licensing ensures minimum competence and ethical standards in professions like medicine and engineering.  
B) Vendor certifications in IT serve as an alternative to professional licensing.  
C) Professional licensing is irrelevant to protecting public safety.  
D) Licensing is mandatory for all IT professionals worldwide.  

#### 5. In the context of society and rules, which of the following are accurate?  
A) Morality is the legal framework established by governments.  
B) Rules are designed to advance the good of members over time.  
C) Ethics involves rational evaluation of morality and behavior.  
D) Society is an association of people organized under a system of rules.  

#### 6. Which of the following scenarios illustrate a conflict between legal and ethical perspectives?  
A) Banning a public demonstration against a repressive regime.  
B) Making public pronouncements about flaws in the judiciary that may be considered contempt of court.  
C) Causing harm to public property.  
D) Giving a convicted murderer due process.  

#### 7. What roles do professional bodies play in maintaining a profession?  
A) Specifying entry requirements to the profession.  
B) Negotiating salaries and working conditions for members.  
C) Defining how members should behave ethically.  
D) Enforcing laws passed by the government.  

#### 8. Which of the following best describe the concept of autonomy in a profession?  
A) Complete freedom to act without any accountability.  
B) Following orders without questioning ethical implications.  
C) Making decisions under the supervision of a chartered professional.  
D) The ability to independently diagnose, treat, follow up, and evaluate work outcomes.  

#### 9. Why might some laws be considered unethical despite being legal?  
A) Because ethical behavior is always illegal.  
B) Because laws may serve the interests of rulers rather than the good of society.  
C) Because laws are always created by unethical rulers.  
D) Because laws never cover all ethical considerations.  

#### 10. Which of the following statements about ethics in the Information Age are correct?  
A) Ethicality always aligns perfectly with legality in technology-related decisions.  
B) Ethical considerations are less important in technology because laws cover all necessary rules.  
C) Rational examination of morality is essential for responsible use of information technology.  
D) Ethics involves evaluating behavior in the context of modern technology’s impact on society.

## Answers

#### 1. What distinguishes ethics from legality in professional practice?  
A) ✗ Not all ethical actions are covered by law; ethics can go beyond legal requirements.  
B) ✓ Ethics is based on societal morality, while legality is based on laws made by rulers or parliaments.  
C) ✓ Laws can be unethical if they serve only the interests of those in power rather than society.  
D) ✗ Ethical rulers do not make unethical laws; this contradicts the definition of ethical rulership.  

**Correct:** B, C


#### 2. Which of the following are characteristics that define a profession?  
A) ✓ Autonomy in decision-making is a key professional trait.  
B) ✓ Expert knowledge gained through formal education distinguishes a profession from mere skill.  
C) ✗ Professions focus on service to society, not just personal financial gain.  
D) ✓ Governance through entrance requirements and discipline is essential for professional integrity.  

**Correct:** A, B, D


#### 3. How do professional bodies differ from trade unions?  
A) ✗ Trade unions do not enforce ethical codes; this is the role of professional bodies.  
B) ✓ Professional bodies focus on governance and ethical standards.  
C) ✓ Trade unions focus on protecting workers’ rights and benefits.  
D) ✗ Only professional bodies regulate entry requirements, not trade unions.  

**Correct:** B, C


#### 4. Which statements about professional licensing are true?  
A) ✓ Licensing ensures minimum competence and ethical standards in regulated professions.  
B) ✓ Vendor certifications in IT often serve as alternative proof of proficiency.  
C) ✗ Licensing is important to protect public safety by ensuring qualified practitioners.  
D) ✗ Licensing is not mandatory for all IT professionals worldwide; IT often lacks formal licensing.  

**Correct:** A, B


#### 5. In the context of society and rules, which of the following are accurate?  
A) ✗ Morality is not the legal framework; it is about societal conduct and what people ought to do.  
B) ✓ Rules are designed to advance the good of members over time.  
C) ✓ Ethics involves rational evaluation of morality and behavior.  
D) ✓ Society is an association of people organized under a system of rules.  

**Correct:** B, C, D


#### 6. Which of the following scenarios illustrate a conflict between legal and ethical perspectives?  
A) ✓ Banning a public demonstration against a repressive regime is legal but ethically questionable.  
B) ✓ Making public pronouncements about judiciary flaws may be legal issue (contempt) but ethically important.  
C) ✗ Causing harm to public property is both illegal and unethical, so no conflict.  
D) ✓ Giving a convicted murderer due process is both legal and ethical, but can be seen as a tension in justice.  

**Correct:** A, B, D


#### 7. What roles do professional bodies play in maintaining a profession?  
A) ✓ Specifying entry requirements is essential for maintaining professional standards.  
B) ✗ Negotiating salaries and working conditions is typically the role of trade unions, not professional bodies.  
C) ✓ Defining ethical behavior is a core role of professional bodies.  
D) ✗ Enforcing government laws is not the role of professional bodies; they govern the profession internally.  

**Correct:** A, C


#### 8. Which of the following best describe the concept of autonomy in a profession?  
A) ✗ Autonomy does not mean acting without accountability; professionals are responsible for their decisions.  
B) ✗ Blindly following orders without ethical consideration contradicts professional autonomy.  
C) ✗ Making decisions under supervision is not full autonomy; autonomy implies independent judgment.  
D) ✓ Autonomy means independent diagnosis, treatment, follow-up, and evaluation.  

**Correct:** D


#### 9. Why might some laws be considered unethical despite being legal?  
A) ✗ Ethical behavior is not always illegal; this is false.  
B) ✓ Laws may serve rulers’ interests rather than society’s good, making them unethical.  
C) ✗ Not all laws are created by unethical rulers; this is an overgeneralization.  
D) ✓ Laws do not cover all ethical considerations, so some ethical issues remain outside legal scope.  

**Correct:** B, D


#### 10. Which of the following statements about ethics in the Information Age are correct?  
A) ✗ Ethicality does not always align perfectly with legality, especially in technology.  
B) ✗ Ethics remain important even if laws exist; laws do not cover all ethical issues.  
C) ✓ Rational examination of morality is essential for responsible use of IT.  
D) ✓ Ethics involves evaluating behavior considering technology’s societal impact.  

**Correct:** C, D
