## 10. Code of Ethics

## Questions

#### 1. Which of the following are true about the purpose of Codes of Ethics and Professional Conduct in ICT?  
A) They are only relevant for software engineers, not other ICT professionals.  
B) They provide prescriptive guidelines to help professionals make ethical decisions.  
C) They serve as legally binding contracts for all ICT professionals worldwide.  
D) They offer recommendations for remediation when ethical violations occur.  

#### 2. The IFIP Code of Ethics includes several parts. Which of the following correctly describe its components?  
A) General Ethical Principles that emphasize honesty, fairness, and respect for privacy.  
B) Professional Responsibilities that require working only within one’s competence and maintaining high standards.  
C) Compliance with the Code, which involves promoting and upholding ethical principles and addressing violations.  
D) Professional Leadership Principles that focus on maximizing profits for organizations.  

#### 3. Regarding the ACM Software Engineering Code of Ethics, which statements are accurate?  
A) Software engineers are encouraged to use software obtained through any means if it benefits the project.  
B) Engineers should prioritize employer interests over public interest in all cases.  
C) Software engineers must approve software only if it is safe and disclose potential dangers.  
D) Maintaining data integrity and respecting privacy are essential parts of the code.  

#### 4. Which of the following best describe the ethical responsibilities of a computing professional acting as a leader?  
A) Encouraging acceptance of social responsibilities within their organization.  
B) Prioritizing rapid system deployment over careful consideration of societal impact.  
C) Managing personnel and resources to improve the quality of working life.  
D) Ensuring the public good is the central concern in all professional work.  

#### 5. Privacy is considered a human right because it supports which of the following?  
A) Development of trusting and intimate relationships.  
B) Concealing illegal activities without consequence.  
C) Individual growth and responsibility.  
D) Freedom to be oneself without external judgment.  

#### 6. Which of the following statements about confidentiality and data protection are correct?  
A) Proper security measures must be employed to protect confidential data.  
B) Data should be shared freely to promote transparency.  
C) Data should be kept indefinitely to allow future analysis.  
D) Data collected for one purpose should not be used for another without consent.  

#### 7. The Right to Information (RTI) Act requires governments to disclose information upon request. What ethical considerations arise from this?  
A) RTI always overrides privacy concerns because transparency is paramount.  
B) Salary details of public employees should never be disclosed under RTI.  
C) Balancing public good and privacy is necessary when releasing information.  
D) RTI disclosures can sometimes infringe on individual privacy rights.  

#### 8. Which of the following are true about the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)?  
A) It allows organizations to use personal data for any purpose once collected.  
B) It came into effect in 2018 and imposes strict rules on data handling.  
C) It applies only to companies based in the European Union.  
D) It strengthens and unifies data protection for individuals within the EU.  

#### 9. In the context of professional competence and ethical practice, which actions are expected from computing professionals?  
A) Ignore existing rules if they conflict with personal beliefs.  
B) Accept and provide appropriate professional review.  
C) Perform work only in areas where they have demonstrated competence.  
D) Strive to achieve high quality in both process and product.  

#### 10. Which of the following statements about the relationship between technology and privacy are accurate?  
A) Technology has made it easier to collect and combine personal information, reducing privacy.  
B) Leaving an electronic trail online has no impact on an individual’s privacy.  
C) Public records are increasingly accessible due to computerized databases and the internet.  
D) Privacy concerns are irrelevant in the digital age because all information is public.

## Answers

#### 1. Which of the following are true about the purpose of Codes of Ethics and Professional Conduct in ICT?  
A) ✗ Codes apply broadly to ICT professionals, not just software engineers.  
B) ✓ They provide prescriptive guidelines to help professionals make ethical decisions.  
C) ✗ They are not legally binding contracts worldwide but recommended practices.  
D) ✓ They offer recommendations for remediation when ethical violations occur.  

**Correct:** B, D


#### 2. The IFIP Code of Ethics includes several parts. Which of the following correctly describe its components?  
A) ✓ General Ethical Principles emphasize honesty, fairness, and respect for privacy.  
B) ✓ Professional Responsibilities include working within competence and maintaining standards.  
C) ✓ Compliance involves promoting ethical principles and addressing violations.  
D) ✗ Leadership principles focus on public good and ethical management, not maximizing profits.  

**Correct:** A, B, C


#### 3. Regarding the ACM Software Engineering Code of Ethics, which statements are accurate?  
A) ✗ Using illegally obtained software is prohibited regardless of project benefit.  
B) ✗ Public interest takes precedence over employer interests unless overridden by moral concerns.  
C) ✓ Engineers must approve software only if safe and disclose dangers.  
D) ✓ Maintaining data integrity and respecting privacy are key ethical requirements.  

**Correct:** C, D


#### 4. Which of the following best describe the ethical responsibilities of a computing professional acting as a leader?  
A) ✓ Encouraging social responsibility within the organization is part of leadership.  
B) ✗ Prioritizing rapid deployment over societal impact contradicts ethical leadership.  
C) ✓ Managing personnel and resources to improve work quality is expected.  
D) ✓ Ensuring public good is central to all work is a core leadership responsibility.  

**Correct:** A, C, D


#### 5. Privacy is considered a human right because it supports which of the following?  
A) ✓ It supports development of trusting and intimate relationships.  
B) ✗ Privacy is not meant to conceal illegal or immoral activities.  
C) ✓ Privacy enables individual growth and responsibility.  
D) ✓ It allows freedom to be oneself without external judgment.  

**Correct:** A, C, D


#### 6. Which of the following statements about confidentiality and data protection are correct?  
A) ✓ Proper security measures are essential to protect confidential data.  
B) ✗ Data should not be shared freely; sharing must be controlled and justified.  
C) ✗ Data should be destroyed responsibly when no longer needed, not kept indefinitely.  
D) ✓ Data collected for one purpose should not be reused without consent.  

**Correct:** A, D


#### 7. The Right to Information (RTI) Act requires governments to disclose information upon request. What ethical considerations arise from this?  
A) ✗ RTI does not always override privacy; balance is required.  
B) ✗ Salary details may be disclosed but must be carefully considered; not categorically forbidden.  
C) ✓ Balancing public good and privacy is necessary when releasing information.  
D) ✓ RTI disclosures can infringe on individual privacy rights.  

**Correct:** C, D


#### 8. Which of the following are true about the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)?  
A) ✗ Organizations cannot use personal data for any purpose without consent.  
B) ✓ Came into effect in 2018 with strict data handling rules.  
C) ✗ GDPR applies to any organization processing EU residents’ data, not just EU-based companies.  
D) ✓ It strengthens and unifies data protection within the EU.  

**Correct:** B, D


#### 9. In the context of professional competence and ethical practice, which actions are expected from computing professionals?  
A) ✗ Ignoring existing rules conflicts with ethical practice.  
B) ✓ Accepting and providing professional review is essential for quality and accountability.  
C) ✓ Working only within demonstrated competence is required.  
D) ✓ Striving for high quality in process and product is expected.  

**Correct:** B, C, D


#### 10. Which of the following statements about the relationship between technology and privacy are accurate?  
A) ✓ Technology has made personal information collection easier, reducing privacy.  
B) ✗ Leaving an electronic trail significantly impacts privacy.  
C) ✓ Public records are more accessible due to digital databases and the internet.  
D) ✗ Privacy remains important despite digital transparency; it is not irrelevant.  

**Correct:** A, C
