## 1. Philosophy of Ethics

## Questions

#### 1. Which of the following best describe the relationship between values, morality, ethics, and law?  
A) Laws are formal rules often based on societal moral principles.  
B) Values are stable beliefs that influence both morality and ethics.  
C) Morality is a systematic study of right and wrong, while ethics are social norms.  
D) Ethics attempts to develop methods to determine which values should guide behavior.  

#### 2. Which statements accurately distinguish ethics from morals?  
A) Morals are personal principles shaped by culture and religion, while ethics are practical rules created by groups or professions.  
B) Morals are external codes imposed by society, whereas ethics are internal personal beliefs.  
C) Ethics always align perfectly with an individual’s personal morals.  
D) Ethics can sometimes require actions that conflict with a person’s moral beliefs.  

#### 3. Which of the following are examples of intrinsic values?  
A) Courage  
B) Wealth  
C) Teamwork  
D) Honesty  

#### 4. How do Eastern and Western ethics fundamentally differ in their approach?  
A) Western ethics are rooted in rational thought and logic, whereas Eastern ethics are more holistic and culturally embedded.  
B) Both traditions reject spiritual or religious influences in ethical thinking.  
C) Eastern ethics often involve internal self-imposed rules, while Western ethics rely on external societal rules.  
D) Eastern ethics emphasize self-direction and personal understanding, while Western ethics focus on universal interconnections.  

#### 5. Which of the following statements about professional ethics are true?  
A) Professional ethics are identical to personal ethics and never conflict.  
B) Personal beliefs always override professional ethical codes in decision-making.  
C) Professional ethics are a set of rules designed to protect the reputation of a profession.  
D) Professional ethics require engineers to adhere to standards that ensure public safety and trust.  

#### 6. Why is studying professional ethics particularly important for engineers?

B) Because engineers’ mistakes can lead to loss of life, not just financial loss.  
C) It allows engineers to avoid all ethical dilemmas in their work.  
D) It ensures engineers always follow the law without question.  

#### 7. Which of the following scenarios illustrate an ethical dilemma in engineering?  
A) A doctor respects a patient’s treatment choice despite personal disagreement.  
B) A project manager asks for a kick-back during a financial crisis.  
C) An engineer follows all company policies without question.

#### 8. Which of the following statements about the term "Ethos" and its relation to ethics is correct?

B) Ethos means “character” and refers to the guiding beliefs of a community or ideology.  
C) Ethics is the philosophy of rightness and wrongness of human action.

#### 9. Which of the following statements about the relationship between ethics and law are accurate?  
A) Ethics can vary between organizations and social groups, while laws are enforced by authorities.  
B) Ethics and laws are identical in their scope and application.  
C) Laws are always ethical and based on universal moral principles.  
D) Laws regulate conduct externally, whereas ethics involve internal reasoning about right and wrong.  

#### 10. Consider the example of a lawyer who reveals a client’s guilt in court out of a moral desire for justice. Which of the following statements are true?  
A) The lawyer’s action is moral but unethical because it violates professional confidentiality.  
B) Ethics and morals always align in professional settings.  
C) The lawyer’s action is both ethical and moral.  
D) This example shows that ethical codes can sometimes conflict with personal morals.

## Answers

#### 1. Which of the following best describe the relationship between values, morality, ethics, and law?  
A) ✓ Laws are formal rules often based on societal moral principles.  
B) ✓ Values are stable beliefs that influence both morality and ethics.  
C) ✗ Morality is about social norms, ethics is the systematic study, so this reverses their roles.  
D) ✓ Ethics attempts to develop methods to determine which values should guide behavior.  

**Correct:** A, B, D


#### 2. Which statements accurately distinguish ethics from morals?  
A) ✓ Morals are personal principles shaped by culture and religion, while ethics are practical rules created by groups or professions.  
B) ✗ Morals are internal personal beliefs, not external codes imposed by society.  
C) ✗ Ethics do not always align with personal morals; conflicts can occur.  
D) ✓ Ethics can sometimes require actions that conflict with a person’s moral beliefs.  

**Correct:** A, D


#### 3. Which of the following are examples of intrinsic values?  
A) ✓ Courage is an intrinsic value, a virtue valued for its own sake.  
B) ✗ Wealth is an extrinsic value, a means to an end.  
C) ✗ Teamwork is an extrinsic value, a means to achieve goals.  
D) ✓ Honesty is an intrinsic value, good in itself.  

**Correct:** A, D


#### 4. How do Eastern and Western ethics fundamentally differ in their approach?  
A) ✓ Western ethics are rational and logical; Eastern ethics are holistic and culturally embedded.  
B) ✗ Both traditions include spiritual or religious influences.  
C) ✓ Eastern ethics are often internally imposed, Western ethics rely more on external societal rules.  
D) ✗ This reverses the focus; Eastern ethics emphasize universal interconnections, not self-direction.  

**Correct:** A, C


#### 5. Which of the following statements about professional ethics are true?  
A) ✗ Personal and professional ethics can conflict; they are not always identical.  
B) ✗ Personal beliefs do not always override professional codes; sometimes professional ethics must be followed despite personal views.  
C) ✓ Professional ethics protect the profession’s reputation by guiding conduct.  
D) ✓ Professional ethics require engineers to ensure public safety and trust.  

**Correct:** C, D


#### 6. Why is studying professional ethics particularly important for engineers?  
A) ✓ It helps develop ethical sensitivity, judgment, and willpower.  
B) ✓ Engineers’ mistakes can cost lives, not just money.  
C) ✗ Studying ethics does not eliminate all ethical dilemmas; it prepares one to handle them.  
D) ✗ Ethics requires critical thinking, not blind obedience to law.  

**Correct:** A, B


#### 7. Which of the following scenarios illustrate an ethical dilemma in engineering?  
A) ✗ This is a medical ethics example, not engineering.  
B) ✓ Asking for a kick-back creates a conflict between financial survival and ethics.  
C) ✗ Following all policies without question is not a dilemma but compliance.  
D) ✗ Delaying a launch to complete QA is an ethical decision, not a dilemma.  

**Correct:** B


#### 8. Which of the following statements about the term "Ethos" and its relation to ethics is correct?  
A) ✗ Ethos means character, not law.  
B) ✓ Ethos means “character” and describes guiding beliefs of a community.  
C) ✓ Ethics is the philosophy of rightness and wrongness of human action.  
D) ✗ Ethos is directly related to rightness and wrongness.  

**Correct:** B, C


#### 9. Which of the following statements about the relationship between ethics and law are accurate?  
A) ✓ Ethics can vary by group; laws are externally enforced.  
B) ✗ Ethics and laws differ in scope and application.  
C) ✗ Laws are not always ethical or based on universal morals.  
D) ✓ Laws regulate externally; ethics involve internal reasoning.  

**Correct:** A, D


#### 10. Consider the example of a lawyer who reveals a client’s guilt in court out of a moral desire for justice. Which of the following statements are true?  
A) ✓ The lawyer’s action is moral but unethical due to confidentiality rules.  
B) ✗ Ethics and morals do not always align in professional settings.  
C) ✗ The action is not ethical because it violates professional codes.  
D) ✓ This example shows ethical codes can conflict with personal morals.  

**Correct:** A, D
