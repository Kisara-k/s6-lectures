## 4. Engineering Ethics 2

## Questions

#### 1. Which of the following characteristics define a professional in the engineering field?  
A) Viewing other members of the profession as competitors rather than colleagues  
B) Accepting and adhering to a code of ethics  
C) Exercising judgment in occupational tasks while following professional standards  
D) Possessing specialized education and training in engineering  

#### 2. Which of the following are considered core professional values essential for engineers?  
A) Continuous learning and teamwork  
B) Integrity and professional attitude  
C) Accountability and responsibility  
D) Personal gain and competition at any cost  

#### 3. Professional ethics in engineering primarily serve to:  
A) Dictate technical specifications for engineering projects  
B) Replace legal requirements and regulations  
C) Provide a set of rules that all members of the profession must follow  
D) Guide behavior towards other people and institutions in a professional environment  

#### 4. How do skills and competencies differ in the context of engineering professionalism?  
A) Competencies are easier to develop and measure than skills  
B) Competencies do not increase with career level, unlike skills  
C) Skills are transferable between roles without adaptation, but competencies require contextual adjustment  
D) Skills are specific, measurable abilities related to tasks, while competencies include broader abilities combining knowledge, experience, and attitude  

#### 5. The International Engineering Alliance (IEA) influences engineering professionalism by:  
A) Setting internationally recognized educational qualifications through accords like the Washington Accord  
B) Enforcing local engineering laws and regulations in member countries  
C) Establishing competency standards for professional engineers globally  
D) Certifying engineers directly without regard to national licensing bodies  

#### 6. Which of the following statements about the roles of engineers, technologists, and technicians is true?  
A) Engineers solve complex engineering problems, while technicians handle well-defined practical tasks  
B) Technicians are responsible for broadly defined engineering problems  
C) Engineering technologists have the same responsibilities as engineers but with less education  
D) Each role has a distinct knowledge profile and level of responsibility to the public  

#### 7. In engineering ethics, the principle of “public goodness is paramount” means:  
A) Engineers must prioritize the health, safety, and welfare of the public above all else  
B) Engineers should focus primarily on maximizing profits for their employers  
C) Proper utilization of funds and resources is essential to protect public interests  
D) Respecting the law is optional if it conflicts with client demands  

#### 8. Which of the following best describe the ethical obligation of engineers regarding their area of competence?  
A) Engineers should only perform services within their area of competence to ensure quality and safety  
B) Expanding beyond one’s competence is acceptable if it benefits the employer financially  
C) Limiting work to one’s competence helps uphold professional dignity and public trust  
D) Engineers can take on any project as long as they have a team to support them  

#### 9. How do leadership and communication skills relate to engineering ethics?  
A) Leadership skills allow engineers to bypass ethical codes when necessary  
B) They enable engineers to manage teams and stakeholders effectively during ethical dilemmas  
C) Good communication helps engineers express opinions truthfully and objectively  
D) They are unrelated to ethical behavior and focus only on project management  

#### 10. Continuous professional development in engineering ethics involves:  
A) Focusing solely on technical skills without regard to ethical principles  
B) Viewing learning as a one-time activity completed during formal education  
C) Encouraging and assisting junior engineers to improve their knowledge and experience  
D) Regularly updating technical knowledge and skills throughout one’s career

## Answers

#### 1. Which of the following characteristics define a professional in the engineering field?  
A) ✗ Professionals see peers as colleagues, not competitors.  
B) ✓ Accepting and following a code of ethics is fundamental to professionalism.  
C) ✓ Exercising judgment and following standards is a key professional trait.  
D) ✓ Possessing specialized education and training is essential to be a professional.  

**Correct:** B, C, D


#### 2. Which of the following are considered core professional values essential for engineers?  
A) ✓ Continuous learning and teamwork are important professional values.  
B) ✓ Integrity and professional attitude are essential values.  
C) ✓ Accountability and responsibility are core values.  
D) ✗ Personal gain and unfair competition contradict professional ethics.  

**Correct:** A, B, C


#### 3. Professional ethics in engineering primarily serve to:  
A) ✗ Ethics guide behavior but do not dictate technical specifications.  
B) ✗ Ethics complement but do not replace legal requirements.  
C) ✓ Provide a set of rules all professionals agree to follow.  
D) ✓ Guide how professionals act toward others in their environment.  

**Correct:** C, D


#### 4. How do skills and competencies differ in the context of engineering professionalism?  
A) ✗ Competencies take longer to develop and are harder to measure than skills.  
B) ✗ Competencies usually increase with career level, unlike skills which may not.  
C) ✓ Skills transfer easily; competencies require adaptation to context.  
D) ✓ Skills are specific abilities; competencies combine knowledge, experience, and attitude.  

**Correct:** C, D


#### 5. The International Engineering Alliance (IEA) influences engineering professionalism by:  
A) ✓ Setting internationally recognized educational qualifications through accords.  
B) ✗ IEA does not enforce local laws; it sets international standards.  
C) ✓ Establishing competency standards for engineers worldwide.  
D) ✗ IEA does not directly certify engineers; national bodies do that.  

**Correct:** A, C


#### 6. Which of the following statements about the roles of engineers, technologists, and technicians is true?  
A) ✓ Engineers solve complex problems; technicians handle well-defined tasks.  
B) ✗ Technicians deal with well-defined, not broadly defined, problems.  
C) ✗ Technologists have different responsibilities, not just less education.  
D) ✓ Each role has distinct knowledge and responsibility levels.  

**Correct:** A, D


#### 7. In engineering ethics, the principle of “public goodness is paramount” means:  
A) ✓ Public health, safety, and welfare come first.  
B) ✗ Profit maximization is secondary to public welfare.  
C) ✓ Proper use of funds/resources protects public interest.  
D) ✗ Respecting the law is mandatory, not optional.  

**Correct:** A, C


#### 8. Which of the following best describe the ethical obligation of engineers regarding their area of competence?  
A) ✓ Engineers must work only within their competence to ensure safety.  
B) ✗ Expanding beyond competence for financial gain is unethical.  
C) ✓ Limiting work to competence upholds dignity and trust.  
D) ✗ Taking any project without competence risks quality and ethics.  

**Correct:** A, C


#### 9. How do leadership and communication skills relate to engineering ethics?  
A) ✗ Leadership does not justify ignoring ethical codes.  
B) ✓ They help manage teams and stakeholders during ethical dilemmas.  
C) ✓ Good communication ensures truthful and objective expression.  
D) ✗ They are integral to ethical behavior, not just project management.  

**Correct:** B, C


#### 10. Continuous professional development in engineering ethics involves:  
A) ✗ Ethical principles are as important as technical skills.  
B) ✗ Learning is ongoing, not a one-time event during education.  
C) ✓ Helping junior engineers improve knowledge and experience.  
D) ✓ Regularly updating knowledge and skills throughout a career.  

**Correct:** C, D
