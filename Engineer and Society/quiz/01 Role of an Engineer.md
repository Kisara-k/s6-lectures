## 2. Role of an Engineer

## Questions

#### 1. What distinguishes an engineer from a scientist?  
A) Scientists primarily work on practical applications for society’s benefit.  
B) Engineers innovate devices and solutions based on scientific theories.  
C) Scientists study the world as it is, engineers create new things that have never existed.  
D) Engineers focus on discovering natural phenomena, while scientists apply these discoveries to create solutions.  

#### 2. Which of the following are essential competencies required to become a Chartered Engineer?  
A) Exclusive focus on technical skills without need for communication abilities.  
B) Leadership and management skills.  
C) Commitment to professional ethics and continuous learning.  
D) Deep knowledge of engineering principles and ability to design systems.  

#### 3. In the context of social responsibilities, which actions are expected from engineers?  
A) Voluntarily educating the public about technological risks.  
B) Refusing to work on projects that may harm public safety.  
C) Speaking out publicly against unethical or illegal engineering practices.  
D) Ignoring environmental impacts if the project is profitable.  

#### 4. Which statements about the role of engineers in society are true?  
A) Engineers must prioritize public safety and welfare above all other interests.  
B) Engineers have no obligation to report illegal or unethical activities in their work.  
C) Engineers are only responsible for the technical success of their projects, not societal impact.  
D) Engineers should ensure efficient use of society’s resources related to technology.  

#### 5. What are the key responsibilities of an engineer when managing a project?  
A) Optimizing resources without compromising overall project quality.  
B) Complying with relevant laws and regulations throughout the project lifecycle.  
C) Delegating all decisions to subordinates to avoid accountability.  
D) Ensuring timely delivery while maintaining quality and safety standards.  

#### 6. Which of the following best describe the ethical principles engineers must uphold?  
A) Prioritizing personal gain over public interest when necessary.  
B) Acting with integrity and fairness in all professional dealings.  
C) Encouraging vocational progress of colleagues and juniors.  
D) Safeguarding health, safety, and environmental sustainability.  

#### 7. How does the “precautionary principle” influence engineering design?  
A) It allows engineers to ignore environmental concerns if the project is economically beneficial.  
B) It advises avoiding actions that could cause serious harm when risks are uncertain.  
C) It encourages engineers to proceed with projects even if risks are unknown.  
D) It requires engineers to build safety redundancies into their designs.  

#### 8. Which of the following are typical roles an engineer may perform in a professional setting?  
A) Decision maker and planner.  
B) Team leader and motivator.  
C) Solely a technical expert without management responsibilities.  
D) Representative and rule enforcer.  

#### 9. What legislative and regulatory frameworks must engineers comply with?  
A) Safety and Factory Ordinance and Environmental Regulations.  
B) Industrial Dispute Act.  
C) Engineering Council Act and Institution of Engineers Act.  
D) Only internal company policies, not national laws.  

#### 10. Which of the following statements about the benefits of being an engineer are accurate?  
A) The profession provides financial security and societal prestige.  
B) Engineers have limited career variety and mostly routine tasks.  
C) Engineers rarely contribute to technological and scientific discovery.  
D) Engineering offers intellectual development and creative thinking opportunities.

## Answers

#### 1. What distinguishes an engineer from a scientist?  
A) ✗ Scientists focus on understanding, not primarily on practical applications.  
B) ✓ Engineers innovate solutions based on scientific theories.  
C) ✓ Engineers create new things; scientists study existing phenomena.  
D) ✗ Engineers apply discoveries; scientists discover phenomena, not the other way around.  

**Correct:** B, C


#### 2. Which of the following are essential competencies required to become a Chartered Engineer?  
A) ✗ Communication skills are essential; exclusive focus on technical skills is insufficient.  
B) ✓ Leadership and management are key competencies.  
C) ✓ Professional ethics and continuous learning are required.  
D) ✓ Knowledge and design skills are fundamental.  

**Correct:** B, C, D


#### 3. In the context of social responsibilities, which actions are expected from engineers?  
A) ✓ Educating the public about risks is part of social responsibility.  
B) ✓ Refusing harmful projects aligns with social responsibility.  
C) ✓ Speaking out against unethical practices is expected.  
D) ✗ Ignoring environmental impacts contradicts ethical engineering.  

**Correct:** A, B, C


#### 4. Which statements about the role of engineers in society are true?  
A) ✓ Public safety and welfare must be prioritized.  
B) ✗ Engineers must report illegal or unethical activities.  
C) ✗ Engineers are responsible for societal impact, not just technical success.  
D) ✓ Efficient use of society’s resources is a key responsibility.  

**Correct:** A, D


#### 5. What are the key responsibilities of an engineer when managing a project?  
A) ✓ Optimizing resources without compromising quality is required.  
B) ✓ Compliance with laws and regulations is mandatory.  
C) ✗ Delegating all decisions to avoid accountability is irresponsible.  
D) ✓ Timely delivery with quality and safety is essential.  

**Correct:** A, B, D


#### 6. Which of the following best describe the ethical principles engineers must uphold?  
A) ✗ Personal gain should never override public interest.  
B) ✓ Integrity and fairness are core ethical values.  
C) ✓ Supporting vocational progress of others is part of professional ethics.  
D) ✓ Protecting health, safety, and environment is fundamental.  

**Correct:** B, C, D


#### 7. How does the “precautionary principle” influence engineering design?  
A) ✗ Ignoring environmental concerns contradicts the principle.  
B) ✓ Avoiding actions with uncertain but serious risks is the principle’s core.  
C) ✗ It advises caution, not proceeding blindly with unknown risks.  
D) ✓ Building safety redundancies aligns with precautionary thinking.  

**Correct:** B, D


#### 8. Which of the following are typical roles an engineer may perform in a professional setting?  
A) ✓ Decision maker and planner are typical responsibilities.  
B) ✓ Team leader and motivator are common roles.  
C) ✗ Engineers usually have management duties beyond technical expertise.  
D) ✓ Representative and rule enforcer roles are often required.  

**Correct:** A, B, D


#### 9. What legislative and regulatory frameworks must engineers comply with?  
A) ✓ Safety and Factory Ordinance and Environmental Regulations are relevant.  
B) ✓ Industrial Dispute Act affects labor relations engineers must consider.  
C) ✓ Engineering Council Act and IESL Act regulate engineering practice.  
D) ✗ Compliance with national laws is mandatory, not just internal policies.  

**Correct:** A, B, C


#### 10. Which of the following statements about the benefits of being an engineer are accurate?  
A) ✓ Financial security and prestige are common advantages.  
B) ✗ Engineering offers diverse and challenging work, not just routine tasks.  
C) ✗ Engineers contribute significantly to technological and scientific discovery.  
D) ✓ Intellectual development and creativity are key benefits.  

**Correct:** A, D
