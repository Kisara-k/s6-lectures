## 2. Role of an Engineer

## Questions

#### 1. What distinguishes an engineer from a scientist?  
A) Engineers create practical solutions based on scientific discoveries.  
B) Scientists apply engineering principles to build devices.  
C) Scientists focus on explaining natural phenomena.  
D) Engineers primarily study the world as it is.

#### 2. Which of the following are essential competencies for a Chartered Engineer?  
A) Deep knowledge of engineering principles.  
B) Ability to communicate and lead teams.  
C) Exclusive focus on technical skills without management.  
D) Commitment to professional ethics and continuous learning.

#### 3. What is the primary responsibility of an engineer in society?  
A) Maximizing profits for their employer.  
B) Prioritizing public safety and welfare.  
C) Innovating without regard to environmental impact.  
D) Ensuring the reliability and safety of their designs.

#### 4. Which actions reflect an engineer’s social responsibility?  
A) Refusing to work on unethical projects.  
B) Ignoring whistleblowing to protect company interests.  
C) Educating the public about technological risks.  
D) Designing sustainable and environmentally friendly technologies.

#### 5. The “precautionary principle” in engineering design means:  
A) Proceeding with projects regardless of potential risks.  
B) Avoiding actions that could cause serious harm when risks are uncertain.  
C) Prioritizing cost savings over safety concerns.  
D) Building redundancy and safety factors into designs.

#### 6. Which of the following best describe the benefits of being an engineer?  
A) Limited career options but high financial rewards.  
B) Intellectual development and creative thinking.  
C) Job satisfaction from solving real-world problems.  
D) Guaranteed prestige without professional responsibility.

#### 7. According to professional codes of conduct, engineers must:  
A) Act with integrity and fairness in all professional matters.  
B) Prioritize company profits over public safety.  
C) Safeguard public health, safety, and the environment.  
D) Disregard the vocational progress of junior engineers.

#### 8. Which roles can an engineer typically perform in a professional setting?  
A) Team leader and motivator.  
B) Decision maker and planner.  
C) Solely a technical specialist without management duties.  
D) Representative and rule enforcer.

#### 9. What are key responsibilities of an engineer overseeing a project?  
A) Ensuring timely delivery and adherence to safety standards.  
B) Ignoring resource optimization to speed up work.  
C) Maintaining quality without compromising safety.  
D) Complying with relevant laws and regulations.

#### 10. Which of the following are included in the categories of engineering responsibilities?  
A) Network planning and design.  
B) Marketing and sales strategy.  
C) Operation and maintenance.  
D) Research and development.

#### 11. What are important job functions of an engineer in managing a team?  
A) Planning and budgeting aligned with corporate goals.  
B) Allocating resources and assigning targets.  
C) Ignoring staff well-being to focus on deadlines.  
D) Organizing competency development programs.

#### 12. Which legislative acts are relevant to engineering practice?  
A) Engineering Council Act 04/2017.  
B) Safety and Factory Ordinance.  
C) Environmental Regulations.  
D) Copyright Act.

#### 13. How should engineers handle situations where a project may harm society?  
A) Speak out publicly against the project.  
B) Continue work without raising concerns.  
C) Refuse to participate if ethical standards are violated.  
D) Report wrongdoing through appropriate channels.

#### 14. Which statements about the relationship between society and engineers are true?  
A) Society demands goods and services that are reliable and safe.  
B) Engineers should prioritize innovation over standards.  
C) Economy and safety are key values engineers must deliver.  
D) Engineers have no responsibility toward environmental sustainability.

#### 15. What does it mean for an engineer to “exercise professional skill and judgment”?  
A) Applying knowledge to solve problems responsibly.  
B) Making decisions without considering ethical implications.  
C) Discharging responsibilities with integrity.  
D) Delegating all critical decisions to non-engineers.

#### 16. Which of the following reflect an engineer’s commitment to sustainability?  
A) Designing technologies that minimize environmental impact.  
B) Ignoring long-term consequences for short-term gains.  
C) Providing expert advice to non-experts about environmental risks.  
D) Abiding by principles of sustainable development.

#### 17. What is the significance of “building in redundancy” in engineering design?  
A) It increases the complexity unnecessarily.  
B) It enhances safety by providing backup systems.  
C) It reduces costs by eliminating extra components.  
D) It ensures continued operation if one part fails.

#### 18. Which of the following are true about the ethical responsibilities of engineers?  
A) They must protect the public interest in health and safety.  
B) They can ignore environmental concerns if the project is profitable.  
C) They should encourage the professional growth of colleagues.  
D) They must uphold the dignity and reputation of the profession.

#### 19. How does an engineer contribute to educating future engineers about social responsibility?  
A) By mentoring and sharing knowledge about ethical practices.  
B) By focusing only on technical skills and ignoring ethics.  
C) By promoting awareness of the societal impact of engineering.  
D) By discouraging discussion of controversial projects.

#### 20. Which of the following best describe the difference between engineering and science?  
A) Engineering applies scientific knowledge to solve practical problems.  
B) Science focuses on creating new devices and systems.  
C) Engineering is concerned with economic use of materials and forces.  
D) Scientists innovate solutions for societal benefit.

## Answers

#### 1. What distinguishes an engineer from a scientist?  
A) ✓ Engineers create practical solutions based on scientific discoveries.  
B) ✗ Scientists apply engineering principles to build devices. (Scientists focus on discovery, not application.)  
C) ✓ Scientists focus on explaining natural phenomena.  
D) ✗ Engineers primarily study the world as it is. (Engineers create new solutions, not just study existing conditions.)  

**Correct:** A, C


#### 2. Which of the following are essential competencies for a Chartered Engineer?  
A) ✓ Deep knowledge of engineering principles.  
B) ✓ Ability to communicate and lead teams.  
C) ✗ Exclusive focus on technical skills without management. (Management and leadership are also required.)  
D) ✓ Commitment to professional ethics and continuous learning.  

**Correct:** A, B, D


#### 3. What is the primary responsibility of an engineer in society?  
A) ✗ Maximizing profits for their employer. (Profit is secondary to public safety.)  
B) ✓ Prioritizing public safety and welfare.  
C) ✗ Innovating without regard to environmental impact. (Environmental impact must be considered.)  
D) ✓ Ensuring the reliability and safety of their designs.  

**Correct:** B, D


#### 4. Which actions reflect an engineer’s social responsibility?  
A) ✓ Refusing to work on unethical projects.  
B) ✗ Ignoring whistleblowing to protect company interests. (Engineers must support whistleblowing.)  
C) ✓ Educating the public about technological risks.  
D) ✓ Designing sustainable and environmentally friendly technologies.  

**Correct:** A, C, D
