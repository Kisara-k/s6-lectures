## 2. Role of an Engineer

## Questions

#### 1. What distinguishes an engineer from a scientist?  
A) Scientists focus on explaining natural phenomena.  
B) Scientists apply engineering principles to build devices.  
C) Engineers create practical solutions based on scientific discoveries.  
D) Engineers primarily study the world as it is.

#### 2. Which of the following are essential competencies for a Chartered Engineer?  
A) Deep knowledge of engineering principles.  
B) Ability to communicate and lead teams.

#### 4. Which actions reflect an engineer’s social responsibility?

#### 6. Which of the following best describe the benefits of being an engineer?

B) Guaranteed prestige without professional responsibility.
C) Intellectual development and creative thinking.  
D) Job satisfaction from solving real-world problems.  

#### 7. According to professional codes of conduct, engineers must:  
A) Disregard the vocational progress of junior engineers.
B) Prioritize company profits over public safety.  
C) Act with integrity and fairness in all professional matters.  
D) Safeguard public health, safety, and the environment.  

#### 8. Which roles can an engineer typically perform in a professional setting?  
A) Team leader and motivator.  
B) Representative and rule enforcer.

#### 14. Which statements about the relationship between society and engineers are true?

D) Engineers have no responsibility toward environmental sustainability.

#### 15. What does it mean for an engineer to “exercise professional skill and judgment”?

#### 16. Which of the following reflect an engineer’s commitment to sustainability?

#### 17. What is the significance of “building in redundancy” in engineering design?

#### 18. Which of the following are true about the ethical responsibilities of engineers?

B) They should encourage the professional growth of colleagues.  
C) They can ignore environmental concerns if the project is profitable.

#### 19. How does an engineer contribute to educating future engineers about social responsibility?  
A) By promoting awareness of the societal impact of engineering.  
B) By mentoring and sharing knowledge about ethical practices.  
C) By discouraging discussion of controversial projects.
D) By focusing only on technical skills and ignoring ethics.

A) Engineering is concerned with economic use of materials and forces.  
B) Scientists innovate solutions for societal benefit.
C) Engineering applies scientific knowledge to solve practical problems.  
D) Science focuses on creating new devices and systems.

## Answers

#### 1. What distinguishes an engineer from a scientist?  
A) ✓ Scientists focus on explaining natural phenomena.  
B) ✗ Scientists apply engineering principles to build devices. (Scientists focus on discovery, not application.)  
C) ✓ Engineers create practical solutions based on scientific discoveries.  
D) ✗ Engineers primarily study the world as it is. (Engineers create new solutions, not just study existing conditions.)  

**Correct:** A, C


#### 2. Which of the following are essential competencies for a Chartered Engineer?  
A) ✓ Deep knowledge of engineering principles.  
B) ✓ Ability to communicate and lead teams.  
C) ✓ Commitment to professional ethics and continuous learning.  
D) ✗ Exclusive focus on technical skills without management. (Management and leadership are also required.)  

**Correct:** A, B, C


#### 3. What is the primary responsibility of an engineer in society?  
A) ✓ Ensuring the reliability and safety of their designs.  
B) ✗ Maximizing profits for their employer. (Profit is secondary to public safety.)  
C) ✗ Innovating without regard to environmental impact. (Environmental impact must be considered.)  
D) ✓ Prioritizing public safety and welfare.  

**Correct:** A, D


#### 4. Which actions reflect an engineer’s social responsibility?  
A) ✓ Refusing to work on unethical projects.  
B) ✗ Ignoring whistleblowing to protect company interests. (Engineers must support whistleblowing.)  
C) ✓ Educating the public about technological risks.  
D) ✓ Designing sustainable and environmentally friendly technologies.  

**Correct:** A, C, D


#### 5. The “precautionary principle” in engineering design means:  
A) ✗ Proceeding with projects regardless of potential risks. (This is opposite to the principle.)  
B) ✓ Avoiding actions that could cause serious harm when risks are uncertain.  
C) ✓ Building redundancy and safety factors into designs.  
D) ✗ Prioritizing cost savings over safety concerns. (Safety is prioritized.)  

**Correct:** B, C
