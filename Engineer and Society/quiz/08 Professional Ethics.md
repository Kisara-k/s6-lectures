## 9. Professional Ethics

## Questions

#### 1. What best describes a defensible decision in professional ethics?  
A) A decision that everyone agrees is correct  
B) A decision that prioritizes personal benefit over ethical considerations  
C) A decision that is completely free of any negative consequences  
D) A decision based on sound reasoning that can be justified in any forum  

#### 2. Which of the following informal ethical tests help identify potentially unethical actions?  
A) Market Test  
B) Smell Test  
C) Shushers Test  
D) Utilitarian Test  

#### 3. According to Kant’s first formulation of the Categorical Imperative, which of the following actions would be considered unethical?  
A) Making promises with the intention of breaking them later  
B) Telling the truth even if it causes discomfort  
C) Breaking a promise with the intention of later fulfilling it  
D) Driving through a red light when no one is around  

#### 4. In the Kallman and Grillo four-step process, what is the primary purpose of Step 2?  
A) To list all stakeholders involved  
B) To isolate and clearly state the ethical dilemma  
C) To analyze the consequences of each alternative  
D) To plan the implementation of the decision  

#### 5. Which of the following statements about consequentialist ethical theories is true?  
A) Consequentialist theories are rarely used in ethical decision making  
B) Utilitarianism seeks to maximize happiness for the greatest number  
C) Egoism prioritizes the welfare of others over oneself  
D) They focus on the inherent morality of actions regardless of outcomes  

#### 6. When applying the “Other Person’s Shoe Test,” what is the main ethical consideration?  
A) Whether the action aligns with formal ethical theories  
B) Whether the action can be hidden from others  
C) Whether the action would be acceptable if you were in the other person’s position  
D) Whether the action benefits the decision-maker  

#### 7. Which of the following best reflects the second formulation of Kant’s Categorical Imperative?  
A) Follow your moral intuition in every situation  
B) Treat people as ends in themselves, not merely as means to an end  
C) Act only according to rules that can be universalized  
D) Maximize happiness for the greatest number of people  

#### 8. In the context of rights and duties, which of the following is correct?  
A) Ethical decisions should ignore conflicting rights if they complicate the dilemma  
B) Neglecting a duty does not affect anyone’s rights  
C) Every right corresponds to a duty that someone must fulfill  
D) Rights and duties are unrelated concepts  

#### 9. Which informal ethical test would most likely reveal an unethical action if you feel uncomfortable telling your family about it?  
A) Market Test  
B) Mom Test  
C) Moral Intuition Test  
D) TV Test  

#### 10. When making an ethical decision, why is it important to consider whether the action serves the common good?  
A) Because it ensures the decision benefits only the decision-maker  
B) Because it guarantees the decision will be legally acceptable  
C) Because it focuses solely on maximizing profits  
D) Because it helps balance individual rights with societal welfare

## Answers

#### 1. What best describes a defensible decision in professional ethics?  
A) ✗ Not everyone may agree on a defensible decision; consensus is not required.  
B) ✗ Prioritizing personal benefit over ethics is not defensible.  
C) ✗ A defensible decision may have some negative consequences; it is not necessarily free of them.  
D) ✓ A defensible decision is based on sound reasoning and can be justified in any forum.  

**Correct:** D


#### 2. Which of the following informal ethical tests help identify potentially unethical actions?  
A) ✓ The Market Test considers if the action harms your personal brand, signaling ethical concerns.  
B) ✓ The Smell Test detects if a situation “smells bad,” suggesting unethical aspects.  
C) ✓ The Shushers Test checks if you want to hide the action, indicating possible unethical behavior.  
D) ✗ The Utilitarian Test is a formal ethical theory, not an informal test.  

**Correct:** A, B, C


#### 3. According to Kant’s first formulation of the Categorical Imperative, which of the following actions would be considered unethical?  
A) ✓ Making promises with the intention of breaking them later cannot be universalized without contradiction.  
B) ✗ Telling the truth aligns with universal moral laws and is ethical.  
C) ✗ Breaking a promise with the intention of later fulfilling it does not violate the universalizability principle.  
D) ✓ Driving through a red light, even if no one is around, cannot be universalized without causing chaos.  

**Correct:** A, D


#### 4. In the Kallman and Grillo four-step process, what is the primary purpose of Step 2?  
A) ✗ Listing stakeholders is part of Step 1.  
B) ✓ Step 2 isolates and clearly states the ethical dilemma to be solved.  
C) ✗ Analyzing consequences is Step 3.  
D) ✗ Planning implementation is Step 4.  

**Correct:** B


#### 5. Which of the following statements about consequentialist ethical theories is true?  
A) ✗ Consequentialist theories, especially utilitarianism, are widely used in ethical decision making.  
B) ✓ Utilitarianism aims to maximize happiness for the greatest number.  
C) ✗ Egoism prioritizes self-interest, not others’ welfare.  
D) ✗ Consequentialist theories focus on outcomes, not inherent morality of actions.  

**Correct:** B


#### 6. When applying the “Other Person’s Shoe Test,” what is the main ethical consideration?  
A) ✗ The test is about perspective-taking, not formal ethical theories.  
B) ✗ Wanting to hide an action relates to the Shushers Test, not this one.  
C) ✓ It asks if the action would be acceptable if you were in the other person’s position.  
D) ✗ The test focuses on empathy, not personal benefit.  

**Correct:** C


#### 7. Which of the following best reflects the second formulation of Kant’s Categorical Imperative?  
A) ✗ Moral intuition is informal and not part of Kant’s formal theory.  
B) ✓ The second formulation requires treating people as ends in themselves, not merely as means.  
C) ✗ This is the first formulation, not the second.  
D) ✗ Maximizing happiness is utilitarianism, not Kant’s imperative.  

**Correct:** B


#### 8. In the context of rights and duties, which of the following is correct?  
A) ✗ Ethical decisions must consider conflicting rights carefully, not ignore them.  
B) ✗ Neglecting a duty can violate someone’s rights.  
C) ✓ Every right corresponds to a duty someone must fulfill.  
D) ✗ Rights and duties are closely related concepts.  

**Correct:** C


#### 9. Which informal ethical test would most likely reveal an unethical action if you feel uncomfortable telling your family about it?  
A) ✗ The Market Test relates to personal brand, not family.  
B) ✓ The Mom Test asks if you would be comfortable telling your mother.  
C) ✗ The Moral Intuition Test is about gut feeling, not specifically family.  
D) ✗ The TV Test focuses on public exposure, not family.  

**Correct:** B


#### 10. When making an ethical decision, why is it important to consider whether the action serves the common good?  
A) ✗ Serving the common good is about benefiting society, not just the decision-maker.  
B) ✗ Legal acceptability is separate from ethical considerations of the common good.  
C) ✗ Maximizing profits alone does not guarantee serving the common good.  
D) ✓ It balances individual rights with societal welfare, ensuring fairness and benefit for all.  

**Correct:** D
