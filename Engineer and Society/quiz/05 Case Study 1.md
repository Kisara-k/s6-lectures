## 6. Case Study 1

## Questions

#### 1. What are the primary reasons for studying engineering ethics through case studies?  
A) To memorize codes of ethics without application  
B) To understand how to relate codes of ethics to decision-making  
C) To develop judgment and analytical skills in practical situations  
D) To experience real-world ethical dilemmas engineers face  

#### 2. Which of the following principles are emphasized in both the IESL and NSPE Codes of Ethics?  
A) Engineers must perform services only within their area of competence  
B) Engineers should always prioritize client confidentiality over public safety  
C) Engineers should avoid continuous professional development  
D) Public safety and welfare are paramount  

#### 3. In the fire investigation case, what ethical conflict does Engineer-A face?  
A) Balancing confidentiality obligations with the duty to protect public safety  
B) Choosing between loyalty to the employer and reporting safety violations  
C) Deciding whether to falsify the forensic report to protect the client  
D) Determining if the settlement agreement can be ignored due to urgency  

#### 4. According to the codes of ethics, when is it acceptable for an engineer to disclose confidential information without client consent?  
A) When the engineer’s judgment about safety is overruled and public welfare is at risk  
B) When the engineer believes the information could harm public health or safety  
C) When the engineer wants to publish research findings regardless of confidentiality  
D) Only when the client explicitly permits disclosure  

#### 5. Which of the following statements about the IESL Code of Ethics is true?  
A) Engineers should build their reputation on merit and fair competition  
B) Engineers are encouraged to work outside their area of competence if needed  
C) Sustainable engineering practices are optional and not emphasized  
D) Engineers must speak out objectively and truthfully in professional matters  

#### 6. In the case study, why is Engineer-A ethically obligated to maintain confidentiality despite concerns about public safety?  
A) Because engineers must always prioritize client interests over public welfare  
B) Because disclosing the report would violate professional dignity  
C) Because the confidentiality is part of a court-approved settlement agreement  
D) Because the safety issues do not pose an immediate or urgent threat  

#### 7. What is a reasonable ethical approach for Engineer-A to address her safety concerns without breaching confidentiality?  
A) Publicly disclose the entire forensic report to warn the public  
B) Conduct further research and discuss technical issues without revealing specific details  
C) Ignore the safety concerns to avoid legal consequences  
D) Attempt to renegotiate the settlement agreement to allow disclosure  

#### 8. Which of the following best describes the role of “sensitive standards, judgment, and willpower” in engineering ethics?  
A) They are optional traits that do not affect ethical decision-making  
B) They replace the need to follow formal codes of ethics  
C) They are personal qualities that influence how engineers resolve ethical dilemmas  
D) They only apply when engineers face legal issues, not ethical ones  

#### 9. How do the NSPE and IESL codes differ in their treatment of engineers’ obligations to clients and the public?  
A) NSPE explicitly requires avoiding deceptive acts, while IESL focuses more on sustainable engineering  
B) NSPE does not require engineers to hold public safety as paramount  
C) IESL allows engineers to prioritize client interests over public safety  
D) Both codes require engineers to act as faithful agents without conflicts of interest  

#### 10. Which of the following actions would violate the ethical principles outlined in the case study?  
A) Engineer-A disclosing confidential information without permission despite no imminent danger  
B) Engineer-A refusing to conduct the investigation due to ethical concerns  
C) Engineer-A reporting safety violations to authorities after being overruled by the client  
D) Engineer-A maintaining confidentiality but seeking alternative ways to raise awareness

## Answers

#### 1. What are the primary reasons for studying engineering ethics through case studies?  
A) ✗ Memorizing codes without application does not help practical understanding  
B) ✓ To understand how to relate codes of ethics to decision-making  
C) ✓ To develop judgment and analytical skills in practical situations  
D) ✓ To experience real-world ethical dilemmas engineers face  

**Correct:** B, C, D


#### 2. Which of the following principles are emphasized in both the IESL and NSPE Codes of Ethics?  
A) ✓ Both codes require engineers to work only within their competence  
B) ✗ Confidentiality is important but does not override public safety  
C) ✗ Continuous professional development is required, not avoided  
D) ✓ Public safety and welfare are paramount in both codes  

**Correct:** A, D


#### 3. In the fire investigation case, what ethical conflict does Engineer-A face?  
A) ✓ The main conflict is between confidentiality and public safety  
B) ✓ Loyalty to employer vs. reporting safety issues is part of the conflict  
C) ✗ Falsifying reports is unethical and not part of the dilemma  
D) ✗ The case does not suggest ignoring the settlement due to urgency  

**Correct:** A, B


#### 4. According to the codes of ethics, when is it acceptable for an engineer to disclose confidential information without client consent?  
A) ✓ If safety concerns are ignored and public welfare endangered, disclosure is required  
B) ✓ When public health or safety is at risk, disclosure is allowed  
C) ✗ Publishing research without permission violates confidentiality  
D) ✗ Disclosure only with client permission is standard unless public safety is involved  

**Correct:** A, B


#### 5. Which of the following statements about the IESL Code of Ethics is true?  
A) ✓ Building reputation on merit and fair competition is emphasized  
B) ✗ Working outside competence is prohibited  
C) ✗ Sustainable engineering is a key principle, not optional  
D) ✓ Speaking objectively and truthfully is required  

**Correct:** A, D


#### 6. In the case study, why is Engineer-A ethically obligated to maintain confidentiality despite concerns about public safety?  
A) ✗ Public welfare takes precedence over client interests if urgent  
B) ✗ Professional dignity does not justify breaking confidentiality  
C) ✓ The confidentiality is legally binding due to a court-approved settlement  
D) ✓ The safety issues are not urgent or immediate threats  

**Correct:** C, D


#### 7. What is a reasonable ethical approach for Engineer-A to address her safety concerns without breaching confidentiality?  
A) ✗ Publicly disclosing the full report violates confidentiality and legal agreements  
B) ✓ Conducting further research and discussing general issues without specifics is appropriate  
C) ✗ Ignoring safety concerns is unethical  
D) ✓ Attempting to renegotiate the settlement is a possible but not guaranteed option  

**Correct:** B, D


#### 8. Which of the following best describes the role of “sensitive standards, judgment, and willpower” in engineering ethics?  
A) ✗ They are essential, not optional, for ethical decision-making  
B) ✗ They do not replace formal codes but complement them  
C) ✓ These personal qualities influence how engineers resolve ethical dilemmas  
D) ✗ They apply to all ethical decisions, not just legal issues  

**Correct:** C


#### 9. How do the NSPE and IESL codes differ in their treatment of engineers’ obligations to clients and the public?  
A) ✓ NSPE explicitly requires avoiding deceptive acts; IESL emphasizes sustainability more  
B) ✗ Both codes hold public safety as paramount  
C) ✗ IESL does not allow prioritizing client interests over public safety  
D) ✓ Both require acting as faithful agents without conflicts of interest  

**Correct:** A, D


#### 10. Which of the following actions would violate the ethical principles outlined in the case study?  
A) ✓ Disclosing confidential information without permission when no imminent danger exists violates ethics  
B) ✗ Refusing investigation may be ethical if conflicts arise but is not a violation here  
C) ✗ Reporting violations after being overruled is encouraged if public safety is endangered  
D) ✗ Maintaining confidentiality while seeking alternative awareness respects ethics  

**Correct:** A
