## 9. Professional Ethics

## Questions

#### 1. What is the primary goal when making ethical decisions in situations where no clearly correct decision exists?  
A) To always follow pre-canned guidelines without exception  
B) To make a decision that benefits the majority regardless of consequences  
C) To make a defensible decision based on sound reasoning that can be justified in any forum  
D) To avoid making any decision to prevent ethical dilemmas  

#### 2. Which of the following informal tests help identify potentially unethical actions by appealing to intuition or social norms?  
A) Market Test  
B) Moral Intuition Test  
C) Shushers Test  
D) Utilitarian Test  

#### 3. According to Kant’s first formulation of the Categorical Imperative, which of the following proposed rules would be considered flawed?  
A) “I may make promises with the intention of later breaking them.”  
B) “I may drive through a red light if it does not endanger anyone.”  
C) “I should always tell the truth regardless of consequences.”  
D) Both A and B  

#### 4. In the Four Step Process for ethical decision making, what is the correct sequence of steps?  
A) Isolate dilemma, list facts, analyze alternatives, make decision and plan implementation  
B) Analyze alternatives, list facts, isolate dilemma, make decision and plan implementation  
C) Make decision, list facts, isolate dilemma, analyze alternatives  
D) List facts, isolate dilemma, analyze alternatives, make decision and plan implementation  

#### 5. When applying the Rights and Duties approach in ethical analysis, which of the following is essential?  
A) Focusing only on the rights of the decision maker  
B) Identifying which rights may be abridged and corresponding neglected duties  
C) Prioritizing duties over rights in all cases  
D) Ignoring stakeholder interests to avoid bias  

#### 6. Which of the following best describes the “Market Test” as an informal ethical guideline?  
A) Evaluating if the action enhances or damages one’s personal brand or reputation  
B) Assessing whether an action would be acceptable if broadcast on television  
C) Determining if the action smells bad or suspicious  
D) Considering if the action treats others as ends rather than means  

#### 7. How does the “Other Person’s Shoe Test” function in ethical decision making?  
A) By applying universal moral laws to the situation  
B) By imagining how one would feel if placed in the other person’s position  
C) By testing if the action can be justified legally  
D) By assessing the financial impact on stakeholders  

#### 8. Which of the following statements about Consequentialist (Teleological) ethical theories is true?  
A) Utilitarianism aims to maximize happiness for the greatest number  
B) Consequentialist theories are rarely used in ethical decision making  
C) They focus primarily on the inherent morality of actions regardless of outcomes  
D) Egoism prioritizes what is best for others over oneself  

#### 9. In Kant’s second formulation of the Categorical Imperative, what is emphasized?  
A) Following societal norms regardless of individual rights  
B) Treating oneself and others always as ends in themselves, never merely as means  
C) Maximizing overall happiness and minimizing harm  
D) Acting only according to rules that can be universalized  

#### 10. According to the Santa Clara University ethical framework, which of the following analyses is NOT part of thinking ethically?  
A) Promoting the greatest good or human welfare  
B) Prioritizing the interests of the most powerful stakeholders over others  
C) Identifying legitimate rights and duties of affected parties  
D) Ensuring all parties are treated fairly, with justified differences if any

## Answers

#### 1. What is the primary goal when making ethical decisions in situations where no clearly correct decision exists?  
A) ✗ Blindly following guidelines is not always possible or desirable in dilemmas.  
B) ✗ Benefits majority regardless of consequences is a consequentialist approach but not the primary goal here.  
C) ✓ Defensible decisions are emphasized as the objective when no correct decision exists.  
D) ✗ Avoiding decisions does not resolve ethical dilemmas.  

**Correct:** C


#### 2. Which of the following informal tests help identify potentially unethical actions by appealing to intuition or social norms?  
A) ✓ Market Test evaluates impact on personal brand, linked to social norms.  
B) ✓ Moral Intuition Test relies on first impressions.  
C) ✓ Shushers Test checks if one wants to hide the action, indicating unethical behavior.  
D) ✗ Utilitarian Test is a formal ethical theory, not an informal test.  

**Correct:** A, B, C


#### 3. According to Kant’s first formulation of the Categorical Imperative, which of the following proposed rules would be considered flawed?  
A) ✓ Making promises with intent to break them fails universalizability test.  
B) ✓ Breaking red light rules universally leads to chaos, so flawed.  
C) ✗ Always telling the truth aligns with universal moral law, so not flawed.  
D) ✗ This is a combination, but only A and B are flawed, so D is not a standalone choice.  

**Correct:** A, B


#### 4. In the Four Step Process for ethical decision making, what is the correct sequence of steps?  
A) ✗ Isolating dilemma before listing facts is illogical.  
B) ✗ Analyzing alternatives before isolating dilemma is incorrect.  
C) ✗ Making decision before analysis is premature.  
D) ✓ Correct order: list facts, isolate dilemma, analyze alternatives, make decision and plan implementation.  

**Correct:** D


#### 5. When applying the Rights and Duties approach in ethical analysis, which of the following is essential?  
A) ✗ Focusing only on decision maker’s rights ignores other stakeholders.  
B) ✓ Identifying abridged rights and neglected duties is core to this approach.  
C) ✗ Prioritizing duties over rights in all cases is not always justified.  
D) ✗ Ignoring stakeholder interests contradicts ethical analysis.  

**Correct:** B


#### 6. Which of the following best describes the “Market Test” as an informal ethical guideline?  
A) ✓ Market Test evaluates if action enhances personal brand or reputation.  
B) ✗ TV Test, not Market Test, involves broadcasting on TV.  
C) ✗ Smell Test checks if situation “smells bad,” unrelated to Market Test.  
D) ✗ Treating others as ends is Kant’s imperative, not Market Test.  

**Correct:** A


#### 7. How does the “Other Person’s Shoe Test” function in ethical decision making?  
A) ✗ Universal moral laws relate to Kant’s imperative, not this test.  
B) ✓ It involves imagining oneself in another’s position to assess fairness.  
C) ✗ Legal justification is separate from this empathy-based test.  
D) ✗ Financial impact is not the focus of this test.  

**Correct:** B


#### 8. Which of the following statements about Consequentialist (Teleological) ethical theories is true?  
A) ✓ Utilitarianism aims to maximize happiness for the greatest number.  
B) ✗ Consequentialist theories are widely used, especially utilitarianism.  
C) ✗ Consequentialism focuses on outcomes, not inherent morality.  
D) ✗ Egoism prioritizes self-interest, not others.  

**Correct:** A


#### 9. In Kant’s second formulation of the Categorical Imperative, what is emphasized?  
A) ✗ Following societal norms regardless of rights contradicts Kant’s ethics.  
B) ✓ Treating oneself and others as ends, never merely as means, is the second formulation.  
C) ✗ Maximizing happiness is utilitarian, not Kantian.  
D) ✗ This is the first formulation, not the second.  

**Correct:** B


#### 10. According to the Santa Clara University ethical framework, which of the following analyses is NOT part of thinking ethically?  
A) ✗ Promoting greatest good is part of the framework.  
B) ✓ Prioritizing powerful stakeholders over others contradicts fairness and rights principles.  
C) ✗ Identifying rights and duties is included.  
D) ✗ Ensuring fair treatment is included.  

**Correct:** B
