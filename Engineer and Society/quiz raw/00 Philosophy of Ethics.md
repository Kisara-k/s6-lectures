## 1. Philosophy of Ethics

## Questions

#### 1. Which of the following statements accurately describe the relationship between values, morals, ethics, and law?  
A) Values are stable beliefs that guide personal choices, while morals govern societal attitudes toward right and wrong.  
B) Ethics is the systematic study of human actions to determine rightfulness or wrongfulness, often based on morals.  
C) Morality is concerned with personal principles, whereas ethics refers to external rules influenced by organizations or groups.  
D) Laws are always identical to ethical principles and never conflict with morals.  

#### 2. Which of the following best illustrate the difference between ethics and morals?  
A) Ethics and morals always align perfectly in every situation.  
B) Ethics are practical rules created by professional bodies, while morals are personal beliefs shaped by culture and religion.  
C) A lawyer revealing client guilt to the court is ethical but may be immoral.  
D) Omerta, the Mafia code of silence, is ethical within the group but morally questionable in society.  

#### 3. Regarding the origins and meanings of ethics and morals, which statements are correct?  
A) Morals rarely change and are influenced by culture, religion, and family.  
B) Morals come from the Latin word 'moralitas', meaning manner or proper behavior.  
C) The term ethics derives from the Greek word 'Ethos', meaning character or guiding beliefs.  
D) Ethics primarily focus on internal personal beliefs, while morals are external societal rules.  

#### 4. Which of the following are true about Eastern and Western ethics?  
A) Western ethics emphasize holistic and cultural approaches, while Eastern ethics prioritize rational thought and logic.  
B) Eastern ethics are imposed externally by society, whereas Western ethics arise from within the individual.  
C) Eastern ethics are rooted in religions like Hinduism and Buddhism; Western ethics have roots in Roman and Christian traditions.  
D) Western ethics emphasize self-direction and personal understanding, while Eastern ethics focus on universal interconnections and liberation from the self.  

#### 5. What are the key differences between personal ethics and professional ethics?  
A) Professional ethics always override personal ethics without exception.  
B) Conflicts can arise when personal beliefs contradict professional codes of conduct.  
C) Professional ethics are less important than personal ethics in decision-making.  
D) Personal ethics relate to everyday life decisions, while professional ethics govern behavior in business and professional contexts.  

#### 6. Why is the study of professional ethics particularly important for engineers?  
A) Engineers’ decisions can impact public safety and welfare, making ethical conduct critical.  
B) Ethical lapses by engineers only result in financial loss, not harm to people.  
C) Understanding professional ethics improves ethical sensitivity, judgment, and willpower.  
D) Engineers are not expected to follow ethical standards if it conflicts with business interests.  

#### 7. Which of the following examples illustrate ethical dilemmas faced by engineers?  
A) Balancing job security against unethical business practices.  
B) Choosing to follow personal beliefs over the professional code of conduct without consequences.  
C) Shipping a new product before quality assurance is complete to meet a deadline.  
D) A project manager requesting a kick-back during a slow business period.  

#### 8. Which statements about the nature of ethics are correct?  
A) Ethics are fixed and universal, never varying between cultures or organizations.  
B) Ethics are only concerned with legal compliance, not with right or wrong conduct.  
C) Ethics can vary depending on the social environment, organization, or group.  
D) Ethics promote human welfare and aim to achieve “the good.”  

#### 9. How do intrinsic and extrinsic values differ?  
A) Extrinsic values are more important than intrinsic values in ethical decision-making.  
B) Intrinsic values are unstable and change frequently over time.  
C) Extrinsic values are means to achieve intrinsic values, such as wealth or teamwork.  
D) Intrinsic values are final destinations like truthfulness and courage, valued for their own sake.  

#### 10. Which of the following statements best describe the complexity in distinguishing ethics from morality?  
A) A community’s ethical standards may differ from an individual’s moral beliefs, leading to contradictions.  
B) The terms ethics and morality are always interchangeable without any distinction.  
C) Ethics and morality can sometimes conflict, such as when a moral action violates professional ethical codes.  
D) Morality is always personal, while ethics are always community-based and external.

## Answers

#### 1. Which of the following statements accurately describe the relationship between values, morals, ethics, and law?  
A) ✓ Values are stable beliefs guiding personal choices; morals govern societal attitudes toward right and wrong.  
B) ✓ Ethics is the systematic study of human actions to determine rightfulness or wrongfulness, often based on morals.  
C) ✓ Morality concerns personal principles; ethics refers to external rules influenced by organizations or groups.  
D) ✗ Laws can conflict with morals; they are not always identical to ethical principles.  

**Correct:** A, B, C


#### 2. Which of the following best illustrate the difference between ethics and morals?  
A) ✗ Ethics and morals do not always align perfectly.  
B) ✓ Ethics are practical rules by professional bodies; morals are personal beliefs shaped by culture and religion.  
C) ✗ A lawyer revealing client guilt is moral but unethical (not ethical but immoral).  
D) ✓ Omerta is ethical within the Mafia but morally questionable in society.  

**Correct:** B, D


#### 3. Regarding the origins and meanings of ethics and morals, which statements are correct?  
A) ✓ Morals rarely change and are influenced by culture, religion, and family.  
B) ✓ Morals come from Latin 'moralitas', meaning manner or proper behavior.  
C) ✓ Ethics derives from Greek 'Ethos', meaning character or guiding beliefs.  
D) ✗ Ethics focus on external rules; morals are internal personal beliefs (reverse of statement).  

**Correct:** A, B, C


#### 4. Which of the following are true about Eastern and Western ethics?  
A) ✗ Western ethics prioritize rational thought; Eastern ethics are holistic and cultural (reverse of statement).  
B) ✗ Eastern ethics are imposed from within oneself, not externally; Western ethics are more individualistic.  
C) ✓ Eastern ethics rooted in Hinduism/Buddhism; Western ethics in Roman/Christian traditions.  
D) ✓ Western ethics emphasize self-direction; Eastern ethics focus on universal interconnections and liberation from self.  

**Correct:** C, D


#### 5. What are the key differences between personal ethics and professional ethics?  
A) ✗ Professional ethics do not always override personal ethics without exception; conflicts can occur.  
B) ✓ Conflicts can arise when personal beliefs contradict professional codes.  
C) ✗ Professional ethics are equally or more important in professional decision-making.  
D) ✓ Personal ethics relate to everyday life; professional ethics govern professional behavior.  

**Correct:** B, D


#### 6. Why is the study of professional ethics particularly important for engineers?  
A) ✓ Engineers’ decisions impact public safety and welfare, making ethics critical.  
B) ✗ Ethical lapses can cost lives, not just money.  
C) ✓ Studying ethics improves sensitivity, judgment, and willpower.  
D) ✗ Engineers are expected to follow ethical standards even if conflicting with business interests.  

**Correct:** A, C


#### 7. Which of the following examples illustrate ethical dilemmas faced by engineers?  
A) ✓ Balancing job security against unethical practices is an ethical dilemma.  
B) ✗ Ignoring professional codes without consequences is unrealistic and not an ethical dilemma example.  
C) ✓ Shipping product before QA completion to meet deadline is an ethical dilemma.  
D) ✓ Kick-back request during slow business is an ethical dilemma.  

**Correct:** A, C, D


#### 8. Which statements about the nature of ethics are correct?  
A) ✗ Ethics vary between cultures and organizations; they are not fixed universally.  
B) ✗ Ethics concern right and wrong conduct beyond just legal compliance.  
C) ✓ Ethics can vary depending on social environment or group.  
D) ✓ Ethics promote human welfare and aim for “the good.”  

**Correct:** C, D


#### 9. How do intrinsic and extrinsic values differ?  
A) ✗ Extrinsic values are not more important than intrinsic values in ethical decision-making.  
B) ✗ Intrinsic values are stable, not unstable or frequently changing.  
C) ✓ Extrinsic values are means to achieve intrinsic values (e.g., wealth).  
D) ✓ Intrinsic values are final destinations valued for their own sake (e.g., truthfulness).  

**Correct:** C, D


#### 10. Which of the following statements best describe the complexity in distinguishing ethics from morality?  
A) ✓ Community ethics may differ from individual morality, causing contradictions.  
B) ✗ Ethics and morality are not always interchangeable; distinctions exist.  
C) ✓ Ethics and morality can conflict, e.g., moral action violating professional ethics.  
D) ✗ Morality can be personal or community-based; ethics can also be personal or social standards.  

**Correct:** A, C
