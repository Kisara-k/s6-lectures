## Answers

#### 1. Which of the following are considered key resources that an engineer-manager is responsible for managing?  
A) ✓ Money is a critical resource that must be managed effectively.  
B) ✓ Materials are essential resources in engineering projects.  
C) ✓ Humans are a primary resource managed by engineers in managerial roles.  
D) ✓ Time is a vital resource that must be managed for project success.  

**Correct:** A, B, C, D


#### 2. In the context of Sri Lankan fully government organizations, which documents primarily govern administrative and financial procedures?  
A) ✓ Establishment Code (also called Administrative Regulations) governs administration.  
B) ✓ Financial Regulations govern financial procedures.  
C) ✗ Shop and Office Employees Act is a labor law, not an administrative or financial regulation.  
D) ✓ Public Administration Circulars provide guidelines and instructions.  

**Correct:** A, B, D


#### 3. Which of the following labor laws specifically address employee benefits and compensation in Sri Lanka?  
A) ✓ Payment of Gratuity Act covers gratuity payments (compensation).  
B) ✓ EPF Act deals with employee provident fund contributions (benefits).  
C) ✗ Industrial Disputes Act deals with dispute resolution, not benefits.  
D) ✓ Maternity Benefits Ordinance covers maternity-related employee benefits.  

**Correct:** A, B, D


#### 4. What are the typical types of employees an engineer-manager should understand when handling human resource management?  
A) ✓ Fixed-Term/Contract employees are common in project-based work.  
B) ✓ Permanent employees are a standard employment type.  
C) ✗ Freelancers are not explicitly mentioned in the lecture content as a typical employee type.  
D) ✓ Casual employees work irregular or temporary hours.  

**Correct:** A, B, D


#### 5. Which of the following are essential knowledge areas for project management according to PMI?  
A) ✗ Financial Management is important but not listed as a PMI knowledge area here.  
B) ✓ Scope Management is a core PMI knowledge area.  
C) ✓ Risk Management is a key PMI knowledge area.  
D) ✓ Stakeholder Management is included in PMI knowledge areas.  

**Correct:** B, C, D


#### 6. Regarding procurement processes, which steps are typically involved in awarding a contract?  
A) ✓ Preparation of specifications is the first step in procurement.  
B) ✓ Award of contract is the final step in procurement.  
C) ✗ Preparation of financial reports is not part of procurement steps.  
D) ✓ Evaluation of tenders is necessary before awarding.  

**Correct:** A, B, D


#### 7. Which statements about recruitment and remuneration in fully government organizations are correct?  
A) ✗ Recruitment is not managed by the Ministry of Treasury.  
B) ✓ Recruitment is guided by the Public Service Commission under the Ministry of Public Administration.  
C) ✗ Salaries and allowances are not decided independently by organizations but by the Ministry of Public Administration.  
D) ✓ Remuneration is decided by the Ministry of Public Administration.  

**Correct:** B, D


#### 8. What are the main functions or roles an engineer-manager should be familiar with?  
A) ✓ Effective communication is a key managerial skill.  
B) ✓ Knowledge of government compliances is necessary for management.  
C) ✓ Understanding rules, regulations, and processes is essential.  
D) ✗ Designing engineering systems is a technical role, not a management function.  

**Correct:** A, B, C


#### 9. Which of the following are types of organizations and their management authorities?  
A) ✓ Fully Government organizations are managed by the Ministry of Public Administration.  
B) ✗ Semi Government organizations are not managed by the Ministry of Public Administration.  
C) ✓ Private organizations are managed by a Board of Directors.  
D) ✓ Semi Government organizations are managed by the Ministry of Treasury (Public Enterprise Department).  

**Correct:** A, C, D


#### 10. In financial management, which of the following correctly describe expenditure types and budgeting concepts?  
A) ✗ Budgeting is very relevant for project management.  
B) ✓ Opex refers to Operational Expenditure.  
C) ✓ Capex refers to Capital Expenditure.  
D) ✓ Financial authority levels determine who can approve expenditures.  

**Correct:** B, C, D
