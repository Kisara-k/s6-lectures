## Answers

#### 1. Which of the following characteristics define a professional?  
A) ✓ Views other members of the profession as peers or colleagues – Professionals recognize others in their field as peers.  
B) ✓ Requires specialized education and training in a specific domain – This is a core aspect of being a professional.  
C) ✗ Works independently without any ethical obligations – Professionals have ethical obligations; independence alone does not define professionalism.  
D) ✓ Exercises judgment and follows relevant professional standards – Professionals apply judgment and adhere to standards.  

**Correct:** A, B, D


#### 2. Which of the following are considered core professional values?  
A) ✓ Integrity – Fundamental to trustworthiness in the workplace.  
B) ✗ Profit maximization – This is a business goal, not a professional value.  
C) ✓ Accountability – A key professional value.  
D) ✓ Responsibility – Essential for professional conduct.  

**Correct:** A, C, D


#### 3. Professional Ethics primarily:  
A) ✗ Apply only to engineers and not to other professions – Professional ethics apply across many professions, not just engineering.  
B) ✓ Are often codified as a set of rules or a code of conduct – Professional ethics are typically formalized.  
C) ✓ Govern behavior towards people and institutions in a business environment – Ethics guide interactions in professional settings.  
D) ✗ Are informal guidelines that vary widely between individuals – Ethics are usually codified and consistent within a profession.  

**Correct:** B, C


#### 4. Engineering Ethics sets standards for engineers’ obligations to which of the following groups?  
A) ✓ Clients – Ethical obligations include serving clients faithfully.  
B) ✗ Competitors – While fair competition is encouraged, direct obligations to competitors are not a primary focus.  
C) ✓ Public – Engineers have a duty to protect public welfare.  
D) ✓ Employers – Engineers must act responsibly towards employers.  

**Correct:** A, C, D


#### 5. Which of the following best distinguishes between skill and competence?  
A) ✓ Skill is easily measurable and specific to a task, competence is broader and based on behavior – Correct distinction as per the lecture.  
B) ✗ Competence can be developed quickly, skill takes years of experience – Competence takes longer to develop; skills can be acquired faster.  
C) ✗ Skill is behavior-based, competence is task-specific – This reverses the definitions.  
D) ✗ Competence is unrelated to attitude or knowledge – Competence includes attitude, knowledge, and other factors.  

**Correct:** A


#### 6. According to the International Engineering Alliance (IEA), which of the following statements are true?  
A) ✗ The IEA only covers engineers, not technologists or technicians – It covers engineers, technologists, and technicians.  
B) ✓ The IEA governs recognition of engineering educational qualifications internationally – This is a primary role of the IEA.  
C) ✗ The IEA is a for-profit organization – It is a not-for-profit organization.  
D) ✓ Members of the IEA establish standards for both education and professional competence – Both are covered by the IEA agreements.  

**Correct:** B, D


#### 7. Match the engineering roles with their knowledge profile and responsible areas:  
A) ✓ Engineer – Solution of complex engineering problems – Correct definition for engineers.  
B) ✗ Engineering Technician – Broadly defined engineering problems – Technicians handle well-defined problems, not broadly defined.  
C) ✗ Engineer – Well defined practical procedures and practices – This describes a technician, not an engineer.  
D) ✗ Engineering Technologist – Solution of complex engineering problems – Complex problems are for engineers, technologists handle broadly defined problems.  

**Correct:** A


#### 8. Which of the following accords correspond correctly to the educational qualifications?  
A) ✓ Sydney Accord – 3-year degree for Technologists – Correct.  
B) ✓ Washington Accord – 4-year degree for Engineers – Correct.  
C) ✓ Dublin Accord – 2-year degree for Technicians – Correct.  
D) ✗ Washington Accord – 2-year degree for Technicians – Incorrect, Washington Accord is for engineers with 4-year degrees.  

**Correct:** A, B, C


#### 9. Which competency standards must professional engineers demonstrate according to the Asia Pacific Economic Corporation?  
A) ✓ Communication and interpersonal skills – Included in the standards.  
B) ✓ Leadership and management – Required competency.  
C) ✗ Marketing and sales skills – Not listed as a required competency.  
D) ✓ Practical application of engineering knowledge – Essential competency.  

**Correct:** A, B, D


#### 10. Which of the following are basic codes in engineering ethics?  
A) ✗ Build reputation through aggressive competition – Ethics require fair competition, not aggressive or unfair.  
B) ✓ Speak out objectively and truthfully in professional matters – Essential ethical obligation.  
C) ✓ Public goodness is paramount, including health and safety of the public – Core ethical principle.  
D) ✓ Practice sustainable engineering to minimize environmental impact – A fundamental ethical code.  

**Correct:** B, C, D
