## 5. Professional and Regulatory Bodies

## Questions

#### 1. What are the primary reasons for having professional bodies in engineering?  
A) To regulate and enforce ethical behavior among members  
B) To provide financial support and loans to engineers  
C) To accredit educational programs and ensure degree standards  
D) To offer continuing professional development (CPD) opportunities  

#### 2. Which of the following statements about becoming a Chartered Engineer through IESL are correct?  
A) Must be an Associate member with at least 5 years of responsible experience  
B) Must pass both a Management Paper and a Technical Paper  
C) Can become a Chartered Engineer immediately after graduation  
D) Required to submit an experience report and a project or design report  

#### 3. Which of the following are functions of a regulatory body in the engineering profession?  
A) Enforcing compliance with national standards  
B) Imposing requirements and conditions on professional practice  
C) Maintaining professional conduct and standards  
D) Organizing social events for engineers  

#### 4. What distinguishes a professional body from a regulatory body?  
A) Professional bodies have the authority to enforce legal penalties  
B) Regulatory bodies are established by legal mandate or government legislation  
C) Regulatory bodies primarily serve the public interest and maintain national standards  
D) Professional bodies are member-driven, non-commercial organizations  

#### 5. Which membership categories exist within the Institution of Engineers Sri Lanka (IESL)?  
A) Student Member  
B) Fellow  
C) Corporate Member (Chartered Engineer)  
D) Apprentice Member  

#### 6. Regarding the recognition of engineering degrees, which of the following are challenges that professional bodies address?  
A) Degrees awarded by foreign universities conducted in foreign countries  
B) Degrees obtained through online courses without accreditation  
C) Degrees conducted by local institutions but awarded by foreign universities  
D) Degrees awarded by local universities versus foreign universities  

#### 7. Which of the following are true about Continuing Professional Development (CPD) programs offered by professional bodies?  
A) They only focus on technical skills, excluding management or ethical training  
B) They include seminars, webinars, and professional discussions  
C) They help members update their knowledge in line with technological changes  
D) They are mandatory for all engineering graduates before employment  

#### 8. What are the responsibilities of the Engineering Council Sri Lanka (ECSL)?  
A) Registration of all categories of engineering practitioners  
B) Providing financial grants to engineering students  
C) Registration of foreign engineering practitioners  
D) Maintenance of professional standards and conduct  

#### 9. Which of the following statements about the career path and hierarchy of engineers in Sri Lanka are correct?  
A) Senior Engineer is typically achieved after 5 years of experience  
B) General Manager positions can be attained without being a Chartered Engineer  
C) A Chartered Engineer is required to reach Deputy General Manager level in most hierarchies  
D) The hierarchy includes positions such as Assistant Director and Director General  

#### 10. Which of the following institutions are recognized as professional bodies in Sri Lanka?  
A) Institute of Chartered Accountants of Sri Lanka  
B) Sri Lanka Institute of Marketing  
C) Engineering Council UK  
D) Institution of Engineers Sri Lanka (IESL)

## Answers

#### 1. What are the primary reasons for having professional bodies in engineering?  
A) ✓ They regulate and enforce ethical behavior among members.  
B) ✗ Providing financial support and loans is not a typical function of professional bodies.  
C) ✓ They accredit educational programs and ensure degree standards.  
D) ✓ They offer continuing professional development (CPD) opportunities.  

**Correct:** A, C, D


#### 2. Which of the following statements about becoming a Chartered Engineer through IESL are correct?  
A) ✓ Must be an Associate member with at least 5 years of responsible experience.  
B) ✓ Must pass both a Management Paper and a Technical Paper.  
C) ✗ Cannot become a Chartered Engineer immediately after graduation; experience and exams are required.  
D) ✓ Required to submit an experience report and a project or design report.  

**Correct:** A, B, D


#### 3. Which of the following are functions of a regulatory body in the engineering profession?  
A) ✓ Enforcing compliance with national standards is a core responsibility.  
B) ✓ Imposing requirements and conditions on professional practice is a key regulatory function.  
C) ✓ Maintaining professional conduct and standards is essential for regulatory bodies.  
D) ✗ Organizing social events is not a regulatory function.  

**Correct:** A, B, C


#### 4. What distinguishes a professional body from a regulatory body?  
A) ✗ Professional bodies do not have legal authority to enforce penalties; that is the role of regulatory bodies.  
B) ✓ Regulatory bodies are established by legal mandate or government legislation.  
C) ✓ Regulatory bodies primarily serve the public interest and maintain national standards.  
D) ✓ Professional bodies are member-driven, non-commercial organizations.  

**Correct:** B, C, D


#### 5. Which membership categories exist within the Institution of Engineers Sri Lanka (IESL)?  
A) ✓ Student Member is a recognized category.  
B) ✓ Fellow is a recognized senior membership category.  
C) ✓ Corporate Member (Chartered Engineer) is a recognized category.  
D) ✗ Apprentice Member is not listed as a category in IESL.  

**Correct:** A, B, C


#### 6. Regarding the recognition of engineering degrees, which of the following are challenges that professional bodies address?  
A) ✓ Degrees awarded by foreign universities conducted abroad also need recognition checks.  
B) ✗ Degrees from unaccredited online courses are not specifically mentioned but generally not recognized.  
C) ✓ Degrees awarded by foreign universities but conducted locally require scrutiny.  
D) ✓ Degrees awarded by local universities need standard verification.  

**Correct:** A, C, D


#### 7. Which of the following are true about Continuing Professional Development (CPD) programs offered by professional bodies?  
A) ✗ CPD covers technical, management, and ethical training, not just technical skills.  
B) ✓ CPD includes seminars, webinars, and professional discussions.  
C) ✓ CPD helps members update knowledge with technological changes.  
D) ✗ CPD is not mandatory before employment but is important for ongoing development.  

**Correct:** B, C


#### 8. What are the responsibilities of the Engineering Council Sri Lanka (ECSL)?  
A) ✓ Registration of all categories of engineering practitioners is a key responsibility.  
B) ✗ Providing financial grants to students is not a stated responsibility.  
C) ✓ Registration of foreign engineering practitioners is included.  
D) ✓ Maintenance of professional standards and conduct is a core function.  

**Correct:** A, C, D


#### 9. Which of the following statements about the career path and hierarchy of engineers in Sri Lanka are correct?  
A) ✓ Senior Engineer is typically achieved after 5 years of experience.  
B) ✗ General Manager positions usually require Chartered Engineer status or equivalent experience.  
C) ✓ Chartered Engineer status is required to reach Deputy General Manager level in most hierarchies.  
D) ✓ The hierarchy includes positions such as Assistant Director and Director General.  

**Correct:** A, C, D


#### 10. Which of the following institutions are recognized as professional bodies in Sri Lanka?  
A) ✓ Institute of Chartered Accountants of Sri Lanka is a professional body.  
B) ✓ Sri Lanka Institute of Marketing is a professional body.  
C) ✗ Engineering Council UK is an international regulatory body, not a Sri Lankan professional body.  
D) ✓ Institution of Engineers Sri Lanka (IESL) is a professional body.  

**Correct:** A, B, D
