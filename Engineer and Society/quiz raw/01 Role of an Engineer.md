## 2. Role of an Engineer

## Questions

#### 1. What distinguishes an engineer from a scientist in their professional roles?  
A) Scientists apply engineering principles to develop new devices.  
B) Engineers focus on creating applications to solve real-world problems.  
C) Scientists primarily develop theories to explain natural phenomena.  
D) Engineers innovate practical solutions based on scientific discoveries.  

#### 2. Which of the following are essential competencies required to become a Chartered Engineer?  
A) Communication and interpersonal skills  
B) Ability to conduct independent scientific research  
C) Knowledge and understanding of engineering principles  
D) Professional commitment and ethical conduct  

#### 3. What are the primary social responsibilities of engineers?  
A) Ensuring public safety and well-being in their work  
B) Refusing to participate in projects that are unethical or illegal  
C) Maximizing profits for their employers at any cost  
D) Educating the public about technological risks and consequences  

#### 4. Which statements correctly describe the role of an engineer in society?  
A) Engineers must prioritize public interest and safety over personal or corporate gain.  
B) Engineers should openly discuss all relevant facts related to their work outcomes.  
C) Engineers have no obligation to consider environmental sustainability in their designs.  
D) Engineers are accountable only for the technical success of their projects, not societal impact.  

#### 5. In the context of engineering ethics, what actions might an engineer take if they encounter wrongdoing in a project?  
A) Ignore the issue to maintain job security  
B) Blow the whistle on illegal or unethical practices  
C) Continue working without raising concerns if the project is profitable  
D) Speak out publicly against the project if it threatens public safety  

#### 6. Which of the following best describe the rules of conduct expected from professional engineers?  
A) Act with fairness and integrity towards colleagues and the public  
B) Prioritize personal gain over public health and safety  
C) Safeguard public interest in matters of health, safety, and the environment  
D) Uphold the dignity and reputation of the profession at all times  

#### 7. What roles can an engineer typically assume within a professional setting?  
A) Team leader and motivator  
B) Sole independent operator without collaboration  
C) Decision maker and manager  
D) Representative and planner  

#### 8. Which responsibilities fall under the categories of an engineer’s duties?  
A) Research and development  
B) Project implementation and operation  
C) Marketing and sales exclusively  
D) Network planning and design  

#### 9. When managing engineering projects, what key job functions must an engineer perform?  
A) Allocating resources and assigning targets to staff  
B) Organizing competency development programs for staff  
C) Planning and budgeting based on corporate goals  
D) Ignoring progress reviews to avoid conflict  

#### 10. Which legislative and regulatory frameworks are relevant to engineering practice?  
A) Environmental Regulations  
B) Engineering Council Act 04/2017  
C) International Trade Agreements  
D) Industrial Dispute Act

## Answers

#### 1. What distinguishes an engineer from a scientist in their professional roles?  
A) ✗ Scientists develop theories; engineers apply them, not the other way around.  
B) ✓ Engineers focus on creating applications to solve real-world problems.  
C) ✓ Scientists primarily develop theories to explain natural phenomena.  
D) ✓ Engineers innovate practical solutions based on scientific discoveries.  

**Correct:** B, C, D


#### 2. Which of the following are essential competencies required to become a Chartered Engineer?  
A) ✓ Communication and interpersonal skills are essential for professional practice.  
B) ✗ Independent scientific research is not a core competency for Chartered Engineers.  
C) ✓ Knowledge and understanding of engineering principles is fundamental.  
D) ✓ Professional commitment and ethical conduct are required.  

**Correct:** A, C, D


#### 3. What are the primary social responsibilities of engineers?  
A) ✓ Ensuring public safety and well-being is a key responsibility.  
B) ✓ Refusing to work on unethical or illegal projects is part of social responsibility.  
C) ✗ Maximizing profits at any cost contradicts ethical engineering practice.  
D) ✓ Educating the public about risks is a voluntary but important responsibility.  

**Correct:** A, B, D


#### 4. Which statements correctly describe the role of an engineer in society?  
A) ✓ Engineers must prioritize public interest and safety over other concerns.  
B) ✓ Engineers should discuss all relevant facts related to their work outcomes.  
C) ✗ Environmental sustainability is a critical consideration in engineering design.  
D) ✗ Engineers are accountable for societal impact, not just technical success.  

**Correct:** A, B


#### 5. In the context of engineering ethics, what actions might an engineer take if they encounter wrongdoing in a project?  
A) ✗ Ignoring issues to protect job security is unethical.  
B) ✓ Whistleblowing is an ethical obligation when illegal or unethical practices occur.  
C) ✗ Continuing without raising concerns violates professional ethics.  
D) ✓ Speaking out publicly against unsafe projects aligns with social responsibility.  

**Correct:** B, D


#### 6. Which of the following best describe the rules of conduct expected from professional engineers?  
A) ✓ Acting with fairness and integrity is required.  
B) ✗ Prioritizing personal gain over public safety contradicts ethical rules.  
C) ✓ Safeguarding public interest in health, safety, and environment is essential.  
D) ✓ Upholding dignity and reputation is mandatory.  

**Correct:** A, C, D


#### 7. What roles can an engineer typically assume within a professional setting?  
A) ✓ Team leader and motivator are common roles.  
B) ✗ Engineers rarely work as sole independent operators without collaboration.  
C) ✓ Decision maker and manager roles are often held by engineers.  
D) ✓ Representative and planner are typical responsibilities.  

**Correct:** A, C, D


#### 8. Which responsibilities fall under the categories of an engineer’s duties?  
A) ✓ Research and development is part of engineering functions.  
B) ✓ Project implementation and operation are key duties.  
C) ✗ Marketing and sales are not primary engineering responsibilities.  
D) ✓ Network planning and design is a core responsibility.  

**Correct:** A, B, D


#### 9. When managing engineering projects, what key job functions must an engineer perform?  
A) ✓ Allocating resources and assigning targets to staff is required.  
B) ✓ Organizing competency development programs supports staff growth.  
C) ✓ Planning and budgeting based on corporate goals is essential.  
D) ✗ Ignoring progress reviews contradicts good management practice.  

**Correct:** A, B, C


#### 10. Which legislative and regulatory frameworks are relevant to engineering practice?  
A) ✓ Environmental Regulations impact engineering projects and compliance.  
B) ✓ Engineering Council Act 04/2017 governs engineering practice.  
C) ✗ International Trade Agreements are not directly related to engineering practice.  
D) ✓ Industrial Dispute Act affects workplace relations relevant to engineers.  

**Correct:** A, B, D
