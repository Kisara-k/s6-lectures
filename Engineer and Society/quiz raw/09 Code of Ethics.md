## 10. Code of Ethics

## Questions

#### 1. Which of the following are true about Codes of Ethics and Professional Conduct in the ICT industry?  
A) They offer guidelines for remediation if violations occur.  
B) They are only relevant to software engineers, not other ICT professionals.  
C) They provide prescriptive guidance for ethical dilemmas faced by practitioners.  
D) They are legally binding documents enforceable by government law.  

#### 2. The IFIP Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct includes which of the following parts?  
A) Technical Standards and Specifications  
B) General Ethical Principles  
C) Professional Leadership Principles  
D) Professional Responsibilities  

#### 3. According to the IFIP Code, which of the following is NOT explicitly listed as a General Ethical Principle?  
A) Respect privacy and confidentiality  
B) Contribute to society and human well-being  
C) Avoid harm  
D) Maximize profit for the employer  

#### 4. Under the ACM Software Engineering Code of Ethics, which of the following actions is considered ethical when dealing with software safety?  
A) Ignore minor safety issues if deadlines are tight  
B) Balance competing interests while prioritizing public safety  
C) Disclose actual or potential dangers of software  
D) Approve software only if it is safe  

#### 5. Which of the following statements about privacy are correct?  
A) Privacy violations only affect legal rights, not human dignity.  
B) Privacy is related to the concept of access and zones of inaccessibility.  
C) Information technology has no significant impact on privacy erosion.  
D) Privacy benefits include individual growth and freedom to be oneself.  

#### 6. Regarding confidentiality and data protection, which practices are recommended?  
A) Share data freely to promote transparency.  
B) Destroy data responsibly when it is no longer needed.  
C) Use data collected for one purpose for unrelated marketing without consent.  
D) Collect only the data necessary for the stated purpose.  

#### 7. Which of the following are responsibilities of a computing professional under the IFIP Code’s Professional Responsibilities section?  
A) Perform work only in areas of competence  
B) Prioritize employer interests over public good in all cases  
C) Accept and provide appropriate professional review  
D) Give comprehensive analysis of computer systems and their impacts  

#### 8. The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is best described as:  
A) A voluntary guideline for data protection in the EU.  
B) A regulation that came into effect in March 2018.  
C) A law enacted by Sri Lanka in 2022.  
D) A regulation that unifies data protection laws across the European Union.  

#### 9. Which of the following are true about professional leadership principles in the IFIP Code?  
A) Leaders should manage personnel to enhance quality of working life.  
B) Leaders should ensure the public good is the central concern in computing work.  
C) Leaders should prioritize organizational profit over ethical principles.  
D) Leaders should create opportunities for professional growth within their teams.  

#### 10. In the ACM Software Engineering Code of Ethics, which of the following are considered ethical management practices?  
A) Provide employees charged with misconduct due process.  
B) Punish employees who express ethical concerns about a project.  
C) Ensure software engineers know policies for protecting confidential information.  
D) Assign work without considering employees’ abilities.

## Answers

#### 1. Which of the following are true about Codes of Ethics and Professional Conduct in the ICT industry?  
A) ✓ They offer guidelines for remediation if violations occur.  
B) ✗ They apply broadly to all ICT professionals, not just software engineers.  
C) ✓ They provide prescriptive guidance for ethical dilemmas faced by practitioners.  
D) ✗ They are not legally binding laws but professional guidelines.  

**Correct:** A, C


#### 2. The IFIP Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct includes which of the following parts?  
A) ✗ Technical Standards and Specifications are not a part of the IFIP Code structure.  
B) ✓ General Ethical Principles is part of the IFIP Code.  
C) ✓ Professional Leadership Principles is included.  
D) ✓ Professional Responsibilities is included.  

**Correct:** B, C, D


#### 3. According to the IFIP Code, which of the following is NOT explicitly listed as a General Ethical Principle?  
A) ✓ Respect privacy and confidentiality are explicitly stated.  
B) ✓ Contribute to society and human well-being is explicitly stated.  
C) ✓ Avoid harm is explicitly stated.  
D) ✗ Maximize profit for the employer is not an ethical principle in the code.  

**Correct:** D


#### 4. Under the ACM Software Engineering Code of Ethics, which of the following actions is considered ethical when dealing with software safety?  
A) ✗ Ignoring safety issues is unethical regardless of deadlines.  
B) ✓ Balancing interests while prioritizing public safety is consistent with the code.  
C) ✓ Disclose actual or potential dangers is required.  
D) ✓ Approve software only if it is safe is a key ethical requirement.  

**Correct:** B, C, D


#### 5. Which of the following statements about privacy are correct?  
A) ✗ Privacy violations affect human dignity, not just legal rights.  
B) ✓ Privacy relates to access and zones of inaccessibility.  
C) ✗ Information technology significantly erodes privacy.  
D) ✓ Privacy benefits include individual growth and freedom to be oneself.  

**Correct:** B, D


#### 6. Regarding confidentiality and data protection, which practices are recommended?  
A) ✗ Sharing data freely contradicts confidentiality principles.  
B) ✓ Destroying data responsibly when no longer needed is recommended.  
C) ✗ Using data for unrelated purposes without consent violates privacy.  
D) ✓ Collecting only necessary data is recommended.  

**Correct:** B, D


#### 7. Which of the following are responsibilities of a computing professional under the IFIP Code’s Professional Responsibilities section?  
A) ✓ Perform work only in areas of competence is required.  
B) ✗ Prioritizing employer interests over public good in all cases contradicts the code.  
C) ✓ Accept and provide appropriate professional review is required.  
D) ✓ Give comprehensive analysis of systems and impacts is required.  

**Correct:** A, C, D


#### 8. The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is best described as:  
A) ✗ GDPR is a binding regulation, not voluntary guidelines.  
B) ✓ It came into effect in March 2018.  
C) ✗ It is not a Sri Lankan law; Sri Lanka has its own Personal Data Protection Act.  
D) ✓ It unifies data protection laws across the EU.  

**Correct:** B, D


#### 9. Which of the following are true about professional leadership principles in the IFIP Code?  
A) ✓ Managing personnel to enhance quality of working life is stated.  
B) ✓ Ensuring public good is central is explicitly stated.  
C) ✗ Prioritizing profit over ethics contradicts the code.  
D) ✓ Creating opportunities for professional growth is stated.  

**Correct:** A, B, D


#### 10. In the ACM Software Engineering Code of Ethics, which of the following are considered ethical management practices?  
A) ✓ Providing due process for misconduct charges is ethical.  
B) ✗ Punishing employees for ethical concerns violates the code.  
C) ✓ Ensuring knowledge of confidentiality policies is ethical.  
D) ✗ Assigning work without considering abilities is unethical.  

**Correct:** A, C
