## 6. Case Study 1

## Questions

#### 1. What are the primary ethical obligations of Engineer-A in the fire investigation case involving a confidential settlement?  
A) Disclose all technical findings immediately to protect public safety regardless of confidentiality  
B) Prioritize the client’s interests over public safety in all circumstances  
C) Seek alternative ways to communicate technical concerns without breaching confidentiality  
D) Maintain confidentiality of the forensic engineering report as required by the court-approved settlement  

#### 2. Which of the following principles from the IESL Code of Ethics are directly relevant to Engineer-A’s dilemma?  
A) Build Reputation on Merit  
B) Speak out objectively and truthfully  
C) Serve benefactors faithfully with no conflict to other principles  
D) Public Goodness is Paramount  

#### 3. According to the NSPE Code of Ethics, what should an engineer do if their professional judgment regarding public safety is overruled by their employer or client?  
A) Immediately resign from the project without further action  
B) Notify the relevant authorities about the potential danger  
C) Keep the information confidential to protect the client’s interests  
D) Inform the client or employer and escalate the issue to appropriate authorities if necessary  

#### 4. In the context of confidentiality and public safety, which of the following statements best describes the conflict faced by Engineer-A?  
A) Public safety concerns always override confidentiality agreements  
B) Confidentiality to the client always overrides public safety concerns  
C) Confidentiality is only important if there is a written agreement, not a court settlement  
D) Both confidentiality and public safety are vital and must be balanced carefully  

#### 5. Which of the following actions would be considered ethically acceptable for Engineer-A under the circumstances?  
A) Ignoring the settlement agreement to warn the public about safety issues  
B) Reporting the issue anonymously to regulatory authorities  
C) Conducting further research to explain technical concerns without revealing confidential details  
D) Disclosing the forensic report contents to the public without permission  

#### 6. The IESL Code of Ethics clause “Limit to area of competence” implies which of the following for Engineer-A?  
A) Engineer-A should expand her competence rapidly to cover all aspects of the case  
B) Engineer-A should only provide opinions on aspects of the investigation within her expertise  
C) Engineer-A can delegate all responsibilities to other experts to avoid ethical dilemmas  
D) Engineer-A must avoid making any statements about the investigation if unsure  

#### 7. Which of the following best describes the role of “judgment” and “will power” in resolving ethical dilemmas faced by engineers?  
A) Judgment is irrelevant if legal agreements dictate confidentiality  
B) Will power is necessary to uphold ethical principles even under pressure  
C) Judgment should always favor the client’s interests over public welfare  
D) Will power is only needed when ethical codes are ambiguous  

#### 8. According to the NSPE Code of Ethics, which of the following is NOT a fundamental canon relevant to this case?  
A) Hold paramount the safety, health, and welfare of the public  
B) Avoid deceptive acts  
C) Perform services only in the areas of competence  
D) Prioritize employer’s financial interests over public safety  

#### 9. What is the significance of the court-approved settlement agreement in Engineer-A’s ethical decision-making?  
A) It nullifies Engineer-A’s ethical obligation to protect public safety  
B) It legally binds Engineer-A to maintain confidentiality, limiting disclosure options  
C) It allows Engineer-A to disclose information if public harm is imminent  
D) It requires Engineer-A to prioritize client interests above all else  

#### 10. Which of the following best summarizes the recommended ethical approach for Engineer-A in this case?  
A) Ignore the settlement and prioritize public safety without regard to legal consequences  
B) Disclose the full forensic report to the public to prevent future harm  
C) Withdraw from the case and avoid any further involvement to prevent conflict of interest  
D) Strictly maintain confidentiality but seek alternative ways to raise awareness of safety concerns

## Answers

#### 1. What are the primary ethical obligations of Engineer-A in the fire investigation case involving a confidential settlement?  
A) ✗ Disclose all technical findings immediately to protect public safety regardless of confidentiality — Disclosure would breach the court order unless imminent harm exists, which is not indicated.  
B) ✗ Prioritize the client’s interests over public safety in all circumstances — Public safety is paramount and cannot be overridden by client interests.  
C) ✓ Seek alternative ways to communicate technical concerns without breaching confidentiality — Ethical middle ground suggested to balance public interest and confidentiality.  
D) ✓ Maintain confidentiality of the forensic engineering report as required by the court-approved settlement — The settlement legally binds Engineer-A to confidentiality.  

**Correct:** C, D


#### 2. Which of the following principles from the IESL Code of Ethics are directly relevant to Engineer-A’s dilemma?  
A) ✗ Build Reputation on Merit — Important generally but not directly related to this dilemma.  
B) ✓ Speak out objectively and truthfully — Relevant to the obligation to truthfulness balanced with confidentiality.  
C) ✓ Serve benefactors faithfully with no conflict to other principles — Relevant because Engineer-A must serve the client but not violate public interest.  
D) ✓ Public Goodness is Paramount — Central to the conflict between confidentiality and public safety.  

**Correct:** B, C, D


#### 3. According to the NSPE Code of Ethics, what should an engineer do if their professional judgment regarding public safety is overruled by their employer or client?  
A) ✗ Immediately resign from the project without further action — Resignation alone is insufficient; further steps are required.  
B) ✓ Notify the relevant authorities about the potential danger — Required to protect public safety when overruled.  
C) ✗ Keep the information confidential to protect the client’s interests — Public safety overrides confidentiality in such cases.  
D) ✓ Inform the client or employer and escalate the issue to appropriate authorities if necessary — Proper protocol before notifying authorities.  

**Correct:** B, D


#### 4. In the context of confidentiality and public safety, which of the following statements best describes the conflict faced by Engineer-A?  
A) ✗ Public safety concerns always override confidentiality agreements — Not always; depends on urgency and harm.  
B) ✗ Confidentiality to the client always overrides public safety concerns — Incorrect; public safety can override confidentiality.  
C) ✗ Confidentiality is only important if there is a written agreement, not a court settlement — Court settlements are legally binding and important.  
D) ✓ Both confidentiality and public safety are vital and must be balanced carefully — Reflects the ethical tension in the case.  

**Correct:** D


#### 5. Which of the following actions would be considered ethically acceptable for Engineer-A under the circumstances?  
A) ✗ Ignoring the settlement agreement to warn the public about safety issues — Violates legal and ethical obligations unless imminent harm exists.  
B) ✗ Reporting the issue anonymously to regulatory authorities — Could breach confidentiality and legal agreements unless imminent harm is present; not supported by case facts.  
C) ✓ Conducting further research to explain technical concerns without revealing confidential details — Ethical way to raise awareness without breaching confidentiality.  
D) ✗ Disclosing the forensic report contents to the public without permission — Breaches confidentiality and court order.  

**Correct:** C


#### 6. The IESL Code of Ethics clause “Limit to area of competence” implies which of the following for Engineer-A?  
A) ✗ Engineer-A should expand her competence rapidly to cover all aspects of the case — Unrealistic and not immediate ethical requirement.  
B) ✓ Engineer-A should only provide opinions on aspects of the investigation within her expertise — Ensures professional integrity and accuracy.  
C) ✗ Engineer-A can delegate all responsibilities to other experts to avoid ethical dilemmas — Delegation is possible but does not remove ethical responsibility.  
D) ✗ Engineer-A must avoid making any statements about the investigation if unsure — Should seek to clarify or defer but not avoid responsibility entirely.  

**Correct:** B


#### 7. Which of the following best describes the role of “judgment” and “will power” in resolving ethical dilemmas faced by engineers?  
A) ✗ Judgment is irrelevant if legal agreements dictate confidentiality — Judgment is always relevant in ethical decisions.  
B) ✓ Will power is necessary to uphold ethical principles even under pressure — Ethical dilemmas require courage and resolve.  
C) ✗ Judgment should always favor the client’s interests over public welfare — Public welfare is paramount.  
D) ✗ Will power is only needed when ethical codes are ambiguous — Will power is needed in all ethical challenges, ambiguous or not.  

**Correct:** B


#### 8. According to the NSPE Code of Ethics, which of the following is NOT a fundamental canon relevant to this case?  
A) ✗ Hold paramount the safety, health, and welfare of the public — Highly relevant.  
B) ✗ Avoid deceptive acts — Relevant to maintaining integrity.  
C) ✗ Perform services only in the areas of competence — Relevant to professional responsibility.  
D) ✓ Prioritize employer’s financial interests over public safety — Contradicts fundamental ethical principles.  

**Correct:** D


#### 9. What is the significance of the court-approved settlement agreement in Engineer-A’s ethical decision-making?  
A) ✗ It nullifies Engineer-A’s ethical obligation to protect public safety — Ethical obligations remain, but disclosure is limited by settlement terms.  
B) ✓ It legally binds Engineer-A to maintain confidentiality, limiting disclosure options — Settlement imposes confidentiality obligations.  
C) ✗ It allows Engineer-A to disclose information if public harm is imminent — No imminent harm indicated; disclosure not automatically allowed.  
D) ✗ It requires Engineer-A to prioritize client interests above all else — Public safety remains paramount despite client interests.  

**Correct:** B


#### 10. Which of the following best summarizes the recommended ethical approach for Engineer-A in this case?  
A) ✗ Ignore the settlement and prioritize public safety without regard to legal consequences — Not ethically or legally justified here.  
B) ✗ Disclose the full forensic report to the public to prevent future harm — Breaches confidentiality and court order.  
C) ✗ Withdraw from the case and avoid any further involvement to prevent conflict of interest — Withdrawal alone does not resolve ethical obligations.  
D) ✓ Strictly maintain confidentiality but seek alternative ways to raise awareness of safety concerns — Balances legal and ethical duties.  

**Correct:** D
