B) Ethical laws always cover every aspect of ethical behavior  
C) Ethics involves evaluating people’s behavior based on rational examination of morality  
D) Laws are created by rulers or parliaments and may not always be ethical

## Answers

#### 1. Which of the following best distinguishes ethics from legality?  
A) ✓ Laws can be unethical if made by unethical rulers  
B) ✗ Ethical rulers may make ethical laws, but this is not guaranteed in all cases  
C) ✗ Not all ethical actions are covered by law; ethics can go beyond legal requirements  
D) ✓ Ethics is based on morality, while legality is based on laws made by rulers or parliaments  

**Correct:** A, D


#### 2. Which characteristics are essential to define a profession?  
A) ✓ Expert knowledge distinct from on-the-job skill is a key characteristic  
B) ✓ Governance through entrance requirements and discipline is required  
C) ✓ Autonomy in diagnosis, treatment, follow-up, and evaluation is essential  
D) ✓ Service to society is a fundamental aspect of a profession  

**Correct:** A, B, C, D


#### 3. How does a professional body differ from a trade union?  
A) ✓ Professional bodies focus on governance and ethical standards  
B) ✗ Trade unions do not set entrance requirements; professional bodies do  
C) ✗ Negotiating salaries is primarily a trade union role, not a professional body’s  
D) ✓ Trade unions focus on protecting members’ rights and working conditions  

**Correct:** A, D


#### 4. Which of the following statements about professional licensing are true?  
A) ✓ Some professions require registration to practice, e.g., medicine and law  
B) ✓ Vendor certifications sometimes drive proficiency in ICT  
C) ✓ There is increasing pressure to regulate ICT as a profession  
D) ✗ IT professions often do not require professional licensing universally  

**Correct:** A, B, C


#### 5. Regarding the role of professional bodies, which are correct?  
A) ✗ Collective bargaining for wages is usually a trade union function  
B) ✓ They define how members should behave professionally  
C) ✓ They specify entry requirements for the profession  
D) ✓ They govern discipline and ethical conduct within the profession  

**Correct:** B, C, D


#### 6. Which of the following are examples of professional bodies and their associated professions?  
A) ✓ Institution of Engineers Sri Lanka - Engineering  
B) ✗ British Computer Society is related to IT, not medical profession  
C) ✓ Chartered Institute of Management Accountants - Accountancy  
D) ✓ Sri Lanka Institute of Architects - Architecture  

**Correct:** A, C, D


#### 7. What is the relationship between morality, ethics, and society’s rules?  
A) ✗ Morality is closely linked to society’s rules, not independent  
B) ✓ Ethics is the rational examination and evaluation of morality  
C) ✗ Society’s rules do not always perfectly align with ethical principles  
D) ✓ Morality refers to society’s rules of conduct about what people ought or ought not to do  

**Correct:** B, D


#### 8. Which of the following scenarios illustrate potential conflicts between legal and ethical perspectives?  
A) ✓ Giving a convicted murderer due process is legal and ethical, but can be controversial  
B) ✓ Causing harm to public property is illegal and unethical, showing overlap but also conflict in enforcement  
C) ✓ Criticizing judiciary flaws may be legal but risk contempt of court, showing conflict  
D) ✓ Banning peaceful demonstrations may be legal under some regimes but unethical  

**Correct:** A, B, C, D


#### 9. What does autonomy in a profession typically involve?  
A) ✗ Setting laws is a governmental function, not professional autonomy  
B) ✓ Responsibility for one’s professional decisions and actions is part of autonomy  
C) ✓ Freedom to diagnose, treat, follow up, and evaluate without external interference  
D) ✗ Complete independence from professional bodies is not typical; governance exists  

**Correct:** B, C


#### 10. Which of the following statements about ethics and laws is accurate?  
A) ✗ Legal compliance does not guarantee ethical behavior in all situations  
B) ✗ Ethical laws do not cover every aspect of ethical behavior  
C) ✓ Ethics involves evaluating people’s behavior based on rational examination of morality  
D) ✓ Laws are created by rulers or parliaments and may not always be ethical  

**Correct:** C, D
