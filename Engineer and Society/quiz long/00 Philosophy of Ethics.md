## Answers

#### 1. What best describes the primary focus of ethics as a philosophical discipline?  
A) ✗ Promoting wealth is not the primary concern of ethics.  
B) ✗ Enforcing laws is the role of legal systems, not ethics.  
C) ✓ Ethics systematically analyzes right and wrong in human actions.  
D) ✗ Personal preferences are subjective and not the focus of ethics.  

**Correct:** C


#### 2. Which of the following are considered intrinsic values?  
A) ✓ Courage is an intrinsic value, valued inherently.  
B) ✗ Wealth is an extrinsic value, a means to an end.  
C) ✗ Teamwork is extrinsic, a means to achieve goals.  
D) ✓ Honesty is an intrinsic value, valued for its own sake.  

**Correct:** A, D


#### 3. How do values differ from ethics?  
A) ✗ Values are not external rules; ethics involves reasoning beyond feelings.  
B) ✗ Values tend to be stable, ethics can evolve with reasoning.  
C) ✗ Values are not legal requirements; laws are.  
D) ✓ Values are stable beliefs; ethics is the study of what those values should be.  

**Correct:** D


#### 4. Which statement best captures the relationship between morality and ethics?  
A) ✓ Morality governs societal attitudes; ethics develops means to determine values.  
B) ✗ Ethics and morality are related, not unrelated.  
C) ✗ Morality is social, ethics involves reasoning about those morals.  
D) ✗ Neither ethics nor morality is inherently more important.  

**Correct:** A


#### 5. Laws are primarily:  
A) ✗ Laws tend to be less flexible than ethics in some cases.  
B) ✓ Laws are rules enforced by authorities, often based on morals.  
C) ✗ Laws and ethics are related but not the same.  
D) ✗ Laws are not personal beliefs but formal rules.  

**Correct:** B


#### 6. The Greek word ‘Ethos’ means:  
A) ✗ Ethos is unrelated to wealth.  
B) ✓ Ethos means character or guiding beliefs of a community.  
C) ✗ Ethos is not simply personal opinion.  
D) ✗ Ethos does not mean law.  

**Correct:** B


#### 7. Which of the following statements about ethics and morals is true?  
A) ✗ Ethics and morals can conflict; they are not always aligned.  
B) ✗ Ethics and morals can and do conflict.  
C) ✓ Ethics are practical group rules; morals are personal principles shaped by culture.  
D) ✗ Morals are internal, ethics are external rules but not feelings.  

**Correct:** C


#### 8. Which example illustrates a situation where ethics and morals might conflict?  
A) ✗ Following safety protocols aligns ethics and morals.  
B) ✓ Lawyer’s confidentiality vs personal belief about guilt is a classic ethics vs morals conflict.  
C) ✗ Laws against theft reflect societal morals, no conflict here.  
D) ✗ Being honest aligns ethics and morals, no conflict.  

**Correct:** B


#### 9. How do Eastern ethics generally differ from Western ethics?  
A) ✗ Eastern ethics are internal, Western ethics are more external.  
B) ✗ They are not identical.  
C) ✓ Eastern ethics emphasize harmony and internal discipline; Western ethics emphasize rational thought and individualism.  
D) ✗ This reverses the actual focus of each tradition.  

**Correct:** C


#### 10. Which of the following is NOT a characteristic of professional ethics?  
A) ✗ Governing professional behavior is a key characteristic.  
B) ✗ Ensuring safety and reliability is a core purpose.  
C) ✗ Moral conflicts with personal beliefs can occur in professional ethics.  
D) ✓ Professional ethics may conflict with personal ethics, so they don’t always align perfectly.  

**Correct:** D


#### 11. Why is it important for engineers to study professional ethics?  
A) ✗ Profit should not override safety in ethics.  
B) ✓ To increase ethical sensitivity and judgment.  
C) ✗ Avoiding legal consequences is not the only or main reason.  
D) ✓ To improve willpower to act ethically under pressure.  

**Correct:** B, D


#### 12. Which of the following best describes an ethical dilemma in engineering?  
A) ✗ Ethical dilemmas involve unclear choices, not clear right/wrong.  
B) ✗ Blindly following policy is not ethical decision-making.  
C) ✓ Ethical dilemmas involve conflicts between competing values requiring judgment.  
D) ✗ Ethical dilemmas affect more than just the engineer personally.  

**Correct:** C


#### 13. What is the main difference between personal ethics and professional ethics?  
A) ✓ Personal ethics are individual beliefs; professional ethics are formal rules set by professions.  
B) ✗ Neither is inherently more important.  
C) ✗ Personal ethics apply broadly, not just at work.  
D) ✗ Conflicts can and do occur between the two.  

**Correct:** A


#### 14. Which of the following statements about values is correct?  
A) ✗ Values are generally stable, not temporary.  
B) ✗ Values strongly influence ethics.  
C) ✗ Values are not legally enforced; laws are.  
D) ✓ Values are emotional investments guiding life choices.  

**Correct:** D


#### 15. Which of the following is an example of extrinsic values?  
A) ✗ Courage is intrinsic.  
B) ✓ Wealth is extrinsic, a means to an end.  
C) ✓ Physical fitness is extrinsic, a means to achieve other goals.  
D) ✗ Truthfulness is intrinsic.  

**Correct:** B, C


#### 16. How can ethics be described in relation to external rules?  
A) ✓ Ethics vary depending on organizations, groups, or cultures.  
B) ✗ Ethics are influenced by society, not purely personal.  
C) ✗ Ethics can vary between environments.  
D) ✗ Ethics are not always imposed by law.  

**Correct:** A


#### 17. Which of the following best explains why ethics is considered a “systematic study”?  
A) ✗ Ethics is not solely based on religion.  
B) ✗ Ethics considers consequences, not ignores them.  
C) ✗ Ethics is not random opinion.  
D) ✓ Ethics uses reasoned analysis to evaluate right and wrong.  

**Correct:** D


#### 18. What does the phrase “What you do when no one is looking” best relate to?  
A) ✗ Ethics involves reasoning about behavior, not just hidden actions.  
B) ✗ Values are broader beliefs, not just hidden actions.

**Correct:** D


#### 19. Which of the following statements about the relationship between ethics and law is true?  
A) ✗ Ethics do not always override laws.  
B) ✗ Laws are not always ethical (some laws may be unjust).  
C) ✗ Ethics and laws are related but not identical.  
D) ✓ Laws are generally based on moral principles but enforced externally.  

**Correct:** D


#### 20. In the context of professional ethics, what is the significance of “ethical willpower”?  
A) ✗ Following personal morals over professional codes may cause conflict, not willpower.  
B) ✗ Knowledge of laws is different from willpower.  
C) ✓ Ethical willpower is the ability to act ethically even when difficult.  
D) ✗ Understanding theories is knowledge, not willpower.  

**Correct:** C
