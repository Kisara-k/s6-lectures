## Answers

#### 1. What best describes the primary focus of ethics as a philosophical discipline?  
A) ✓ Ethics systematically analyzes right and wrong in human actions.  
B) ✗ Personal preferences are subjective and not the focus of ethics.  
C) ✗ Enforcing laws is the role of legal systems, not ethics.  
D) ✗ Promoting wealth is not the primary concern of ethics.  

**Correct:** A


#### 2. Which of the following are considered intrinsic values?  
A) ✗ Wealth is an extrinsic value, a means to an end.  
B) ✗ Teamwork is extrinsic, a means to achieve goals.  
C) ✓ Courage is an intrinsic value, valued inherently.  
D) ✓ Honesty is an intrinsic value, valued for its own sake.  

**Correct:** C, D


#### 3. How do values differ from ethics?  
A) ✗ Values are not legal requirements; laws are.  
B) ✗ Values tend to be stable, ethics can evolve with reasoning.  
C) ✓ Values are stable beliefs; ethics is the study of what those values should be.  
D) ✗ Values are not external rules; ethics involves reasoning beyond feelings.  

**Correct:** C


#### 4. Which statement best captures the relationship between morality and ethics?  
A) ✗ Morality is social, ethics involves reasoning about those morals.  
B) ✗ Neither ethics nor morality is inherently more important.  
C) ✓ Morality governs societal attitudes; ethics develops means to determine values.  
D) ✗ Ethics and morality are related, not unrelated.  

**Correct:** C


#### 5. Laws are primarily:  
A) ✗ Laws and ethics are related but not the same.  
B) ✗ Laws tend to be less flexible than ethics in some cases.  
C) ✗ Laws are not personal beliefs but formal rules.  
D) ✓ Laws are rules enforced by authorities, often based on morals.  

**Correct:** D


#### 6. The Greek word ‘Ethos’ means:  
A) ✗ Ethos is unrelated to wealth.  
B) ✓ Ethos means character or guiding beliefs of a community.  
C) ✗ Ethos does not mean law.  
D) ✗ Ethos is not simply personal opinion.  

**Correct:** B


#### 7. Which of the following statements about ethics and morals is true?  
A) ✗ Morals are internal, ethics are external rules but not feelings.  
B) ✓ Ethics are practical group rules; morals are personal principles shaped by culture.  
C) ✗ Ethics and morals can conflict; they are not always aligned.  
D) ✗ Ethics and morals can and do conflict.  

**Correct:** B


#### 8. Which example illustrates a situation where ethics and morals might conflict?  
A) ✗ Following safety protocols aligns ethics and morals.  
B) ✗ Being honest aligns ethics and morals, no conflict.  
C) ✗ Laws against theft reflect societal morals, no conflict here.  
D) ✓ Lawyer’s confidentiality vs personal belief about guilt is a classic ethics vs morals conflict.  

**Correct:** D


#### 9. How do Eastern ethics generally differ from Western ethics?  
A) ✗ Eastern ethics are internal, Western ethics are more external.  
B) ✗ They are not identical.  
C) ✗ This reverses the actual focus of each tradition.  
D) ✓ Eastern ethics emphasize harmony and internal discipline; Western ethics emphasize rational thought and individualism.  

**Correct:** D


#### 10. Which of the following is NOT a characteristic of professional ethics?  
A) ✓ Professional ethics may conflict with personal ethics, so they don’t always align perfectly.  
B) ✗ Governing professional behavior is a key characteristic.  
C) ✗ Moral conflicts with personal beliefs can occur in professional ethics.  
D) ✗ Ensuring safety and reliability is a core purpose.  

**Correct:** A


#### 11. Why is it important for engineers to study professional ethics?

#### 12. Which of the following best describes an ethical dilemma in engineering?

#### 13. What is the main difference between personal ethics and professional ethics?  
A) ✗ Neither is inherently more important.

#### 14. Which of the following statements about values is correct?
