## 2. Role of an Engineer

## Questions

#### 1. What distinguishes an engineer from a scientist?  
A) Scientists apply engineering principles to build devices.  
B) Scientists focus on explaining natural phenomena.  
C) Engineers create practical solutions based on scientific discoveries.  
D) Engineers primarily study the world as it is.  

#### 2. Which of the following are essential competencies for a Chartered Engineer?

#### 3. What is the primary responsibility of an engineer in society?

B) Prioritizing cost savings over safety concerns.

#### 6. Which of the following best describe the benefits of being an engineer?

B) Guaranteed prestige without professional responsibility.  
C) Job satisfaction from solving real-world problems.  
D) Limited career options but high financial rewards.  

#### 7. According to professional codes of conduct, engineers must:

B) Act with integrity and fairness in all professional matters.  
C) Prioritize company profits over public safety.  
D) Safeguard public health, safety, and the environment.  

#### 8. Which roles can an engineer typically perform in a professional setting?

B) Team leader and motivator.

#### 14. Which statements about the relationship between society and engineers are true?

#### 15. What does it mean for an engineer to “exercise professional skill and judgment”?

#### 16. Which of the following reflect an engineer’s commitment to sustainability?

A) By mentoring and sharing knowledge about ethical practices.  
B) By discouraging discussion of controversial projects.  
C) By promoting awareness of the societal impact of engineering.  
D) By focusing only on technical skills and ignoring ethics.  

#### 20. Which of the following best describe the difference between engineering and science?  
A) Scientists innovate solutions for societal benefit.  
B) Engineering is concerned with economic use of materials and forces.  
C) Engineering applies scientific knowledge to solve practical problems.  
D) Science focuses on creating new devices and systems.

## Answers

#### 1. What distinguishes an engineer from a scientist?  
A) ✗ Scientists apply engineering principles to build devices. (Scientists focus on discovery, not application.)  
B) ✓ Scientists focus on explaining natural phenomena.  
C) ✓ Engineers create practical solutions based on scientific discoveries.  
D) ✗ Engineers primarily study the world as it is. (Engineers create new solutions, not just study existing conditions.)  

**Correct:** B, C


#### 2. Which of the following are essential competencies for a Chartered Engineer?  
A) ✓ Ability to communicate and lead teams.  
B) ✓ Commitment to professional ethics and continuous learning.  
C) ✓ Deep knowledge of engineering principles.  
D) ✗ Exclusive focus on technical skills without management. (Management and leadership are also required.)  

**Correct:** A, B, C


#### 3. What is the primary responsibility of an engineer in society?  
A) ✓ Prioritizing public safety and welfare.  
B) ✗ Innovating without regard to environmental impact. (Environmental impact must be considered.)  
C) ✓ Ensuring the reliability and safety of their designs.  
D) ✗ Maximizing profits for their employer. (Profit is secondary to public safety.)  

**Correct:** A, C


#### 4. Which actions reflect an engineer’s social responsibility?  
A) ✓ Designing sustainable and environmentally friendly technologies.  
B) ✓ Educating the public about technological risks.  
C) ✗ Ignoring whistleblowing to protect company interests. (Engineers must support whistleblowing.)  
D) ✓ Refusing to work on unethical projects.  

**Correct:** A, B, D


#### 5. The “precautionary principle” in engineering design means:  
A) ✗ Proceeding with projects regardless of potential risks. (This is opposite to the principle.)  
B) ✗ Prioritizing cost savings over safety concerns. (Safety is prioritized.)  
C) ✓ Building redundancy and safety factors into designs.  
D) ✓ Avoiding actions that could cause serious harm when risks are uncertain.  

**Correct:** C, D


#### 6. Which of the following best describe the benefits of being an engineer?  
A) ✓ Intellectual development and creative thinking.  
B) ✗ Guaranteed prestige without professional responsibility. (Prestige comes with responsibility.)  
C) ✓ Job satisfaction from solving real-world problems.  
D) ✗ Limited career options but high financial rewards. (Engineering offers variety, not limited options.)  

**Correct:** A, C
