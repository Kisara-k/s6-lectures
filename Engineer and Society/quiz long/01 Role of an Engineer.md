## 2. Role of an Engineer

## Questions

#### 1. What distinguishes an engineer from a scientist?  
A) Scientists apply engineering principles to build devices.  
B) Scientists focus on explaining natural phenomena.  
C) Engineers primarily study the world as it is.  
D) Engineers create practical solutions based on scientific discoveries.  

#### 2. Which of the following are essential competencies for a Chartered Engineer?  
A) Deep knowledge of engineering principles.  
B) Ability to communicate and lead teams.  
C) Exclusive focus on technical skills without management.  
D) Commitment to professional ethics and continuous learning.  

#### 3. What is the primary responsibility of an engineer in society?  
A) Ensuring the reliability and safety of their designs.  
B) Innovating without regard to environmental impact.  
C) Prioritizing public safety and welfare.  
D) Maximizing profits for their employer.  

#### 4. Which actions reflect an engineer’s social responsibility?  
A) Ignoring whistleblowing to protect company interests.  
B) Educating the public about technological risks.  
C) Refusing to work on unethical projects.  
D) Designing sustainable and environmentally friendly technologies.  

#### 5. The “precautionary principle” in engineering design means:  
A) Avoiding actions that could cause serious harm when risks are uncertain.  
B) Building redundancy and safety factors into designs.  
C) Prioritizing cost savings over safety concerns.  
D) Proceeding with projects regardless of potential risks.  

#### 6. Which of the following best describe the benefits of being an engineer?  
A) Guaranteed prestige without professional responsibility.  
B) Job satisfaction from solving real-world problems.  
C) Intellectual development and creative thinking.  
D) Limited career options but high financial rewards.  

#### 7. According to professional codes of conduct, engineers must:  
A) Safeguard public health, safety, and the environment.  
B) Prioritize company profits over public safety.  
C) Act with integrity and fairness in all professional matters.  
D) Disregard the vocational progress of junior engineers.  

#### 8. Which roles can an engineer typically perform in a professional setting?  
A) Decision maker and planner.  
B) Team leader and motivator.  
C) Representative and rule enforcer.  
D) Solely a technical specialist without management duties.  

#### 9. What are key responsibilities of an engineer overseeing a project?  
A) Complying with relevant laws and regulations.  
B) Ignoring resource optimization to speed up work.  
C) Ensuring timely delivery and adherence to safety standards.  
D) Maintaining quality without compromising safety.  

#### 10. Which of the following are included in the categories of engineering responsibilities?  
A) Research and development.  
B) Operation and maintenance.  
C) Network planning and design.  
D) Marketing and sales strategy.  

#### 11. What are important job functions of an engineer in managing a team?  
A) Ignoring staff well-being to focus on deadlines.  
B) Organizing competency development programs.  
C) Planning and budgeting aligned with corporate goals.  
D) Allocating resources and assigning targets.  

#### 12. Which legislative acts are relevant to engineering practice?  
A) Copyright Act.  
B) Safety and Factory Ordinance.  
C) Environmental Regulations.  
D) Engineering Council Act 04/2017.  

#### 13. How should engineers handle situations where a project may harm society?  
A) Speak out publicly against the project.  
B) Report wrongdoing through appropriate channels.  
C) Refuse to participate if ethical standards are violated.  
D) Continue work without raising concerns.  

#### 14. Which statements about the relationship between society and engineers are true?  
A) Society demands goods and services that are reliable and safe.  
B) Economy and safety are key values engineers must deliver.  
C) Engineers have no responsibility toward environmental sustainability.  
D) Engineers should prioritize innovation over standards.  

#### 15. What does it mean for an engineer to “exercise professional skill and judgment”?  
A) Delegating all critical decisions to non-engineers.  
B) Discharging responsibilities with integrity.  
C) Making decisions without considering ethical implications.  
D) Applying knowledge to solve problems responsibly.  

#### 16. Which of the following reflect an engineer’s commitment to sustainability?  
A) Ignoring long-term consequences for short-term gains.  
B) Abiding by principles of sustainable development.  
C) Providing expert advice to non-experts about environmental risks.  
D) Designing technologies that minimize environmental impact.  

#### 17. What is the significance of “building in redundancy” in engineering design?  
A) It ensures continued operation if one part fails.  
B) It enhances safety by providing backup systems.  
C) It increases the complexity unnecessarily.  
D) It reduces costs by eliminating extra components.  

#### 18. Which of the following are true about the ethical responsibilities of engineers?  
A) They must protect the public interest in health and safety.  
B) They can ignore environmental concerns if the project is profitable.  
C) They must uphold the dignity and reputation of the profession.  
D) They should encourage the professional growth of colleagues.  

#### 19. How does an engineer contribute to educating future engineers about social responsibility?  
A) By mentoring and sharing knowledge about ethical practices.  
B) By focusing only on technical skills and ignoring ethics.  
C) By discouraging discussion of controversial projects.  
D) By promoting awareness of the societal impact of engineering.  

#### 20. Which of the following best describe the difference between engineering and science?  
A) Scientists innovate solutions for societal benefit.  
B) Engineering applies scientific knowledge to solve practical problems.  
C) Science focuses on creating new devices and systems.  
D) Engineering is concerned with economic use of materials and forces.

## Answers

#### 1. What distinguishes an engineer from a scientist?  
A) ✗ Scientists apply engineering principles to build devices. (Scientists focus on discovery, not application.)  
B) ✓ Scientists focus on explaining natural phenomena.  
C) ✗ Engineers primarily study the world as it is. (Engineers create new solutions, not just study existing conditions.)  
D) ✓ Engineers create practical solutions based on scientific discoveries.  

**Correct:** B, D


#### 2. Which of the following are essential competencies for a Chartered Engineer?  
A) ✓ Deep knowledge of engineering principles.  
B) ✓ Ability to communicate and lead teams.  
C) ✗ Exclusive focus on technical skills without management. (Management and leadership are also required.)  
D) ✓ Commitment to professional ethics and continuous learning.  

**Correct:** A, B, D


#### 3. What is the primary responsibility of an engineer in society?  
A) ✓ Ensuring the reliability and safety of their designs.  
B) ✗ Innovating without regard to environmental impact. (Environmental impact must be considered.)  
C) ✓ Prioritizing public safety and welfare.  
D) ✗ Maximizing profits for their employer. (Profit is secondary to public safety.)  

**Correct:** A, C


#### 4. Which actions reflect an engineer’s social responsibility?  
A) ✗ Ignoring whistleblowing to protect company interests. (Engineers must support whistleblowing.)  
B) ✓ Educating the public about technological risks.  
C) ✓ Refusing to work on unethical projects.  
D) ✓ Designing sustainable and environmentally friendly technologies.  

**Correct:** B, C, D

C) ✗ Prioritizing cost savings over safety concerns. (Safety is prioritized.)
