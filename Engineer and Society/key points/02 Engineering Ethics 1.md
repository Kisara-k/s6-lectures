## 3. Engineering Ethics 1

## Key Points

#### 1. 🎓 Profession and Professional Attributes  
- A profession requires advanced education and training.  
- Members of a profession follow special ethical standards and codes of conduct.  
- Work in a profession involves sophisticated skills, intellectual judgment, and discretion.  
- Membership requires formal education, not just practical training.  
- Professional bodies set admission and conduct standards and enforce them.  
- Professions produce significant public good and are usually well compensated.

#### 2. 👩‍💼 Who is a Professional?  
- A professional performs specialized work requiring high education and training.  
- Professionals see other members as peers and colleagues.  
- They exercise judgment and follow professional standards.  
- Professionals agree to work ethically according to a code of ethics.

#### 3. 📜 Professional Ethics  
- Professional ethics are principles governing behavior in a business or professional environment.  
- These ethics are often codified as a code of conduct that all members follow.  
- Professional ethics guide how individuals should act toward people and institutions in their profession.

#### 4. 🌟 Core Professional Values  
- **Accountability:** Taking responsibility for one’s actions and outcomes.  
- **Responsibility:** Performing assigned duties diligently and following rules.  
- **Integrity:** Being honest, truthful, and not taking credit for others’ work.  
- **Professional Attitude:** Maintaining positivity, motivation, openness to feedback, and handling stress well.  
- **Continuous Learning:** Always seeking to improve knowledge and skills.  
- **Teamwork:** Collaborating effectively and ensuring team alignment.

#### 5. ⚙️ Engineering Ethics  
- Engineering ethics is applied ethics focused on engineers’ obligations to the public, clients, employers, and the profession.  
- Engineers face ethical issues in technical practice and business conduct.  
- Common ethical issues include conflicts of interest, bribery, confidentiality, legal compliance, and use of employer assets.

#### 6. 🛠️ Importance of Engineering Ethics  
- Engineers are trusted to provide safe and reliable products and services.  
- They must adhere to high standards of professional behavior and ethical conduct.  
- Unethical or incompetent engineering can cause financial loss and loss of life.  
- Engineers often face ethical dilemmas requiring sound judgment and willpower.

#### 7. ⚖️ Ethical Dilemmas in Engineering  
- Engineers must balance practical constraints with ethical principles.  
- Ethical dilemmas require rational decision-making and cooperation.  
- Engineers are key decision-makers in business environments and must handle ethical challenges responsibly.

#### 8. 📏 Principles of Engineering Ethics  
- **Respect for Law, Public Good, and Life:** Engineers must prioritize public welfare and obey laws.  
- **Accuracy and Rigor:** Engineers must perform duties competently, update skills, and avoid misleading others.  
- **Integrity and Honesty:** Engineers must be fair, avoid corruption, and ensure their work does not harm others.  
- **Responsible Leadership:** Engineers should lead responsibly, stay informed on technology, and communicate truthfully.
