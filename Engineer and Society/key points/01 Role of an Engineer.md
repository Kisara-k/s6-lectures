## 2. Role of an Engineer

## Key Points

#### 1. 🛠️ Definition of Engineering  
- Engineering is the profession applying mathematical and natural sciences with judgment to economically utilize materials and forces of nature for mankind’s benefit.  
- Chartered Engineers are professionals recognized for competence, experience, and ethical standards in engineering.

#### 2. 🔬 Engineer vs Scientist  
- Scientists discover theories explaining natural phenomena.  
- Engineers innovate devices and solutions using scientific discoveries to solve real-world problems.

#### 3. 🌍 Society’s Expectations from Engineers  
- Engineers must deliver goods and services that are reliable, safe, secure, standardized, and economical.

#### 4. 🎓 Competencies Required for Chartered Engineers  
- Knowledge and understanding of engineering principles.  
- Ability to design and develop processes, systems, services, and products.  
- Responsibility, management, or leadership skills.  
- Communication and interpersonal skills.  
- Professional commitment and ethical conduct.

#### 5. ⚖️ Roles and Responsibilities of Engineers  
- Prioritize public safety and interest above all else.  
- Be accountable for the societal impact of their work.  
- Consider all relevant facts and possible outcomes in design, development, and deployment.  
- Refuse to work on unethical projects and speak out against wrongdoing.  
- Support whistleblowers and contribute to non-profit and community projects.  
- Educate future engineers on social responsibilities.  
- Conduct ethical risk and safety assessments.  
- Design sustainable technologies and follow the precautionary principle.  
- Build safety factors and redundancies into designs.

#### 6. 💼 Benefits of Being an Engineer  
- Job satisfaction, variety of career opportunities, challenging work, intellectual development, societal benefit, financial security, prestige, and involvement in innovation.

#### 7. 📜 Rules of Conduct for Engineers  
- Uphold dignity and reputation of the profession.  
- Act with fairness, integrity, and respect for health, safety, and the environment.  
- Exercise professional skill and judgment to the best of their ability.  
- Encourage vocational progress of colleagues.

#### 8. 👷‍♂️ Roles of an Engineer in Practice  
- Team leader, manager, representative, motivator, planner, decision maker, and rule follower.  
- Oversee operations ensuring timely delivery, safety, compliance, economy, and quality.

#### 9. 🏗️ Categories of Engineering Responsibilities  
- Network planning and design.  
- Project implementation.  
- Operation and maintenance.  
- Research and development.

#### 10. 📊 Key Job Functions  
- Planning and budgeting aligned with corporate goals.  
- Resource allocation and task assignment.  
- Process establishment and improvement.  
- Progress review comparing expected vs actual results.  
- Organizing competency development programs.  
- Staff welfare, performance appraisal, counseling, and coaching.

#### 11. 📜 Legislative Requirements  
- Engineering Council Act 04/2017.  
- Institution of Engineers Sri Lanka (IESL) Act.  
- Safety and Factory Ordinance.  
- Industrial Dispute Act.  
- Environmental regulations.
