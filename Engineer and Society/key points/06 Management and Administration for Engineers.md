## 7. Management and Administration for Engineers

## Key Points

#### 1. 🛠️ Management & Administration for Engineers  
- Engineers manage resources: humans, money, materials, and time.  
- Key management areas: Human Resource Management, Financial Management, Project Management, Procurement.  
- Engineers need knowledge of rules, regulations, government compliances, and effective communication.

#### 2. 🏢 Types of Organizations  
- Fully Government organizations are managed under the Ministry of Public Administration.  
- Semi-Government organizations are managed by the Ministry of Treasury - Public Enterprise Department.  
- Private organizations are managed by a Board of Directors.

#### 3. ⚖️ Labour Laws of Sri Lanka  
- Shop and Office Employees Act No 19 of 1954 and amendments.  
- Wages Board Ordinance No 27 of 1941 and amendments.  
- Factories Ordinance No 45 of 1942 and amendments.  
- Industrial Disputes Act No 43 of 1950 and amendments.  
- Workmen's Compensation Ordinance No 19 of 1934 and amendments.  
- Trade Union Ordinance No 14 of 1935 and amendments.  
- Maternity Benefits Ordinance No 32 of 1939 and amendments.  
- Termination of Employment of Workmen (Special Provisions) Act No 45 of 1971 and amendments.  
- Employees Provident Fund (EPF) Act No 15 of 1958 and amendments.  
- Employees Trust Fund (ETF) Act No 46 of 1980 and amendments.  
- Payment of Gratuity Act No 12 of 1983 and amendments.  
- National Minimum Wages of Workers Act No 3 of 2016.  
- Budgetary Relief Allowance Act No 4 of 2016.  
- Inland Revenue Act No 24 of 2017.

#### 4. 📜 Administration of Government Organizations  
- Fully Government organizations follow Administrative Regulations (Establishment Code), Financial Regulations, and Public Administration Circulars.  
- Semi-Government organizations follow Shop and Office Act, PED Circulars, and their own Administrative and Financial Manuals approved by their Board of Directors.

#### 5. 👥 Recruitment in Fully Government Organizations  
- Recruitment is done as per Public Service Commission guidelines under the Ministry of Public Administration.  
- Salaries and benefits are decided by the Ministry of Public Administration.

#### 6. 👔 Additional Knowledge for Engineer-Managers  
- Must understand recruitment, performance evaluation, attendance, working conditions, leave, benefits, disciplinary matters, and employee types (permanent, fixed-term/contract, casual, probation).  
- Need knowledge of organization vision & mission, business strategy, finance, sales & marketing, HR management, administration, project management, and legal aspects.

#### 7. 💰 Financial Management  
- Operational Expenditure (Opex) refers to day-to-day expenses.  
- Capital Expenditure (Capex) refers to investments in long-term assets.  
- Budgeting and financial authority levels are essential for managing finances.

#### 8. 🛒 Procurement Process  
- Steps: Preparation of specifications, preparation of tender documents, evaluation, and award.  
- Tender Boards and Technical Evaluation Committees oversee procurement.

#### 9. 📅 Project Management Knowledge Areas (PMI)  
- Project Integration Management  
- Scope Management  
- Time Management  
- Cost Management  
- Quality Management  
- Human Resource Management  
- Communications Management  
- Risk Management  
- Procurement Management  
- Stakeholder Management
