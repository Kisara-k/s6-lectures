## 8. CSE Perspective

## Key Points

#### 1. 🌍 Engineer in Society (CSE Perspective)
- Being an engineer is not just about the job you do, but how you do the job.
- Engineers must consider the social impact of their work.

#### 2. 📚 Definitions: Society, Rules, Morality, Ethics
- Society is an association of people organized under a system of rules.
- Rules are designed to advance the good of members over time.
- Morality refers to a society’s rules of conduct about what people ought or ought not to do.
- Ethics is the rational examination and evaluation of morality and people’s behavior.

#### 3. ⚖️ Ethics vs. Legality
- Ethics is based on morality; laws are made by rulers or parliaments.
- Ethical laws may be made by ethical rulers; unethical rulers may make unethical laws.
- Not all ethical behavior is covered by law.

#### 4. 🛠️ Characteristics of a Profession
- Expert knowledge is distinct from skills learned on the job.
- Autonomy includes diagnosis, treatment, follow-up, and evaluation.
- Governance involves setting entrance requirements and discipline.
- Service to society is a fundamental aspect of a profession.

#### 5. 🏛️ Professional Bodies
- Professional bodies specify entry requirements and define member behavior.
- Examples include:
  - Institution of Engineers Sri Lanka (Engineering)
  - Sri Lanka Institute of Architects (Architecture)
  - Computer Society of Sri Lanka, British Computer Society (IT)
  - Sri Lanka Medical Association (Medicine)
  - Sri Lanka Institute of Marketing, Chartered Institute of Marketing (Marketing)
  - Chartered Institute of Management Accountants (Accountancy)

#### 6. 🔄 Professional Bodies vs. Trade Unions
- Professional bodies focus on governance (e.g., IESL).
- Trade unions focus on protecting workers’ rights (e.g., CEB Engineers Union).

#### 7. 🏅 Professional Licensing
- Some professions require professional registration to practice (e.g., medical, legal, engineering).
- IT often does not require professional licensing but may rely on vendor certifications.
- There is increasing pressure to regulate IT as a profession.

#### 8. ⚖️ Legal and Ethical Perspectives
- Public criticism of judiciary flaws may be contempt of court.
- Convicted criminals must be given due process.
- Banning public demonstrations against repressive regimes raises ethical concerns.
- Causing harm to public property is both illegal and unethical.
