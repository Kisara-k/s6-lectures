## 4. Engineering Ethics 2

## Key Points

#### 1. 👩‍💼 Definition of a Professional  
- A professional carries out a specialized job requiring high education and training.  
- Professionals belong to a group, see others as peers, exercise judgment, and follow a code of ethics.

#### 2. 🌟 Professional Values  
- Key values include Accountability, Responsibility, Integrity, Professional Attitude, Continuous Learning, and Teamwork.

#### 3. 📜 Professional Ethics  
- Professional ethics are codified rules governing behavior in a business environment.  
- All members of a profession follow the same ethical code.

#### 4. ⚙️ Engineering Ethics  
- Engineering ethics sets standards for engineers’ obligations to the public, clients, employers, and the profession.  
- It applies to both technical and business aspects of engineering practice.

#### 5. 🛠️ Skills vs Competencies  
- Skill: A specific, measurable expertise in a task (e.g., Java programming, AutoCAD).  
- Competence: The ability to effectively perform a role, combining skill, knowledge, experience, attitude, and application ability.  
- Competencies develop over time and are context-specific; skills can be developed faster and are task-specific.

#### 6. 🌍 International Engineering Alliance (IEA)  
- IEA is a global organization with members from 41 jurisdictions in 29 countries.  
- It governs recognition of engineering education and professional competence through international agreements.  
- Key accords: Washington Accord (4-year engineer degree), Sydney Accord (3-year technologist degree), Dublin Accord (2-year technician degree).

#### 7. 👷 Engineering Roles  
- Engineer: Solves complex engineering problems.  
- Engineering Technologist: Defines and applies engineering procedures for broadly defined problems.  
- Engineering Technician: Performs well-defined practical procedures.

#### 8. ✅ Competency Standards for Professional Engineers  
- Practical application of engineering knowledge.  
- Leadership and management skills.  
- Communication and interpersonal skills.  
- Professional conduct and ethics.

#### 9. 📋 Basic Codes in Engineering Ethics  
- Practice sustainable engineering to minimize environmental impact.  
- Public health, safety, and welfare are paramount.  
- Uphold professional dignity, honor, and integrity.  
- Build reputation on merit, avoid unfair competition.  
- Work only within areas of competence.  
- Serve employers/clients faithfully without conflicts of interest.  
- Speak objectively and truthfully.  
- Continuously develop knowledge and skills throughout the career.
