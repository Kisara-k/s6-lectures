## 6. Case Study 1

## Key Points

#### 1. 🧑‍🔧 Case Background  
- Engineer-A was hired by Lawyer-X to conduct a forensic engineering investigation of a building fire.  
- The investigation revealed safety issues with the building materials used.  
- Lawyer-X and Client-Y reached a private, court-approved settlement with the accused parties.  
- The settlement included a confidentiality clause preventing Engineer-A from disclosing the forensic report.  

#### 2. ⚖️ Ethical Dilemma  
- Engineer-A is conflicted between maintaining confidentiality and the obligation to protect public safety.  
- The forensic report contains important safety findings that could affect public welfare.  
- The settlement legally binds Engineer-A to keep the report confidential.  

#### 3. 📜 Relevant Codes of Ethics (IESL & NSPE)  
- Both codes prioritize the health, safety, and welfare of the public as paramount.  
- Engineers must maintain confidentiality unless public safety is at risk.  
- Engineers should notify authorities if their safety concerns are ignored or overruled.  
- Engineers must act as faithful agents to clients but avoid conflicts with public interest.  
- Engineers should speak out truthfully and objectively.  

#### 4. 🔍 Case Analysis  
- The confidentiality obligation arises from a court-approved settlement, not just a private agreement.  
- There is no indication of an immediate or urgent threat to public safety in this case.  
- Engineer-A’s ethical obligation is to maintain confidentiality under the settlement terms.  
- Engineer-A may explore alternative ways to address safety concerns without breaching confidentiality.  

#### 5. 📌 Conclusion  
- Engineer-A must maintain confidentiality as required by the court settlement.  
- Engineer-A should seek alternative methods to raise awareness of safety issues without revealing confidential details.
