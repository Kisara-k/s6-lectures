## 1. Philosophy of Ethics

## Key Points

#### 1. 🧠 Philosophy of Ethics  
- Ethics is a philosophical discipline that studies right and wrong, good and bad, relating to the well-being of sentient beings.  
- Professional Ethics is a branch of ethics focused on ethical principles applicable to the engineering profession.

#### 2. 💡 Values, Morality, Ethics & Law  
- Values are stable, long-lasting beliefs about what is important, divided into intrinsic (e.g., honesty, courage) and extrinsic (e.g., wealth, teamwork) values.  
- Morality governs a society’s attitude toward right and wrong (e.g., respect others, keep promises).  
- Ethics is the systematic study of human actions to determine rightfulness or wrongfulness and promotes human welfare.  
- Law consists of rules enforced by authorities, generally based on society’s moral principles.

#### 3. ⚖️ Ethics vs Morals  
- Morals are personal principles shaped by culture, religion, and family, rarely changing.  
- Ethics are practical rules or codes created by groups or professions to guide behavior.  
- Ethics and morals can conflict; ethical behavior is not always moral, and moral behavior is not always ethical.

#### 4. 🌏 Eastern vs Western Ethics  
- Eastern ethics focuses on universal interconnections, internal discipline, respect, and harmony, rooted in religions like Hinduism and Buddhism.  
- Western ethics emphasizes rational thought, self-direction, and the triumph of good over evil, rooted in Roman and Christian traditions.

#### 5. 👷‍♂️ Professional Ethics  
- Professional ethics govern behavior in a professional environment, distinct from personal ethics.  
- Conflicts can arise between personal and professional ethics (e.g., police enforcing laws they personally disagree with).

#### 6. 🎯 Importance of Studying Professional Ethics  
- Engineers must adhere to high ethical standards to ensure safety and reliability.  
- Professional ethics improves ethical sensitivity, knowledge of standards, judgment, and willpower.  
- Unethical or incompetent engineering can cost lives, not just money.

#### 7. ⚠️ Ethical Dilemmas in Engineering  
- Ethical dilemmas occur when engineers face conflicting values, such as accepting kick-backs or shipping products without complete quality assurance.  
- Engineers must balance ethical principles against practical pressures in decision-making.
