## Answers

#### 1. Which of the following are important characteristics of an effective data visualization title?  
A) ✓ Titles should be the largest text to establish hierarchy.

C) ✓ Upper left placement aligns with Western reading habits.  
D) ✗ Detailed numeric data belongs in the graph, not the title.  

**Correct:** A, B, C


#### 2. Why is it recommended to label data points directly rather than using a separate legend?  
A) ✓ Reduces eye movement and cognitive load.

C) ✓ Legends interrupt brain’s interpretation by forcing back-and-forth eye movement.
D) ✓ Direct labels speed up interpretation.  

**Correct:** A, C, D


#### 3. When is it acceptable to use the "n/a" rating in the data visualization checklist?  
A) ✗ Partial compliance should be rated 1, not n/a.  
B) ✓ When a guideline truly does not apply (e.g., pie charts have no axes).  
C) ✗ n/a is not for disagreement or full compliance.
D) ✓ When a guideline is intentionally broken to make a point.  

**Correct:** B, D


#### 4. Which of the following statements about axis scales in bar charts are true?
