## Answers

#### 1. What is the primary purpose of data visualization?  
A) ✓ Visualization provides visual representations to help people perform tasks more effectively.  
B) ✓ Visualization augments human cognition by leveraging perception.  
C) ✗ Visualization does not aim to replace human decision-making but to support it.  
D) ✗ Summarizing data is part of analysis but visualization often shows more than just summaries.  

**Correct:** A, B


#### 2. Why is it important to keep a human in the loop during data analysis with visualization?  
A) ✓ Ill-specified problems require human judgment and flexibility.  
B) ✗ Many problems are ill-specified, so questions are not always known in advance.  
C) ✓ Humans verify and build trust in automatic solutions through visualization.  
D) ✗ Visualization supports humans; it does not fully replace them.  

**Correct:** A, C


#### 3. Which of the following are reasons why vision is preferred over other senses for data visualization?  
A) ✓ Vision provides a high-bandwidth channel to the brain.  
B) ✗ Touch has limited record/replay capacity and low bandwidth.  
C) ✗ Sound is sequential and lower bandwidth, not suitable for overview.  
D) ✓ Vision supports parallel and pre-attentive processing.  

**Correct:** A, D


#### 4. What are the limitations of using sound as a channel for data visualization?  
A) ✓ Sound has lower bandwidth than vision.  
B) ✗ Sound does not support pre-attentive processing like vision.  
C) ✗ Sound is not effective for representing large datasets visually.  
D) ✓ Sound provides a sequential stream, not simultaneous overview.  

**Correct:** A, D


#### 5. Why is it important to represent all the data rather than just summaries in visualization?  
A) ✓ Details help confirm expected and find unexpected patterns.  
B) ✓ Summaries can hide anomalies or outliers.  
C) ✗ Summaries can lose important information.  
D) ✓ Visualizing all data helps assess model validity.  

**Correct:** A, B, D


#### 6. What does Anscombe’s Quartet demonstrate about data visualization?  
A) ✓ Visualization reveals patterns statistics alone cannot.  
B) ✓ Different datasets with identical statistics can look very different visually.  
C) ✗ Summary statistics alone are not always sufficient.  
D) ✗ Visualizing raw data is necessary despite known statistics.  

**Correct:** A, B


#### 7. Which of the following are considered resource limitations that visualization designers must address?  
A) ✓ Computational limits like processing time and memory are important.  
B) ✓ Display limits such as pixel constraints and information density matter.  
C) ✗ Human senses have bandwidth limits; they are not unlimited.  
D) ✓ Human limits including attention and memory are critical.  

**Correct:** A, B, D
