## Answers

#### 1. What is the primary purpose of the Domain Situation level in the Nested Model?  
A) ✗ Algorithm optimization is a separate level

D) ✓ To identify the target users and understand their real-world context  

**Correct:** D

A) ✗ Observing users is domain-level activity

D) ✗ This is about visual encoding, not abstraction  

**Correct:** B


#### 3. Why is it important to validate the visual encoding and interaction idioms separately from the data/task abstraction?  
A) ✗ Visual encoding affects perception, not algorithm speed directly

D) ✗ Computational benchmarks do not confirm visual encoding effectiveness
