## Answers

#### 1. Which of the following are key components of the economic model for assessing the value of a visualization method?  
A) ✗ Aesthetic appeal is not part of the economic cost-benefit model.  
B) ✓ Number of users and frequency of use affect the total gain and cost calculations.  
C) ✓ Initial development costs and initial costs per user are explicitly part of the cost components in the model.  
D) ✓ The value of knowledge gained per session and exploration costs are central to the model’s profit calculation.  

**Correct:** B, C, D


#### 2. According to the model, what factors can cause subjectivity in visualization outcomes?  
A) ✓ The choice of visualization parameters and specification affects the image and interpretation.  
B) ✓ Initial knowledge influences how much new information is extracted.  
C) ✗ Automated statistical tests aim to reduce subjectivity, not cause it.  
D) ✓ Perceptual skills vary among users, affecting knowledge gained.  

**Correct:** A, B, D


#### 3. Why might some advanced visualization methods fail to be widely adopted in practice?  
A) ✓ Users prefer familiar, straightforward solutions with proven value.  
B) ✓ High initial costs per user and session discourage adoption.  
C) ✗ Advanced methods do not always produce misleading results; this is not a general cause of failure.  
D) ✗ Lack of alternative methods is not a reason for failure; often alternatives exist.  

**Correct:** A, B


#### 4. In the context of data visualization checklist guidelines, which of the following are recommended practices for text usage?  
A) ✗ Using both numeric labels and y-axis scales is redundant and discouraged.  
B) ✗ Text should be horizontal; vertical or diagonal titles/annotations are discouraged.  
C) ✓ Data labels should be placed near data points to reduce eye movement and improve clarity.  
D) ✓ Short, descriptive titles left-justified in the upper left corner are recommended.  

**Correct:** C, D


#### 5. What are some implications of interaction in visualization as discussed in the content?  
A) ✓ Interaction increases exploration costs and time spent by users.  
B) ✓ Allowing free parameter tuning can increase subjectivity and bias results.  
C) ✗ Interaction can improve understanding but also has drawbacks like increased subjectivity and cost.  
D) ✓ Good defaults or automatic settings reduce the need for extensive interaction and user effort.  

**Correct:** A, B, D


#### 6. Which statements about the role of alternative methods in visualization are true?  
A) ✓ New methods must demonstrate clear advantages over existing ones to gain adoption.  
B) ✗ The existence of alternatives affects user adoption; better existing methods reduce new method users.  
C) ✓ If automated methods reliably extract needed information, visualization may be unnecessary.  
D) ✗ Visualization is not always superior; automated methods can sometimes replace it.  

**Correct:** A, C


#### 7. How does the paper characterize the relationship between visualization as technology, art, and science?  
A) ✓ Technology focuses on effectiveness and efficiency.  
B) ✗ Art focuses on elegance and beauty, not provable effectiveness and efficiency.  
C) ✗ They are related, not independent or unrelated.  
D) ✓ The views are strongly related and can stimulate each other’s progress.  

**Correct:** A, D


#### 8. What challenges are associated with quantifying the knowledge gained (∆K) from visualization?  
A) ✗ Knowledge gained can be misleading or negative, not always positive.  
B) ✓ It is paradoxical because we visualize to gain insight but do not know what information is contained beforehand.  
C) ✗ Knowledge gained is not directly observable or easily measured.  
D) ✓ The value of insight is subjective and hard to assess.  

**Correct:** B, D


#### 9. Which of the following are true regarding the use and evaluation of texture-based flow visualization in Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD)?  
A) ✓ The number of users and frequency of use are relatively low compared to other fields.  
B) ✗ These methods are not widely integrated or routinely used in commercial CFD software.  
C) ✗ The additional knowledge gained is unclear and not definitively superior to traditional methods.  
D) ✓ High initial costs and unclear benefits contribute to limited adoption.  

**Correct:** A, D


#### 10. According to the content, what are some key considerations for developing effective data visualizations?  
A) ✓ Color schemes should consider cultural connotations and colorblindness for accessibility.  
B) ✗ Excessive gridlines and borders add clutter and should be minimized.  
C) ✗ Multiple y-axes confuse viewers; better alternatives exist such as connected scatter plots or side-by-side graphs.  
D) ✓ Axis intervals should be equidistant and graphs two-dimensional to avoid distortion.  

**Correct:** A, D
