## Answers

#### 1. What is the primary purpose of the Domain Situation level in the Nested Model?  
A) ✗ This relates to visual encoding, not domain understanding

C) ✓ To identify the target users and understand their real-world context  
D) ✗ Algorithm optimization is a separate level  

**Correct:** C


#### 2. Which of the following best describes Data/Task Abstraction?  
A) ✗ This is about visual encoding, not abstraction

C) ✗ System performance measurement is algorithm-level  
D) ✗ Observing users is domain-level activity  

**Correct:** B


#### 3. Why is it important to validate the visual encoding and interaction idioms separately from the data/task abstraction?  
A) ✗ Computational benchmarks do not confirm visual encoding effectiveness
