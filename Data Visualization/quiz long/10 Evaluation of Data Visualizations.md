## Answers

#### 1. Which of the following are important characteristics of an effective data visualization title?  
A) ✓ Upper left placement aligns with Western reading habits.

D) ✓ Titles should be the largest text to establish hierarchy.  

**Correct:** A, C, D


#### 2. Why is it recommended to label data points directly rather than using a separate legend?  
A) ✓ Reduces eye movement and cognitive load.

D) ✓ Legends interrupt brain’s interpretation by forcing back-and-forth eye movement.  

**Correct:** A, C, D


#### 3. When is it acceptable to use the "n/a" rating in the data visualization checklist?  
A) ✓ When a guideline is intentionally broken to make a point.  
B) ✓ When a guideline truly does not apply (e.g., pie charts have no axes).  
C) ✗ Partial compliance should be rated 1, not n/a.  
D) ✗ n/a is not for disagreement or full compliance.  

**Correct:** A, B
