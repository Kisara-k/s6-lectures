## Answers

#### 1. What is the primary purpose of data visualization?  
A) ✓ Visualization augments human cognition by leveraging perception.  
B) ✗ Visualization does not aim to replace human decision-making but to support it.  
C) ✗ Summarizing data is part of analysis but visualization often shows more than just summaries.  
D) ✓ Visualization provides visual representations to help people perform tasks more effectively.  

**Correct:** A, D


#### 2. Why is it important to keep a human in the loop during data analysis with visualization?  
A) ✗ Many problems are ill-specified, so questions are not always known in advance.  
B) ✓ Humans verify and build trust in automatic solutions through visualization.  
C) ✗ Visualization supports humans; it does not fully replace them.  
D) ✓ Ill-specified problems require human judgment and flexibility.  

**Correct:** B, D


#### 3. Which of the following are reasons why vision is preferred over other senses for data visualization?  
A) ✗ Sound is sequential and lower bandwidth, not suitable for overview.  
B) ✗ Touch has limited record/replay capacity and low bandwidth.  
C) ✓ Vision supports parallel and pre-attentive processing.  
D) ✓ Vision provides a high-bandwidth channel to the brain.  

**Correct:** C, D


#### 4. What are the limitations of using sound as a channel for data visualization?  
A) ✗ Sound does not support pre-attentive processing like vision.  
B) ✗ Sound is not effective for representing large datasets visually.  
C) ✓ Sound provides a sequential stream, not simultaneous overview.  
D) ✓ Sound has lower bandwidth than vision.  

**Correct:** C, D


#### 5. Why is it important to represent all the data rather than just summaries in visualization?  
A) ✗ Summaries can lose important information.  
B) ✓ Summaries can hide anomalies or outliers.  
C) ✓ Visualizing all data helps assess model validity.  
D) ✓ Details help confirm expected and find unexpected patterns.  

**Correct:** B, C, D


#### 6. What does Anscombe’s Quartet demonstrate about data visualization?  
A) ✗ Summary statistics alone are not always sufficient.  
B) ✓ Visualization reveals patterns statistics alone cannot.  
C) ✓ Different datasets with identical statistics can look very different visually.  
D) ✗ Visualizing raw data is necessary despite known statistics.  

**Correct:** B, C


#### 7. Which of the following are considered resource limitations that visualization designers must address?

B) ✓ Computational limits like processing time and memory are important.  
C) ✓ Display limits such as pixel constraints and information density matter.  
D) ✗ Human senses have bandwidth limits; they are not unlimited.

#### 8. What is meant by the “sweet spot” in visualization design regarding information density?
