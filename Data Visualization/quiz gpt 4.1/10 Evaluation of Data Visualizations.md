## 11. Evaluation of Data Visualizations

## Questions

#### 1. Which of the following are recommended practices for labeling data in a visualization?
A) Eliminating legends when possible to reduce eye movement  
B) Placing data labels directly on or near the data points  
C) Labeling every axis tick and every data point numerically  
D) Using a separate legend for all data series  


#### 2. When evaluating the effectiveness of a data visualization, which factors are considered part of the economic model’s “costs”?
A) Time spent exploring and interpreting the visualization  
B) Time spent developing the visualization method  
C) User training and adoption time  
D) The value of knowledge gained from the visualization  


#### 3. Which statements about the use of color in data visualizations are TRUE?
A) Action colors should highlight key data, while less important data should use muted colors  
B) Red-green color combinations are always safe for colorblind viewers  
C) Visualizations should remain legible when printed in black and white  
D) Color schemes should be chosen intentionally, not left as software defaults  


#### 4. Which of the following can lead to misleading or incorrect interpretations in data visualizations?
A) Starting the y-axis of a bar chart above zero  
B) Using three-dimensional effects or bevels  
C) Applying inappropriate interpolation methods  
D) Muting gridlines to a faint gray  


#### 5. In the theoretical model of visualization, which elements influence the amount of knowledge a user gains from a visualization?
A) The specification and parameters of the visualization  
B) The type and complexity of the data being visualized  
C) The number of users who view the visualization  
D) The user’s prior knowledge and perceptual skills  


#### 6. Which of the following are valid reasons why a technically advanced visualization method might not be widely adopted in practice?
A) Existing simpler methods already meet user needs  
B) Lack of integration with commonly used software tools  
C) High initial user costs and steep learning curve  
D) The method produces more insight than existing alternatives  


#### 7. Which practices help ensure that text in a data visualization is readable and effective?
A) Making all text vertical to save space  
B) Ensuring axis labels are at least 9-point font on paper or 20-point on screen  
C) Using black or very dark text on a white or transparent background  
D) Using a clear hierarchy of text sizes for titles, subtitles, and labels  


#### 8. Which statements about subjectivity in data visualization are correct?
A) Visualization is always an objective process, independent of the viewer  
B) Visualization outcomes can depend on the user’s prior knowledge  
C) Allowing users to freely adjust visualization parameters can increase subjectivity  
D) Sharing visualizations openly can reduce, but not eliminate, subjectivity  


#### 9. According to the cost-benefit model, which factors would most likely increase the overall value (profit) of a visualization method?
A) Low costs for user training and session setup  
B) High initial development costs  
C) Large number of users and frequent use  
D) High value of knowledge gained per session  


#### 10. Which of the following are potential pitfalls or limitations in the field of data visualization, as discussed in the lecture?
A) Visualization methods can sometimes produce negative knowledge (mislead users)  
B) All new visualization methods are inherently better than existing ones  
C) High interaction costs can outweigh the benefits of exploration  
D) Lack of empirical evaluation and validation of new methods

## Answers

#### 1. Which of the following are recommended practices for labeling data in a visualization?
A) ✓ Eliminating legends when possible helps viewers interpret data more efficiently.  
B) ✓ Placing data labels directly on or near the data points reduces cognitive load and improves clarity.  
C) ✗ Labeling every axis tick and every data point numerically creates redundancy and clutter.  
D) ✗ Using a separate legend for all data series can interrupt interpretation and is discouraged when direct labeling is possible.  

**Correct:** A, B


#### 2. When evaluating the effectiveness of a data visualization, which factors are considered part of the economic model’s “costs”?
A) ✓ Time spent exploring and interpreting the visualization is an exploration cost (Ce).  
B) ✓ Time spent developing the visualization method is an initial development cost (Ci).  
C) ✓ User training and adoption time is an initial user cost (Cu).  
D) ✗ The value of knowledge gained is a benefit, not a cost.  

**Correct:** A, B, C


#### 3. Which statements about the use of color in data visualizations are TRUE?
A) ✓ Action colors should highlight key data, while muted colors are for less important data.  
B) ✗ Red-green combinations are problematic for colorblind viewers and should be avoided.  
C) ✓ Visualizations should remain interpretable in black and white for accessibility and printing.  
D) ✓ Intentional color schemes are recommended to reinforce meaning and branding.  

**Correct:** A, C, D


#### 4. Which of the following can lead to misleading or incorrect interpretations in data visualizations?
A) ✓ Starting the y-axis above zero in a bar chart can exaggerate differences and mislead viewers.  
B) ✓ Using three-dimensional effects or bevels can distort perception and misrepresent data.  
C) ✓ Inappropriate interpolation methods can create artifacts or false patterns.  
D) ✗ Muting gridlines to a faint gray reduces clutter and is recommended, not misleading.  

**Correct:** A, B, C


#### 5. In the theoretical model of visualization, which elements influence the amount of knowledge a user gains from a visualization?
A) ✓ The specification and parameters of the visualization influence what is shown and how it is perceived.  
B) ✓ The type and complexity of the data being visualized affect how much new knowledge can be extracted.  
C) ✗ The number of users does not affect individual knowledge gain, only overall impact.  
D) ✓ The user’s prior knowledge and perceptual skills directly affect how much is learned.  

**Correct:** A, B, D


#### 6. Which of the following are valid reasons why a technically advanced visualization method might not be widely adopted in practice?
A) ✓ Existing simpler methods may already meet user needs, reducing motivation to switch.  
B) ✓ Lack of integration with commonly used software tools is a barrier to practical use.  
C) ✓ High initial user costs and steep learning curve discourage adoption.  
D) ✗ Producing more insight than alternatives would encourage, not discourage, adoption.  

**Correct:** A, B, C


#### 7. Which practices help ensure that text in a data visualization is readable and effective?
A) ✗ Making all text vertical reduces readability and is discouraged except for certain axis labels.  
B) ✓ Ensuring axis labels are at least 9-point on paper or 20-point on screen meets minimum readability standards.  
C) ✓ Using black or very dark text on a white or transparent background maximizes contrast and legibility.  
D) ✓ A clear hierarchy of text sizes helps viewers distinguish importance and improves readability.  

**Correct:** B, C, D


#### 8. Which statements about subjectivity in data visualization are correct?
A) ✗ Visualization is not always objective; outcomes can vary by viewer and settings.  
B) ✓ User’s prior knowledge can influence interpretation, making visualization subjective.  
C) ✓ Allowing users to freely adjust parameters can increase subjectivity and variability in interpretation.  
D) ✓ Sharing visualizations openly can reduce, but not eliminate, subjectivity by allowing others to judge.  

**Correct:** B, C, D


#### 9. According to the cost-benefit model, which factors would most likely increase the overall value (profit) of a visualization method?
A) ✓ Low costs for user training and session setup reduce total costs, increasing value.  
B) ✗ High initial development costs decrease overall value.  
C) ✓ Large number of users and frequent use increase total benefit.  
D) ✓ High value of knowledge gained per session increases the benefit side of the model.  

**Correct:** A, C, D


#### 10. Which of the following are potential pitfalls or limitations in the field of data visualization, as discussed in the lecture?
A) ✓ Visualizations can mislead and produce negative knowledge if poorly designed.  
B) ✗ Not all new methods are inherently better; many are not adopted for good reasons.  
C) ✓ High interaction costs can outweigh the benefits, making some methods impractical.  
D) ✓ Lack of empirical evaluation and validation is a recognized limitation in the field.  

**Correct:** A, C, D
